# XMage patches

Changes to upstream XMage live here as `NNNN-description.patch` files (`git diff` format, paths relative to the XMage root). `build-xmage.sh` applies them in name order after fetching the pinned commit, and the build fails if one no longer applies.

Each patch needs a header comment that gives the reason, the upstream issue or pull request if there is one, and the condition for removing it.

At the current pin (`xmage_1.4.62V1`) seven patches carry Two-Headed Giant: three from v0.5.0's spike, three from v0.5.1's card audit and one from v0.6.1 (see [the Two-Headed Giant note](../../../docs/engine/two-headed-giant.md), and [the audit's review](../../../docs/engine/two-headed-giant-audit.csv) for every card it looked at):

| Patch | What it changes | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0001-two-headed-giant-shared-team-turns.patch` | Shared team turns (rule 805): a game type may name teammates (`Game.getTeammates`, empty by default); the active team takes one turn together, untaps, draws, plays a land each and discards to hand size; each of its players is an active player; teammates are not opponents; the team attacks and blocks together; the human player stops in its team's turn as in its own | 12 core files and `HumanPlayer` |
| `0002-two-headed-giant-shared-life-and-poison.patch` | A team's shared life total (810.9) and team poison (810.10), through overridable methods of `GameImpl` | `PlayerImpl`, `GameImpl` |
| `0003-two-headed-giant-ai-teammates.patch` | The computer player never aims a harmful spell at its teammate, and scores its team's permanents | AI's `PossibleTargetsSelector` and `GameStateEvaluator2`, MAD's `SimulatedPlayer2` |
| `0004-two-headed-giant-rules-audit.patch` (v0.5.1) | The rules the audit found missing: a step of the team's turn is each of its players' (805.4d, `TeamStepEvent`), and "the active player" of an ability triggered there is that player (805.9, `Game.asActivePlayer`); Sagas and the initiative at each player's own step; "not your turn" and the core's "your turn" checks; skips, extra phases and steps, and turn control are the team's (805.8); each player of the active team is an attacking player (805.10a, 805.10c), "whenever a player attacks" triggers for each, and a creature put onto the battlefield attacking is aimed by its controller (508.4); can't lose and can't win are the team's (810.8a); life totals set for each player or exchanged (810.9d, 810.9e, 810.9g, 810.9h); poison checks and losses are the team's (810.10a, 810.10b; the poison methods become public `Game` methods, replacing 0002's); Assist counts the teammate's mana | 34 core files |
| `0005-two-headed-giant-ai-team-combat.patch` (v0.5.1) | The computer player attacks with its team's attackers at the defending team in mind, blocks with the defending team's, leaves its teammate's blocks alone, and counts the team's poison against 15 | MAD's `ComputerPlayer6` and `CombatUtil` |
| `0006-two-headed-giant-card-audit.patch` (v0.5.1) | The cards whose own code the audit fixed one by one: "your turn" by player id, custom "each player's step" triggers and the ones whose text does not say "that player", watchers of the turn's beginning, "you attack with", Mirkwood Trapper, poison checks, Melira, and the life-setting cards | 88 card classes in `Mage.Sets` |
| `0007-two-headed-giant-team-priority.patch` (v0.6.1) | Team priority and team choices: a team has priority, and passes once each of its players has passed in succession (805.5); a team puts all its players' triggered abilities on the stack in one order, picked by its chooser (805.7, 805.2: `Game.getTeamChooser`, named by the game type); mulligans and opening-hand actions team by team, the starting team first (805.3: `Game.inTeamOrder`); one combined attack and one combined block, each checked as a whole, a human player asked again when a teammate changes it (805.10b, 805.10d) | `Game`, `GameImpl`, `Combat`, `Mulligan` |

Every game type other than Two-Headed Giant names no teammates, so XMage behaves as unpatched for Legacy and Commander: each change reduces to XMage's own code without teammates, all engine tests that predate the patches pass unchanged, and `TwoHeadedGiantAuditTest.aLegacyDuelPlaysAsBefore` and `aLegacyDuelPlaysTheReviewsPatternsAsBefore` play the audited patterns in Legacy duels.

To change a patch: apply them in order to the pinned commit in a scratch clone, one commit each; make the change as a fixup of the patch it belongs to and fold it in (`git rebase --autosquash`); then write each commit's `git diff` under its patch's header. The image the engine build makes has the compiled XMage in `/xmage/m2`, so a container from it can rebuild only the changed modules and run the engine tests against them.
