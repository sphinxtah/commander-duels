# Plan for v0.6.2

Named by the owner on 2026-10-24, during v0.6.1: "When you're finished with all this work, close the version, commit, and push to Github. The next version will be v0.6.2." A patch version. Its scope is the owner's to choose at the start, in a new chat; this lists what v0.6.1 left and what it found.

## Carried over

From earlier versions, still open:
- **The rare cards the Two-Headed Giant audit left wrong** (17), each with its reason in [the audit's review](../engine/two-headed-giant-audit.csv): Urabrask's draw replacement, nine cards that take the turn's holder as "the attacking player", four watchers keyed by the holder, Storage Matrix, Elkin Lair, Brine Elemental's double skip.
- **A browser journey that plays Two-Headed Giant with four people** against the real engine. `scripts/api_practice_game.py` has no Two-Headed Giant option either; `scripts/engine_human_game.py` gained one in v0.6.1.

## From v0.6.1

Left open or decided along the way, for the owner to keep or revisit:
- **Team choices in order** ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md#team-priority-and-team-choices-v061)): the choices a team's players make at once go in turn order; the rules let the team pick its own order, and the engine does not ask. Effects that ask "each player in turn" keep XMage's order from the effect's controller, as in every format.
- **A bot in control plays for the controlled player** ([a bot in control](../engine/human-seat.md#a-bot-in-control-v061)): its stand-in is XMage's AI playing that player's turn in that player's interest, so a bot's Mindslaver does not turn the human's turn against them.
- **Alpha and Beta's prices are thin evidence** ([pack prices](../pack-prices.md#alpha-and-beta-v061)): the Beta booster from two sales, the Alpha booster and both starter decks estimated from it. Refresh them when more sales appear.
- **Beta's print runs** follow Scrye's per-rarity table, which totals 7.8 million cards; Wizards gives 7.3 million, so Beta's counts may be up to 7% high. Scaling them down is the owner's call.
- **Own auctions on the market**: shown since v0.6.1, badged as the viewer's own. Madden's market leaves them out; the owner may prefer a filter.
- **The auction tests under load**: on a busy machine the whole Vitest run once failed a different one of the auction page's tests each time, each passing alone; the auction journey checked the seller's balance, which a game won in a parallel journey changed, and now checks the auction house's part of the ledger.

## Already on main

- **Booster data on deploy** (after v0.6.1's tag, from the owner's report that Alpha and Beta were not in the shop after the catalog sync): the worker queues a booster data run as it starts when products have no booster data yet ([the shop](../shop.md)). Built and tested; it goes live with v0.6.2's tag.

## The owner's requests

- **An alliance's cards before choosing it** (the owner, 2026-10-24, at the end of v0.6.1): "When choosing your alliance, clicking on the planeswalker brings up a tile showing all of the cards they get from that planeswalker. You can click on each card to make them larger to view. In that tile is also a button for "Begin Alliance with PLANESWALKER_NAME"." Today Profile's picker allies at once on a click ("Ally with <name>"); the new tile shows the alliance's cards first (each opening larger, as card details do elsewhere), with "Begin Alliance with <name>" to choose it. Locked Planeswalkers may show their cards too, without the button.

- **Granular filters in My Library** (the owner, 2026-10-24): filter the library's cards finely (for example by name, set, rarity, finish, colour, type and mana value, as the auction house's search does).
- **Add to deck from My Library** (the owner, 2026-10-24): each card offers "Add to deck", which opens a pop-up to choose the deck it goes into.
- **The deck editor's deck as cards** (the owner, 2026-10-24): "make the Decks menu decks show the cards as an option instead of just the current list, and cards should be default." Opening a deck shows its cards as card pictures by default, with the current list as the other view.
