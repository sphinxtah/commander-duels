# Plan for v0.9.3

Opened at the close of v0.9.2 on 2026-11-09 ([release notes](../releases/0.9.2.md)). Its scope is the owner's to give at the start, in a new chat: one round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults, then the work. Closed on 2026-11-09 ([release notes](../releases/0.9.3.md)); the next version is v0.9.4 ([the plan](0.9.4.md)).

## The owner's answers at the start (2026-11-09)

- **Scope:** all four groups: the campaign's difficulty; the finale and the campaign's tuning (the incarnation's test, the barks, the post-campaign challenge); the Two-Headed Giant and King's Table browser journeys; the quick wins (the lobby's order and host search, deleting decks from the trash for good, the backlog's stale lines).
- **The lever:** rework the late wardens' decks (7 to 14) toward plans XMage's AI plays well, keeping each warden's commander, colours and theme.
- **The targets:** read from the handoff, which gives a forgiving first ward, a clear rise in every chapter and a finale beaten only rarely; the reference win rates by tier were set from it (docs/campaign.md).
- **Bugs:** the testers' reports, read on the live site: there were none.
- **During the work:** every warden's deck is won whole on its first defeat ("By the end, the player wins all of the decks they had to face"); rework the finale's 99 for the AI too; players who already won a reworked warden keep the deck they won.

## The campaign's difficulty (from v0.9.1's measurement; not taken up in v0.9.2)

[The first run](../campaign.md#difficulty-measured-v091) found the curve backwards: the wardens from ward 7 on (skill 4 to 6) won none of 24 games against the loaner at skill 3, and the early wardens won 4 of 18. Questions for the owner:

- **Targets.** The handoff's win rates by tier; the repository doesn't have them.
- **The lever.** Rework the later wardens' decks toward plans XMage's AI plays well, or give the later bots a stronger opening (the handoff asks for no life or mana bonuses).
- **A wider run:** the starter decks as references, more games, the reference at skill 2 and 4.
- **The finale:** its stand-in ran out of the engine's 2 GB heap once, and one game ended on turn 2 with both players at 40.
