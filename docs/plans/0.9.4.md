## The campaign's difficulty

- **Wider samples.** v0.9.3 measured each ward with 9 games (three reference decks, three games each); the noise is about ±16 points. A run of 30 games a ward on a quiet machine would settle the curve. The harness's `-Dcampaign.trace` prints a game's log.
- **In play.** Every ward from 7 on was rebuilt: play them, with the loaner and with players' own decks, for the barks' moments (each deck was checked to still produce its ward's triggers), the bots' turn length (Liliana's was slow before v0.9.3's change) and the lessons.
- **The engine's memory.** Watch the engine's log on the live site for "light searches" and "did not stop": v0.9.3 keeps a search shallow when a copy of the game is heavy or the heap is full.
