# Plan for v0.9.13: operations, the release gate, and the economy's foundations removed

The fourth and last stage of [the Preparation Framework](0.9.9.md), split from [v0.9.12](0.9.12.md) by the owner on 2026-11-13 and cut down by him on 2026-11-14, after v0.9.12 was released and deployed.

## Decisions at the start of v0.9.13

The owner, 2026-11-14:

- **No economy changes.** None of v0.9.12's proposals is approved, now or later: "I want to keep the game pure, as if it were real life (opportunity to get cards, etc)." The code they stood on is removed, the recording of farming signals included.
- **No playgroups, no scheduling, no leagues.** All of the specification's section 13 is out of the framework and out of v1.0.0: "my server can't really keep up anyway." Leagues may come back as a project of their own, for v2.0.0 or later.
- **The finale stands** without the Power Nine: he has cleared the decks.
- Still in force from 2026-11-13: no path to a specific card, and no Rule Zero disclosures.

## What it holds

| Work | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove the economy's foundations** | Everything v0.9.12 added for section 11 ([the economy](../economy.md)): the policies and their routes and console command, entitlements and the starter-staple pool, functional access to cards not owned, quest alternatives, the catch-up and the streak policies, the farming signals and their recording in a game's eligibility, the economy report. The game pays and owns as it did in v0.9.11. See the questions below for the data |
| **Rollout and operations** (§15) | Gates so an unfinished workflow cannot be reached through an unguarded route; metrics for access refusals and settlement failures; development fixtures; the operations documentation (backups, migrations, compatibility, rollback limits) |
| **The release gate** (§16) | Each remaining foundation's exit criteria checked and written down; migrations run against the representative account; the precise v1.0.0 backlog, naming the foundation each feature stands on, without what was dropped |

## To settle before the removal

- **Migration 0047's table and column** (`entitlements`, `daily_quests.alternative`). The framework's rule is expand-only migrations, and the specification authorizes no destructive one. Either they stay, unused and empty, or the owner approves a migration that drops them. Nothing was ever written to them unless a policy was turned on by hand.
- **`games.eligibility`** keeps what v0.9.10 put there (the mode and what a game may pay). Only the `signals` and `seats` that v0.9.12 added go.
- **What stays from v0.9.12:** the campaign's rule and decks, the deck workshop, Cryptic Spires, Rule Zero table profiles, large boards. The mode policies approved on 2026-11-13 stay too (a sandbox game earns nothing, a borrowed deck earns as a table game); the one about leagues has nothing left to govern.

## Not in it

The player-facing features of [v1.0.0](1.0.0.md), which no longer include progression and acquisition changes, persistent playgroups or private Sealed leagues.
