# Plan for v0.9.13: groups, leagues, operations and the release gate

The fourth and last stage of [the Preparation Framework](0.9.9.md), split from [v0.9.12](0.9.12.md) by the owner on 2026-11-13. It builds on v0.9.10's access grants and modes and v0.9.12's table profiles.

| Foundation (spec §) | Builds on | First piece of work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Playgroups, scheduling and leagues** (§13) | Friends, tables, notifications; v0.9.10's grants; v0.9.12's saved table profiles | Groups and membership with roles, invitations and removal that revokes private access; events across time zones and daylight-saving changes; standings from recorded results that reprocessing cannot double; Sealed pools apart from libraries, with pool revisions and scheduled additions |
| **Rollout and operations** (§15) | Site settings, the audit log, leased jobs, the deploy kit; v0.9.9's recording metrics and retention; v0.9.12's policies | Gates for unfinished workflows, so a disabled one cannot be reached through an unguarded route; an Administration panel for the economy's policies (the console command changes them today); metrics for access refusals and settlement failures; development fixtures; the operations documentation |
| **The release gate** (§16) | Everything before it | Each foundation's exit criteria checked and written down; migrations run against the representative account; the precise v1.0.0 backlog, which names the foundation each feature stands on and what it turned out to be |

## Carried from v0.9.12

- **The economy's proposals** wait for the owner's decision, each with its simulated numbers ([the economy](../economy.md)). Nothing here depends on them.
- **Ward 14.** Its public stand-in was strengthened after the Power Nine left it and measured again ([the campaign](../campaign.md#no-power-nine-no-reserved-list-v0912)); whether the finale is as hard as it should be is the owner's to say after playing it.
- **League reward policy.** Leagues pay nothing until one is approved (the mode policy approved at v0.9.12's start).

## Not in it

The player-facing features of [v1.0.0](1.0.0.md). Two of the specification's items are dropped from both by the owner's decision of 2026-11-13: a path to a specific card, and Rule Zero disclosures.
