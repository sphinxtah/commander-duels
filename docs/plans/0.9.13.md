# Plan for v0.9.13: operations, the release gate, and the economy's foundations removed (as built)

The fourth and last stage of [the Preparation Framework](0.9.9.md), split from [v0.9.12](0.9.12.md) by the owner on 2026-11-13 and cut down by him on 2026-11-14, after v0.9.12 was released and deployed.

## Decisions at the start of v0.9.13

The owner, 2026-11-14:

- **No economy changes.** None of v0.9.12's proposals is approved, now or later: "I want to keep the game pure, as if it were real life (opportunity to get cards, etc)." The code they stood on is removed, the recording of farming signals included.
- **No playgroups, no scheduling, no leagues.** All of the specification's section 13 is out of the framework and out of v1.0.0: "my server can't really keep up anyway." Leagues may come back as a project of their own, for v2.0.0 or later.
- **The finale stands** without the Power Nine: he has cleared the decks.
- Still in force from 2026-11-13: no path to a specific card, and no Rule Zero disclosures.

## Decisions as it was built

The owner, 2026-11-14, at the start of the work:

- **Migration 0047's data: drop it all, guarded.** A new migration drops the `entitlements` table and `daily_quests.alternative`, takes the farming signals out of the games v0.9.12 recorded and deletes the policies' settings, and refuses a database where a policy was ever used ([the economy](../economy.md#the-data)).
- **Nothing to fix on the live site** since v0.9.12 was deployed.
- **Released as v0.9.12 was**, through his Chrome.
- **One request of his own:** on an opened deck pack, "Save as a Legacy deck" and "Keep as individual cards" go down onto the bar beside "Back to the shop".
- **And one for v1.0.0:** Play from the main menu as a wizard, one option a slide, ending on "Start Game" ([the backlog](1.0.0.md#10-play-as-a-wizard-the-owners-request)).

## What it left

- **A panel in Administration** for the numbers and the workflows' stages: they are a JSON route and a console command, and the panel is v1.0.0's (its handoff names dashboards).
- **The engine is untouched.** Its suite runs in the hosted checks; the private images are built from this version's public ones.

## Not in it

The player-facing features of [v1.0.0](1.0.0.md), which no longer include progression and acquisition changes, persistent playgroups or private Sealed leagues.
