# Plan for v0.9.5

Opened at the close of v0.9.4 on 2026-11-10 ([release notes](../releases/0.9.4.md)). Its scope is the owner's to give at the start, in a new chat: one round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults, then the work.

## The owner's request: quest boards (2026-11-10, noted during v0.9.4)

The headline of v0.9.5. Today every player has three daily quests drawn from about a dozen, all of them wins in games ([quests](../quests.md#daily-quests)). The owner wants:
- **Two boards: daily and weekly.**
- **Daily: five quests instead of three**, growing harder from the first to the fifth, drawn from a much larger pool than today's, so players don't keep seeing the same ones.
- **Weekly: twenty goals a week**, mostly outside the games: win an auction, buy two packs that are on sale, list a rare in the auction house, build a new deck, and many more. About **200 canned weekly quests** in rotation. The week resets **Sunday at midnight** (Arizona time, as the day does today).

**The owner's decisions (2026-11-10):**
- **Board bonuses**, on top of each quest's own tokens: **400 tokens** for completing all five daily quests, **2,000 tokens** for all twenty weekly goals.
- **Each quest completes once and pays once.**
- **Streaks** (the owner, 2026-11-10):
  - **Daily streak.** Each day in a row that all five dailies are done, the board bonus grows by 100 tokens: 400 on the first day, 500 on the second, and so on to 1,000 on the seventh. It stays at 1,000 every day after, as long as the streak holds.
  - **Weekly streak.** Each week in a row that all twenty weekly goals are done, the board bonus grows by 200 tokens: 2,000 in the first week, 2,200 in the second, and so on to 5,000 in the sixteenth. It stays at 5,000 after that.
  - **A broken streak starts over** at 400 or 2,000. A daily streak breaks on a day whose board isn't finished, and a weekly streak on a week whose board isn't finished.
  - **To settle at the start:**
    - whether a streak is shown on its board (current streak, today's bonus, the next one);
    - that the days and weeks are Arizona's calendar days and Sunday-midnight weeks;
    - that the streak is kept in the ledger's own records, so it can't be rebuilt by replaying requests.
- **Farming: Claude's recommendations, taken by the owner.**
  - Each non-game goal counts only what the server records itself, and only real exchanges:
    - an auction won counts only against another account's bid or listing, above a minimum price;
    - a rare listed counts once it stays up for at least an hour, or once it sells;
    - a pack on sale counts at purchase;
    - a deck built counts once it is legal, has a full list and lasts for a day.
  - Nothing a player does with their own second account, or undoes right after, counts.
- **No swap.** The daily swap of today goes, on both boards; a quest drawn stays the day's or the week's.

**Still to settle at the start:**
- The tokens each quest pays, by step (daily) and by goal (weekly).
- How "harder" climbs from quest 1 to 5: more wins, harder formats, stronger bots, players instead of bots.
- The list of non-game actions and how each is read from the records:
  - auctions won and listed;
  - packs bought, and on sale;
  - decks built;
  - cards added to the library;
  - friends;
  - tables hosted;
  - campaign wards;
  - sleeves.
- Whether the twenty weekly goals all show at once.
- The main menu's tile, and a Quests page holding both boards.
- What happens to today's daily quests and their history.
- Achievements built on the boards.

## Toward no stand-ins: what is left

The decision of v0.9.4 ([the catalog](../catalog.md#toward-no-stand-ins-v094)): packs stay genuine, every card a pack can deal should play as printed, and stand-ins stay only as the casual fallback. What is left of the cards Leng's packs can deal that the engine lacks:
- **Classic cards, batch 2** (about 20 that need no new engine piece): Frankenstein's Monster, Reflecting Mirror, Suffocation, Silhouette, Ertai's Meddling, Shaman's Trance, Arcum's Whistle, Winter's Chill, Spoils of War, Quarum Trench Gnomes, Backdraft, North Star, Magnetic Web, Ogre Enforcer, Ghostly Flame, Equinox, Secret of Bloodbending (Avatar), and Illusionary Mask (Alpha, Beta, Unlimited).
- **The text changers** (about 10): Sleight of Mind (Alpha to Fifth Edition and Ice Age), Alter Reality, Glamerdye, Trait Doctoring, Whim of Volrath, Spectral Shift, Swirl the Mists, Balduvian Shaman. They need a text-changing effect (color words and basic land types) the engine lacks: one core piece, then the cards.
- **Single cards that need a piece of their own**: Spy Kit ("has all names of nonlegendary creature cards"), Cryptic Spires ("as you create your deck, circle two colors": the deck editor could record the circles).
- **The owner's decisions**: the ante cards (Darkpact, Contract from Below, Demonic Attorney; Amulet of Quoz, Timmerian Fiends) stay stand-ins for now (the owner, 2026-11-10); Conspiracy's own card type (25 conspiracies, which start the game in the command zone and are chosen in a draft); Shahrazad (subgames); Falling Star (dexterity).
- **The Un-sets** (481 pack cards): Unfinity's Attractions and stickers are subsystems of their own; the silver-bordered cards play only at casual tables anyway.
- **XMage's own releases**: move the pin with each one (1.4.63's rebase took three hand-merged patches), especially while new sets' cards are still arriving (Star Trek on 2026-11-13).

## The campaign

- **The difficulty run on GitHub's runners** (`.github/workflows/campaign-difficulty.yml`, v0.9.4): its first run, 30 games a ward, is in [the campaign](../campaign.md#difficulty-on-githubs-runners-v094); ward 13's row was still playing at v0.9.4's close (Actions run 42863855360's summary has it). What it asks: Daretti (100% for the references, target 80%) and Oko (73%, target 60%) a little stronger; Dovin (53%, 65%) and Ob Nixilis (23%, 35%) a little weaker; Geyadrone Dihada (43%, 30%) stronger; and the finale's public stand-in (30%, 10%), which plays without the High Priest and the incarnation, compared with a run of the private engine image if one can be had. Then run the workflow again.
- **A duel's restoration after an engine restart** (looked at in v0.9.4, not built): XMage keeps a game in memory, with its players' threads and the human seats' waits, so restoring one means either a snapshot of the game state that a new process can resume (XMage's state copies do not carry the players' threads) or replaying a game from its seed and every answer (the bots' searches depend on time, so their choices would have to be recorded too). Either is a version of its own. Until then an interrupted duel counts for nothing and the screen leads back to the campaign.
- **Carried**: the finale's incarnation in play (its ultimate, the bot's recast), voice-over.
