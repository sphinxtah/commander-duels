# Plan for v0.4.4: chat between friends and at the table

Noted on 2026-10-21, during v0.4.2, from the owner's words: "in the v0.4.4 release we'll have chatting between friends and during gameplay, which will have the ability to message allies separately." It builds on v0.4.3's player search, profiles, friends, presence and the notification bell ([the v0.4.3 plan](0.4.3.md)). This is a placeholder to start from; v0.4.3 may change it, and the owner decides at the start.

## What the owner asked for

- **Chat between friends:** messages between two players who are friends, outside games, with the bell (v0.4.3) telling of new ones.
- **Chat during a game,** at the table, among the players seated there.
- **Messages to allies only.** At a table with several players, a player can message the players they are allied with apart from the whole table.

## For the start questions

- **Who an ally is at the table.** Teammates where a format has teams (Two-Headed Giant is not built yet: [the feasibility note](../engine/two-headed-giant.md)); in a free-for-all Commander game, players who agree to it during the game; or friends seated at the same table. Bots never read or send chat.
- **The table's chat and hidden information.** Chat is between people and never carries game state, but a message to allies is hidden from the other players, as their hands are: the leak checks the browser journeys run on every frame would cover chat frames too.
- **Moderation and safety.** The scope's conduct rules and the terms of use; reporting a message (the v0.4.1 report form could carry it); blocking a player (v0.4.3); whether administrators can read reported messages; how long messages are kept, and the privacy policy's words for them; limits on how fast messages can be sent.
- **Where it lives.** The app's WebSocket for the table already carries a game's frames; friends' chat needs a connection of its own while the app is open, or the bell's refresh.
- **SysOp.** SysOp sees every hand and library in a running game ([SysOp's full access](../identity.md#sysop-the-local-root-account)); whether that extends to allies-only messages at tables it observes is the owner's call.

## Decisions at the start of v0.4.4

Asked and answered on 2026-10-22:
1. **Allies are agreed during the game.** A player offers an alliance from another player's portrait; it starts when they accept, and either may end it at any time. Everyone sees who is allied with whom; only the allies read their messages.
2. **Friends' chat: a Messages page and docked windows.** The Messages page holds the conversations and their history; small windows docked at the bottom right stay open from page to page. New messages count on the bell and pop up beside it.
3. **Administrators read reported messages only**, with the 20 messages before each in its chat that the reporter saw.
4. **Extras: quick emotes at the table.** Not this time: muting a player at the table, a word filter, typing and seen marks.

**Defaults stated at the start**, which the owner did not change: chat is between friends only (a block or an ended friendship stops new messages); messages arrive at once over a connection the app keeps open; SysOp reads every chat, allies-only messages at tables it watches included, under its standing full access, with every reading through its reader audited; bots never read or send chat, and practice games have none; a player out of the game still uses the table's chat; friends' messages are kept a year, a table's chat 30 days.

What was built is in [chat](../chat.md).

## Added during v0.4.4

On 2026-10-22, the owner: "when I opened a new pack, not all of the images came up. I also saw this happen in the cards list. Sometimes the images just don't load." The screenshot showed 8 of a pack's 14 pictures. Found: every download from Scryfall's image host took a share of the per-account queue meant for Scryfall's rate-limited name search (8 an account), so the 9th new card onwards was refused at once; and the Cards page, the pack opening and the starter decks' pictures never asked again after a failure, unlike the table's. Fixed: downloads wait their turn (4 at once an account, 16 in all), and every card picture retries with backoff ([the game screen](../game-screen.md#cards)).

After it, v0.4.5 is the facelift ([the v0.4.5 plan](0.4.5.md)).
