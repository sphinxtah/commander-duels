# Plan for v0.5.1

Named by the owner on 2026-10-22, at the start of v0.5.0: "The next version will be v0.5.1." Proposed as a patch version: fixes for what the owner and the beta testers find in v0.5.0. At its start the owner added the rest of Two-Headed Giant and kept the number ([decisions](#decisions-at-the-start-of-v051)).

## The owner's reports

1. **The player search by email found no one** (2026-10-22, during v0.5.0, noted for v0.5.1). Searching the top bar for an active player's whole email address (a gmail.com address) answered "No player found with that email address. Only a whole address finds a player, and only one who allows it." Not investigated yet, as the owner asked for a note only. What to check first ([finding players](../social.md#the-player-search)):
   - the player's privacy setting: whether they turned off being found by email;
   - the address Google gave at sign-up against the one typed (Gmail ignores dots and treats googlemail.com as gmail.com; the search matches the stored address whole, case aside);
   - whether the player has a handle and an active account, and whether either blocked the other;
   - who searched (SysOp is never found, but searches without limit).
   Then fix whichever it is, with a test of the case.

## Carried over from v0.5.0

Decisions the subagents and I took while building v0.5.0 that the owner may revisit (details in [the v0.5.0 release notes](../releases/0.5.0.md#decisions-taken-while-building)):
- **Quests:** paid at once, no claim button; all about wins; drawn at the day's first look.
- **Achievements:** the set of 29 and their rewards (6,450 tokens in all); none from SysOp's set wins.
- **Queues:** bots at skill 4; a player alone with bots plays a practice game; a place is dropped after 150 seconds without a look.
- **Best of three:** at most five games; five minutes to sideboard; the loser chooses play or draw; "Concede the match" on the Play page acts without a confirmation.
- **Two-Headed Giant:** teammates' decks share no card but basic lands (the tournament rules' current text); the bot decks' pairing is unbalanced (red and green won 25 of 26 measured games); tables and queues do not offer it yet.
- **The library by edition:** copies naming no edition take the owned edition with the most copies; an unowned edition is switched only once, at deploy.

## Also known

- **Two-Headed Giant's cards known to misbehave** ([the spike's report](../engine/two-headed-giant.md#cards-known-to-misbehave)): "not your turn", "each player's upkeep", skip effects, "can't lose", poison counts, Assist and priority by team. The rest of the format is about two and a half to three and a half versions.
- **No browser journey plays a player's library by edition** end to end (the shop journey signs in as SysOp, who has no library).

## Decisions at the start of v0.5.1

Asked and answered on 2026-10-23, in two rounds. The owner's two new reports came with the start:
1. **Bigger cards on the Cards page.** "When in Cards > Your Planeswalker alliance tab cards, I want to be able to click the cards in there to make them bigger and easier to read." Answered: **a big picture, on every tab.** A click opens the card's picture alone, as large as the screen allows, over a dimmed page; a click, Esc or the close button closes it. The same on All cards, My library and the alliance tab, the Godlike tier's custom cards included (they could not be clicked at all).
2. **Card corners on the table.** "During gameplay some of the cards in my hand have corners that aren't cropped exactly; the box for the cards has sharp corners, but the actual cards have curved corners", with a screenshot of a hand of Swamps and Monomania beside the magnifier's Welding Jar. The cause: the table draws Scryfall's normal and large pictures, JPEGs whose corners are filled in, while the magnifier draws the PNG, whose corners are transparent. Not asked: every card the table draws gets rounded corners, in both painters.
3. **The player search by email** (the report above): the owner allowed **reading the live site's data, read-only**, to find the cause, and the testers' reports in Administration.
4. **The decisions carried over from v0.5.0 stand, with three changes:**
   - "Concede the match" asks for a confirmation.
   - The Two-Headed Giant bot decks are re-paired so neither team's colours win most games.
   - **Two-Headed Giant comes to human tables and the queues.**
5. **All of Two-Headed Giant's remainder, as v0.5.1.** Offered a split (fixes as v0.5.1, Two-Headed Giant as v0.6.0), the owner kept everything in this version and its number. The four parts ([what remains](../engine/two-headed-giant.md#what-remains-and-the-revised-estimate)):
   - **Human tables with teams** (FMT10): four humans, or humans with bot teammates or opponents; teams seated together, readiness and concession by team; both winners paid.
   - **A Two-Headed Giant queue** beside Legacy and Commander, with bots after a minute as today. **Teams form solo or with a friend:** a player queues alone and is paired, or invites a friend and they queue as a team.
   - **Team chat and the teammate's hand:** a fixed team channel in the table chat, and the teammate's hand shown on the table.
   - **The full card audit:** the misbehaving rule patterns patched (805.4d step triggers, 805.8 skips and turn control, 810.8a can't-lose, 810.10a poison, Assist, "not your turn") and the 134 cards that read the active player directly reviewed, with scripted tests for each pattern.
