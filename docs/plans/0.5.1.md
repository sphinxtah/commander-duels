# Plan for v0.5.1

Named by the owner on 2026-10-22, at the start of v0.5.0: "The next version will be v0.5.1." A patch version: fixes for what the owner and the beta testers find in v0.5.0. This proposes; the owner decides at the start, in a new chat.

## The owner's reports

1. **The player search by email found no one** (2026-10-22, during v0.5.0, noted for v0.5.1). Searching the top bar for an active player's whole email address (a gmail.com address) answered "No player found with that email address. Only a whole address finds a player, and only one who allows it." Not investigated yet, as the owner asked for a note only. What to check first ([finding players](../social.md#the-player-search)):
   - the player's privacy setting: whether they turned off being found by email;
   - the address Google gave at sign-up against the one typed (Gmail ignores dots and treats googlemail.com as gmail.com; the search matches the stored address whole, case aside);
   - whether the player has a handle and an active account, and whether either blocked the other;
   - who searched (SysOp is never found, but searches without limit).
   Then fix whichever it is, with a test of the case.

## Carried over from v0.5.0

Decisions the subagents and I took while building v0.5.0 that the owner may revisit (details in [the v0.5.0 release notes](../releases/0.5.0.md#decisions-taken-while-building)):
- **Quests:** paid at once, no claim button; all about wins; drawn at the day's first look.
- **Achievements:** the set of 29 and their rewards (6,450 tokens in all); none from SysOp's set wins.
- **Queues:** bots at skill 4; a player alone with bots plays a practice game; a place is dropped after 150 seconds without a look.
- **Best of three:** at most five games; five minutes to sideboard; the loser chooses play or draw; "Concede the match" on the Play page acts without a confirmation.
- **Two-Headed Giant:** teammates' decks share no card but basic lands (the tournament rules' current text); the bot decks' pairing is unbalanced (red and green won 25 of 26 measured games); tables and queues do not offer it yet.
- **The library by edition:** copies naming no edition take the owned edition with the most copies; an unowned edition is switched only once, at deploy.
