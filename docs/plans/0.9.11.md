# Plan for v0.9.11: the economy, decks, tables, groups, large boards and operations

The third stage of [the Preparation Framework](0.9.9.md), staged by the owner on 2026-11-11 ([the audit](audit-0.9.9.md#how-the-work-is-staged)). It builds on v0.9.10's modes and access grants. If its six foundations cannot all meet their exit criteria, a v0.9.12 takes the rest: the owner's call when v0.9.10 closes.

| Foundation (spec §) | Builds on | First piece of work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Economy and acquisition** (§11) | The token ledger, packs, supply, auctions; v0.9.10's modes | Functional access for basic lands that mints nothing; quotes charged through the ledger by their key; a simulation tool over the existing payouts and balances; proposals for the owner. Every policy off until he approves it |
| **Deck workshop and analytics** (§12) | Revisions, results by revision; v0.9.10's modes for the separations | Folders and tags; a diff of two revisions; restore into the draft; aggregates with their sample size; wishlists, missing cards, substitutions |
| **Rule Zero profiles** (§9) | Tables' settings and readiness, the lobby | A versioned profile with descriptive and enforced parts, saved presets, lobby search, the profile bound to the game |
| **Playgroups, scheduling and leagues** (§13) | Friends, tables, notifications; v0.9.10's grants | Groups and membership, events across time zones, standings from recorded results, Sealed pools apart from libraries |
| **Large boards** (§14) | The table and its controls | Token equivalence, batch combat commands checked by XMage's combat rules, auto-yields that stop when a decision changes, performance fixtures |
| **Rollout and operations** (§15) | Site settings, the audit log, leased jobs, the deploy kit; v0.9.9's recording metrics and retention | Gates for unfinished workflows, metrics for access refusals and settlement failures, fixtures, and the operations documentation |

It closes with the framework's release gate ([v0.9.9's plan](0.9.9.md#the-release-gate)) and the precise v1.0.0 backlog.
