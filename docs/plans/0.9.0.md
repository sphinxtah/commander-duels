# Plan for v0.9.0

Opened at the close of v0.8.9 on 2026-11-06 ([release notes](../releases/0.8.9.md)). The owner, at v0.8.9's start: "We'll make Carried repo-plan items to v0.9.0." Its scope is the owner's to give at the start, in a new chat.

## Carried to v0.9.0 (the owner, 2026-11-06)

- **Weathered Sentinels.** XMage's effect stops all of its controller's creatures from attacking, in every format; fixing it changes Legacy and Commander too.
- **Browser journeys** of Two-Headed Giant with four people, and of King's Table, against the real engine.
- **The campaign's tuning in play**: difficulty against a reference deck suite, the barks' triggers, the lessons' moments, the finale's incarnation ([the v0.8.9 plan](0.8.9.md#what-v080-left-read-first) has the details), and the handoff's optional post-campaign challenge, a duel's restoration after a reconnect, and voice-over.
- **The v0.6.2 to v0.6.4 notes**: unlimited auctions (Buy Now only, never ending: a limit per player, or lowering the price without withdrawing?), the basic land packs' views, the guided deck creation and the library filters.
- **The music and the scroll lock in play.**

## Scope agreed for this version (2026-11-07)

The owner, at the start, took five items and answered the start questions with the recommended choice each time:

1. **A campaign back in a deck's sleeve, checked on every read:** one its owner no longer has shows the default, and the deck keeps its choice, wearing it again once it is earned.
2. **Restoring a removed sleeve:** a removal keeps each deck's choice, and a restore puts the sleeve back on those decks. Whoever may remove may restore (`reports.manage`), audited, even past the owner's 20 sleeves.
3. **Weathered Sentinels:** fixed in every format (patch 0012).
4. **The v0.6.2 to v0.6.4 notes:** unlimited auctions' Buy Now price changes in place, and a player may have 50 up at once; the basic land packs keep their editions, scale and shelf, and gain a summary by land and edition; the v0.6.2 choices (guided deck creation offers only cards the engine plays, My library's filters start folded and reset, the alliance tile closes on Begin Alliance, old pack openings list every card pulled) stay as they are.
5. **King's Table's stall:** bot Generals press the attack on the opposing Generals beside them unless badly outmatched.

The browser journeys, the campaign's tuning and the music and scroll lock in play move to v0.9.1.

## Done

- **Sleeves on the library (the owner's bug, 2026-11-07).** A deck wearing a sleeve still showed the official back on its library pile: the pile, and the pile of backs at a long hand's end, drew the viewer's own back instead of the seat's sleeve. Both now draw their owner's sleeve, as every face-down card already did. The plain table without WebGL (left by v0.8.9) does the same now: its face-down cards, libraries and magnified backs wear each owner's sleeve.
- **King's Table attacks (the owner, 2026-11-07): not a bug.** With the house rule off, a King can't attack while both of its Generals stand beside it (809.4b); the owner's game had it off.

- **Everything in the scope above**: see [the release notes](../releases/0.9.0.md).
