# Plan for v0.5.0

Named by the owner on 2026-10-22, while v0.4.5 was being built: "Next version will be v0.5.0." The owner has not said yet what it holds. This proposes; the owner decides at the start, in a new chat.

## Carried over from v0.4.5

Decisions the subagents and I took during v0.4.5 that the owner may revisit (details in [the v0.4.5 release notes](../releases/0.4.5.md)):
1. **SysOp is exempt from a table's rules,** under its standing full access: its decks need only cards in the game, at any table.
2. **A pack sheet with no playable card at all** gives nothing for that slot, so the pack is a card smaller. No live booster is known to have one; the live shop data was not checked.
3. **A staff suspension takes the card out of every draft,** and restoring it does not put it back.
4. **After a reset** the old handle is free for anyone, and the profile keeps the ledgers' history (for example, "1 win counted, −1 set").
5. **The bots' starter and fixture decks are not checked against a table's rules;** none holds a banned card today.
6. **XMage 1.4.62 is still the newest release.** Six prepare cards (Whiplash Wordsmith and five Alchemy cards) and about 2,600 others wait for a release that implements them; the next engine update brings back whatever it learns.

## Decisions at the start of v0.5.0

Asked and answered on 2026-10-22, in three rounds. The owner chose all four candidate features rather than one:
1. **Quests, achievements and statistics** (PROG04, PROG07, PROG08).
   - **Daily quests read from results.** Three quests a day from a pool, judged from the server's own record of a finished game and the deck played (win a Commander game, win with a red deck, beat a skill-4 bot, win at a human table). Each pays 100–250 tokens; one swap a day; the day resets at midnight, Arizona time. No quests about events inside a game.
   - **Achievements are badges that pay once.** Each is a badge on the profile (a player pins up to three); earning one pays a one-time token reward through the ledger.
   - **Totals public, history private.** A profile's totals (wins and losses, by format, achievements) are seen by whoever may open the profile; the game-by-game history (date, format, opponents, deck, result, length) only by its player and SysOp.
2. **Public queues and rematches** (NET01, AI06).
   - **Bots after a wait.** A queue per format: Legacy for two, and Commander for two, three or four seats, each size its own queue. After 60 seconds with too few players, the game starts with bots in the empty seats, unless the player chose to wait for humans only.
   - **Casual only.** No ranked queue or rating (NET04) yet: with about 20 beta testers a humans-only ranked queue would mostly stand empty.
   - **Rematch** at the end of a game.
3. **Best-of-three Legacy with sideboards** (FMT05–06). **Each game won counts:** every game won inside a match counts as a win and pays tokens as a single game does; the match result is recorded for the statistics.
4. **Two-Headed Giant** (FMT08–10, T09): the spike on a patched XMage, and, **if it holds, a first playable Two-Headed Giant practice game against bots** on the Play page, marked as early. If it does not hold, the go/no-go note with a revised estimate, and the Play page keeps "Coming soon".
5. **The library shows the cards** and every tab of the Cards page (All cards, My library, the alliance's) gets the same Pictures/List switch, remembered in the browser.
6. **The six decisions carried over from v0.4.5 stand** as they are.
