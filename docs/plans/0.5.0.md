# Plan for v0.5.0

Named by the owner on 2026-10-22, while v0.4.5 was being built: "Next version will be v0.5.0." The owner has not said yet what it holds. This proposes; the owner decides at the start, in a new chat.

## Carried over from v0.4.5

Decisions the subagents and I took during v0.4.5 that the owner may revisit (details in [the v0.4.5 release notes](../releases/0.4.5.md)):
1. **SysOp is exempt from a table's rules,** under its standing full access: its decks need only cards in the game, at any table.
2. **A pack sheet with no playable card at all** gives nothing for that slot, so the pack is a card smaller. No live booster is known to have one; the live shop data was not checked.
3. **A staff suspension takes the card out of every draft,** and restoring it does not put it back.
4. **After a reset** the old handle is free for anyone, and the profile keeps the ledgers' history (for example, "1 win counted, −1 set").
5. **The bots' starter and fixture decks are not checked against a table's rules;** none holds a banned card today.
6. **XMage 1.4.62 is still the newest release.** Six prepare cards (Whiplash Wordsmith and five Alchemy cards) and about 2,600 others wait for a release that implements them; the next engine update brings back whatever it learns.
