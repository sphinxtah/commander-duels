# Plan for v0.4.2: fixes for what the owner and the beta testers find

Prepared on 2026-10-21, when v0.4.1 closed. The owner named v0.4.2 at the start of v0.4.1: once the site is ready for about 20 beta testers (v0.4.1), v0.4.2 fixes the bugs the owner and the testers find. Their reports lead; this plan gathers what earlier versions left open, so the first chat of v0.4.2 can start from it. It proposes; the owner decides at the start.

## Decisions at the start of v0.4.2

Agreed on 2026-10-21, with the owner's own list:

- **The 64-game limit, live.** After 0.4.1's deploy the VM's `.env` still said `CD_GAMES_MAX=6`, so the engine ran 6 games ("at most 6 games at once" in its start line). `deploy.sh` now raises that 6 to 64 when it deploys over 0.1.1–0.4.1, and keeps any other value.
- **The owner's report: an alliance's cards.** "When a player changes their Planeswalker alignment, their cards don't update to that Planeswalker's playable cards", and Cards → Godlike still showed The Sphinx's custom cards to SysOp allied with another Planeswalker. The API's library did follow the alliance; the Cards page's "Godlike cards" tab listed every custom card to any Godlike account, and SysOp, who builds from the whole catalog, saw no alliance anywhere. Chosen fix (the first option): the tab becomes the allied Planeswalker's ("Teferi's cards"), for everyone including SysOp, with The Sphinx's custom cards only while allied with The Sphinx; the deck builder's Godlike list likewise; SysOp keeps building from everything else.
- **Ended sessions:** kept as now, until the account is deleted, as the privacy policy says.
- **The terms line:** added under the sign-in button.
- **Testers' reports:** none yet; they come later in this version or the next.
- **The earlier defaults** (item 5 below) stay as they are, unchanged by this version.

## Added during v0.4.2 (the owner, 2026-10-21)

- **A pop-up for the two free starter decks**, the ten decks with check boxes, its words a call to action, opening each time a player signs in until they have chosen; the commander's card as each deck's picture. Then, in the owner's words, "we're making it a wizard to set up their gameplay experience": a big welcome with a quick look at the game in screenshots, then the player's name (the handle, which later in-game messaging will show), the two free decks and the alliance.
- **The ten starter decks in Leng's shop**, on a tab of their own. The first two are free and leave the 1,000 welcome tokens untouched; after them each deck is priced "appropriately based on the cards in them", and sells once (the owner's answer).
- **No Planeswalker without a playable card:** the ten whose every card the engine cannot play leave the roster, with their portraits.
- **Close v0.4.2 when done**, commit and push, and note v0.4.3: more bug fixing and features, starting with artwork for Leng's shop packs ([the v0.4.3 plan](0.4.3.md)).

## Start with

1. **The reports.** The owner's own list, and the testers' problem reports in Administration. Each is reproduced first (a test or a journey that fails), then fixed, and its report set to fixed or closed. Anything that turns out to be a new feature rather than a defect is noted for a minor version instead.
2. **What v0.4.1 could not check by hand,** on the live site after its deploy:
   - the Google side: the consent screen in production, the branding check passed, and a stranger's Google account reaching the waiting screen;
   - the whole path of a new tester: request, approval, handle, starter decks, a first game, a report with a phone screenshot;
   - a screenshot over 750 KB through the TSH proxy, once its block for `/api/v1/reports` is in;
   - "Delete account" on a real account, and the daily removal of requests never approved (its first audit entry, `account.unapproved_deleted`);
   - many players at once: the engine's log line per bot ("Human game … bot …") shows whether any search was cut off or never started.
3. **What v0.4.0 could not check by hand,** still open:
   - the custom cards in a real game, seen from both sides of the table;
   - a human table between two real players, including an away vote;
   - a pack bought and opened against the live catalog;
   - a real win's tokens, and the back pay of past wins in each player's ledger.
4. **Found during v0.4.1** ([the v0.4.1 plan](0.4.1.md#found-during-v041-left-for-v042)):
   - SysOp's custom card upload previews with a `blob:` address, which the site's Content-Security-Policy refuses;
   - ended sessions keep their address and browser until the account is deleted;
   - the terms say "by signing in, you agree", and the landing page does not say so.
5. **Choices the builds made without asking,** for the owner to keep or change:
   - v0.4.0: a table's game waits up to an hour without an answer (practice games 15 minutes); the host leaving closes the table, and an unstarted table closes after 3 hours; no pack below 10 tokens, and a new set sells at 500 until priced; the booster data job weekly; the private set's name, "Commander Duels: The Sphinx";
   - v0.4.1: deleting an account refuses while it is seated in a running game, rather than conceding for it; the 30 days count from the first sign-in, not from a refusal; reports are limited to 20 a day, SysOp exempt; a report's first reading is audited; the counts, not the reports themselves, prefix the tab's title.
6. **Housekeeping.** The old git worktrees under `.claude/worktrees/` on the development PC, those of v0.4.1 included, still make `git gc` report "Permission denied". All their branches are merged.
