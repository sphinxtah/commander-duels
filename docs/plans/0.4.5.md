# Plan for v0.4.5: the facelift

Noted on 2026-10-21, after v0.4.2 closed, from the owner's words. v0.4.3 (pack artwork, player search, friends, presence, the notification bell; [plan](0.4.3.md)) and v0.4.4 (chat; [plan](0.4.4.md)) come first. This proposes; the owner decides at the start.

## What the owner asked for

"v0.4.5 will be a facelift version."

1. **A modern main menu.** "Modernize the main menu areas... give it the flash and pizzazz of a videogame. Make it look official and launch ready in style." Today the app is a sidebar of pages (Play, Tables, Campaign, Learn, Decks, Cards, Shop, Collection, Profile, Settings) over the site's dark green and gold.
2. **No Campaign.** "Remove the campaign stuff altogether, we probably won't have that for a very long time." The sidebar's Campaign page is a placeholder for the scope's M4 (`web/src/App.tsx`).
3. **No Collection area.** "Get rid of the Collection area." Also a placeholder page today; a player's cards are on Cards → My library.
4. **No unplayable cards.** "We'll remove all of the 'not playable' cards from the game entirely." Today every card stays searchable and shows why it is not playable (the catalog's statuses, [catalog](../catalog.md#statuses-cat06cat09)); the change hides every card the engine cannot play from the whole site.
5. **Tournament-illegal cards stay.** "We will keep tournament illegal cards, though, as they are NOT banned here."
6. **Banned cards only where the table's host allows them.** "For gameplay, the person starting the table has the option to allow banned cards with a checkbox. The decks must be checked before entry so banned cards don't end up in non-banned games." Since v0.4.0 any legal deck sits at any table ("anything goes", [human tables](../tables.md)); this adds the host's choice and a check of every deck before it is seated.

## For the start questions

- **The look.** What "videogame" means here: a title screen with the banner art and motion (Magic Duels' own menus, the game screens of Arena or Hearthstone), large tiles for Play, Decks, Shop and the Alliance, sound on the menus, the portrait and tier crest front and centre. How far it goes: the menus and the shell only, or the pages behind them too (Cards, Decks, Profile, Administration). Reduced motion, larger text and the phone layout keep working.
- **Learn.** The sidebar's other placeholder: kept, removed with Campaign, or made into the game's help (the scope's UI10–UI11).
- **What "not playable" removes.** Search, card pages, the deck builder, libraries, the alliance lists, Leng's packs (a pack can pull a card the engine cannot play: skip it in the draw, replace it, or keep the pack's real contents), and decks that already hold such cards (the catalog keeps every card for its history; only what players see changes). Whether SysOp and administrators still see them, for reviewing the catalog.
- **"Tournament illegal" and "banned".** Scryfall gives each card a legality per format: legal, not legal (for example a card outside the format's sets, an Un-card or a playtest card), restricted or banned. Which of these the owner means by "tournament illegal" (kept, so playable anywhere) and by "banned" (allowed only at tables whose host ticks the box), for Legacy and for Commander, and whether practice games against bots get the same box.
- **The check before entry.** Every deck is checked when its player sits down and again when the game starts, against the table's setting, so a deck changed after it was seated cannot bring a banned card in.
