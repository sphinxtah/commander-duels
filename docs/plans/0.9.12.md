# Plan for v0.9.12: the economy, decks, tables and large boards

The third stage of [the Preparation Framework](0.9.9.md), staged by the owner on 2026-11-11 as v0.9.11 ([the audit](audit-0.9.9.md#how-the-work-is-staged)); v0.9.11 went to a game's start after v0.9.10 reached the live site ([v0.9.11's notes](../releases/0.9.11.md)), so this stage is v0.9.12. It builds on v0.9.10's modes and access grants.

## Decisions at the start of v0.9.12

The owner, 2026-11-13, before any work:

- **Two versions.** Six foundations were staged here. v0.9.12 takes four (the economy, the deck workshop, Rule Zero profiles, large boards); [v0.9.13](0.9.13.md) takes playgroups and leagues, rollout and operations, the framework's release gate and the precise v1.0.0 backlog.
- **GitHub as in v0.9.11.** The branch is pushed from the workspace; the merge to `main`, the release and the private images go through the owner's browser.
- **Carried from v0.9.11:** Cryptic Spires, played exactly.
- **Campaign opponents play no Power Nine and no Reserved List card** ("I also want the decks for the campaign opponents to NOT have any copies of the power 9 or other alpha/beta/ultra expensive cards"). Of three readings he chose the Power Nine and the Reserved List only: "I still want the higher level wards to produce some awesome rares." It covers every ward, the court and the private finale; players keep the decks they already won.
- **The mode policies** proposed in v0.9.10 are approved: a sandbox game earns nothing, a game with a borrowed deck earns as a table game, and leagues keep standings and statistics and pay nothing until a league reward policy is approved.
- **Economy proposals to prepare:** functional basic lands, a pool of starter staples, quest catch-up and streaks. Every one stays off until he approves it with its numbers in hand.
- **Dropped: a path to a specific card.** No quotes and no purchase of a named card, in v0.9.12 or v1.0.0: "I've changed my mind on those. I want it to be more realistic."
- **Dropped: Rule Zero disclosures.** No computed or declared deck disclosures and no deck analysis, in v0.9.12 or v1.0.0, for the same reason. The table profile itself stays.
