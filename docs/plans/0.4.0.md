# Plan for v0.4.0: tokens and Leng's card shop

Recorded on 2026-10-20, during v0.3.0, from the owner's instructions. The owner named v0.4.0 as the version that brings **a token economy** and **packs**, bought with tokens from the game's store, **Leng's card shop**. [v0.3.1](0.3.1.md) stages it: players' own card libraries, the ledger, levels and fair bots. This plan records what the owner asked for, a first sketch of the rules, and the questions for the start of that thread. It proposes; the decisions belong to the start of v0.4.0.

## What the owner asked for

- **Tokens for winning against bots: 5 to 20, by the bot's difficulty.** Beating several bots at once multiplies the tokens: each defeated bot pays its own amount.
- **Tokens for winning against humans: 50 to 500, by the level of the player beaten** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, and so on up to Godlike), spread over that range by exact level.
- **Packs, bought with tokens in Leng's card shop.** A pack's price follows how rare the pack is: a Revised pack might cost 150,000 tokens, a newer one 500.
- **Every pack from Revised to the current set** is on sale.
- **Real boosters.** Their contents are random, but follow the pull ratios and luck of real packs of that set.
- **Alpha and Beta** are never sold: they are won only in tournaments, which come many versions later.

## The rewards, as a first proposal

**Against bots.** The practice table's bot skill runs from 1 to 6. Spread evenly over 5 to 20, that is 3 tokens a step, paid per defeated bot:

| Bot skill | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tokens per bot | 5 | 8 | 11 | 14 | 17 | 20 |

A Commander win over three skill-4 bots pays 3 × 14 = 42 tokens. If bots at one table may one day have different skills, each pays its own amount.

**Against humans.** Six tiers spread evenly over 50 to 500 is 90 tokens a tier:

| Tier | 1st (Bronze) | 2nd (Silver) | 3rd (Gold) | 4th (Platinum) | 5th (Mythic) | 6th (Godlike) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tokens | 50 | 140 | 230 | 320 | 410 | 500 |

- **The six tiers,** as the owner set them on 2026-10-20 ([v0.3.1's tier list](0.3.1.md#the-tier-list)): Bronze (from the start), Silver (10 wins), Gold (100), Platinum (1,000), Mythic (10,000) and Godlike (100,000).
- **"By exact level", as a smooth curve.** If "exact level" means the beaten player's exact standing, not only their tier, `tokens = 50 + 90 × log10(1 + wins)`, capped at 500, gives the same amounts at the tier thresholds:

| Wins | 0 | 10 | 100 | 1,000 | 10,000 | 100,000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tokens | 50 | 144 | 230 | 320 | 410 | 500 |

  In between, it rises smoothly (for example 50 wins pays 204).
- **Several humans beaten at once** pay each one's amount, as bots do.

## Leng's card shop and its packs

- **The shop** is a new page: the packs on sale with their price in tokens, the player's balance, and buying, then opening, a pack. Opening is one durable transaction: it takes the tokens, fixes the contents once, and adds the cards to the player's library for good. Asking again returns the same pack (PROG02).
- **Which packs.** Every set's booster from Revised (April 1994) to the current set. Not Alpha or Beta. Settled at the start:
  - **The 1993–94 sets before Revised.** Unlimited, Arabian Nights and Antiquities: in, or kept with Alpha and Beta for tournaments?
  - **Which products count:** every expansion and core set; the Masters and other draft sets; Commander products and other supplements; and, since 2020, the separate draft, set, collector and play boosters.
- **Pull ratios.** Packs follow each set's real booster:
  - its slot counts (Revised: 11 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 rare);
  - its print-sheet frequencies;
  - mythic rares since Shards of Alara (2008), about one pack in eight;
  - foils since Urza's Legacy (1999);
  - the special slots of each modern booster type.
  - **The data.** MTGJSON's set files describe the boosters of many sets: sheets, the weight of each card in a sheet, and the weight of each booster layout. Check its licence and its coverage set by set at the start; a set without data needs a reviewed definition before it goes on sale.
  - **The scope's caution (PROG02)** says virtual packs must not imply retail odds. The owner wants real ratios, so the shop follows the published collation where it is known, and says where its data comes from.
- **Prices, by pack rarity.** The owner's examples (Revised 150,000, a new set 500) are about the ratio of real sealed booster prices, roughly 300 to 1. A proposal: each set's price is 500 tokens times its booster's market price divided by a current booster's, rounded. An administrator edits the price list, with every change audited. Where the market prices come from (Scryfall has none for sealed product) is a start question.
- **Pulled cards are the player's own.** Unlike an alliance's cards, they stay in the library whatever the alliance (v0.3.1's rule), and duplicates count.

## What the scope says

- **No real money.** No payment processor, cash store, cash-out or card trading. Tokens are virtual currency only (PROG03, PAR18).
- **The ledger.** Append-only, with unique grant and redeem keys and reconciliation. A balance is derived from it or cached in the same transaction. Administrative grants need a reason and an audit entry (PROG03).
- **Rewards from committed results.** Never from what a browser reports, never twice after a reconnect, replay or worker retry, and with the policy's revision recorded on each reward (PROG04, PROG05, NET10). The backlog adds: durable result processing before any rewards.
- **Anti-farming.** PROG05 asks for caps and anti-farming rules for bot and practice games. With bots paying up to 20 tokens a win, settle at the start:
  - whether short games, turn-limit endings, games against skill-1 bots or concessions pay;
  - and whether a daily cap applies.
- **Ownership** is an application progression concept, not ownership of a physical or licensed card (scope section 2).

## Questions for the start of v0.4.0

- **Human rewards.** By tier (the first table) or by exact wins (the smooth curve)?
- **Bot rewards.**
  - Per bot as proposed.
  - Which endings pay.
  - Any daily cap.
  - Whether a practice game against bots still pays once v0.3.1's fair bots are in, or only after the AI02 audit is done.
- **Packs on sale.**
  - The pre-Revised sets.
  - The kinds of product.
  - Modern booster types.
- **Prices.** The formula from market prices (and from where), or a hand-set list.
- **The booster data.** MTGJSON's licence and coverage; reviewed definitions for the gaps.
- **Starting balance and other sources.** A new player's starting tokens. Any other source of tokens (quests, PROG04) or other uses (cosmetics, PROG06).
- **SysOp's grants of packs.** Tokens are settled (below); may SysOp also give packs directly, and may administrators give tokens too, or SysOp alone?

## SysOp gives tokens

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-20: **SysOp can give tokens to any player by hand**, just as SysOp can set any player's number of wins (v0.3.1).
- In Administration, beside the win count.
- Each grant is a ledger entry with its own key, the amount and a reason, and it is audited (PROG03).
- A grant of tokens is kept apart from tokens earned by games, so balances and statistics can tell them apart.
- SysOp's own account may receive them too, for testing.
