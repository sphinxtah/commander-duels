# Plan for v0.6.1

Named by the owner on 2026-10-23, during v0.6.0: "The next version will be v0.6.1. We will continue the ongoing projects leftover from before and then some bug fixing." A patch version: the projects carried over from earlier versions first, then the bugs the owner and the testers find.

## The projects carried over

From v0.5.1, still open ([its release notes](../releases/0.5.1.md#known-not-done), [the v0.6.0 plan](0.6.0.md#carried-over-from-v051)):
- **Two-Headed Giant's team priority and team choices** (805.5–805.7) and attacks legal as a whole (805.10b): teammates still receive priority and declare attackers, blockers and triggers one after the other. The format stays marked Early until then.
- **The rare cards the audit left wrong**, each with its reason in [the audit's review](../engine/two-headed-giant-audit.csv): Urabrask's draw replacement, nine cards that take the turn's holder as "the attacking player", four watchers keyed by the holder, Storage Matrix, Elkin Lair, Brine Elemental's double skip.
- **A browser journey that plays Two-Headed Giant with four people** against the real engine.
- **A bot cannot take control of a turn** (XMage refuses at the pin): keep the refusal, give bots a fixed rule, or patch XMage so an AI acts for the controlled player. Word of Command and Opposition Agent have no test of their own.

## From v0.6.0

Decisions taken while building Tivit's Auction House that the owner may revisit ([the auction house](../auctions.md)):
- **Basic lands at deploy.** Migration 0028 grants every player the basics their past packs pulled and their starter decks held. A deck that holds more (built before v0.6.0 from free basics, or a grandfathered v0.3.1 deck) is collection-locked until fixed. If many players are caught, a one-time grant of basics could follow.
- **An account reset or deleted while leading another auction:** its bid is returned and the auction runs on with no leader, its starting bid the least again.
- **SysOp's listings** come from its unlimited library, any card the game can play in a finish it was printed in, up to 20 at once (the owner's standing rule that SysOp has everything); administrators sell only copies granted to them. Cancelling an auction is SysOp's alone; the log is also open to administrators given `auctions.manage`.
- **Buy Now's colour** is the royal purple of Tivit's robes, the page's one accent beside the app's gold.

Also known:
- Under the load of the whole verify suite at once on Vite's development server, `decks.spec.ts` and `shop.spec.ts` can pass their 30 s; alone they take about 15 s.
- SysOp's practice games through the API once printed "keepalive ping failed" from the test script's WebSocket while passing.

## Bug fixing

The owner's and the testers' reports (Administration → Reports) lead, then anything the owner names at the start. The owner's list so far:
- **Deleting a deck asks nothing** (the owner, 2026-10-24): it should ask for confirmation first, as conceding a match does.

## The top bar (the owner, 2026-10-24)

With a screenshot of the main menu: "In v0.6.1 it's time to retire the menu items at the very top left. I want Home to be the main card/tile area in the middle. The global search should be centered on the tiles at the top."
- **The top bar's page links go** (Home, Play, Tables, Decks, Cards, Shop): the main menu's tiles are the way to every page, and the brand at the top left leads home, as the band's "Main menu" button does on every other page.
- **The player search moves to the middle** of the top bar, centred over the tiles' column.
- **Answered by the owner the same day:** "This is a PC game. Phone support really focuses on buying and tearing packs, and the social features. Gameplay is complicated and difficult on a small screen." So phones need no way between pages beyond the tiles; on a phone the pages that matter are the shop and opening packs, Tivit's Auction House (added by the owner: buying, bidding and selling must be easy on a phone), and friends, chat and the bell. The tokens, friends, bell and account "can stay there for now", at the top right.

## Start questions

At the start of v0.6.1, before any work: which carried-over projects to take, in what order; the owner's list of bugs; and whether to revisit any of v0.6.0's decisions above. One round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults.

## Decisions at the start of v0.6.1

Asked and answered on 2026-10-24, the recommended option each time:
- **The carried-over projects:** Two-Headed Giant's team priority and team choices (805.5–805.7) with attacks legal as a whole (805.10b). The audit's rare cards and the four-player browser journey stay carried over to a later version.
- **The order:** the top bar and the bugs first, then Two-Headed Giant.
- **A bot taking control of a turn:** patch XMage so the AI makes the controlled player's decisions, with a test each for Word of Command and Opposition Agent. Taken after team priority.
- **v0.6.0's decisions:** all kept as they are.
- **The bugs:** the owner named none beyond deleting a deck without a confirmation.

## Added during v0.6.1

- **Alpha and Beta in Leng's shop** (the owner, 2026-10-24: "Also incorporate alpha and beta packs in Leng's shop for sale"). This reverses the decision of v0.4.0 that kept them for tournaments. The owner's answers:
  - they are sold now, and a tournament may still give them as prizes later;
  - priced on the same market curve as every pack (an Alpha booster's market price is several times Unlimited's);
  - their cards follow the real print runs, so a printing stops being dealt once as many copies as were printed are in libraries;
  - boosters and starter decks, as for Unlimited.
- **Own auctions missing from the market** (the owner, 2026-10-24, with screenshots): "I posted 2 cards to the auction house and they aren't showing up on the listing page." The transfer list showed both, but the market's search, and its count of live auctions, left out the viewer's own listings by design (as Madden's market does). Fixed: the market shows them, badged as the viewer's own.
- **Sign out and Profile's tabs** (the owner, 2026-10-24, before the close, with a screenshot of the account's menu): "Move Sign Out from profile to the dropdown in the upper right corner. Also, in Profile, lets get some tabs in there to clean it up... the scrolling goes on for too long. The default (first) tab should be Achievements, second tab should be Alliance, and the rest are up to you for order. The sign out everywhere will stay in this area."
- **The close-out** (the owner, 2026-10-24): "When you're finished with all this work, close the version, commit, and push to Github. The next version will be v0.6.2."
