# Plan for v0.9.1

Opened at the close of v0.9.0 on 2026-11-07 ([release notes](../releases/0.9.0.md)). Its scope is the owner's to give at the start, in a new chat.

## Carried to v0.9.1

- **Browser journeys** of Two-Headed Giant with four people, and of King's Table, against the real engine.
- **The campaign's tuning in play**: difficulty against a reference deck suite, the barks' triggers, the lessons' moments, the finale's incarnation ([the v0.8.9 plan](0.8.9.md#what-v080-left-read-first) has the details), and the handoff's optional post-campaign challenge, a duel's restoration after a reconnect, and voice-over.
- **The music and the scroll lock in play.**

## Noted during v0.9.0

- **King's Table's Generals** press the attack by a plain rule (an untapped blocker that kills and survives keeps a creature home). If games still stall, weigh the King's defence and the whole board, or default practice games to the house rule on.
- **The defender choice** now offers only the defenders an attacker can attack (patch 0012, every format). Watch for a restriction XMage checks only after the declaration.
- **Decks cleared by a removal before v0.9.0** don't come back with a restore.

## To look at in play

From v0.9.0: a sleeved deck's library pile and long hand at every table size and on the plain table; a removal and a restore on the live site; Weathered Sentinels in Commander; King's Table with the house rule off; a land pack's summary on a phone. From v0.8.9: sleeves on a phone, the profile pop-up over the table, an administrator's eye icon.

## Bug fixing

The owner's and the testers' reports (Administration → Reports) lead, then anything the owner names at the start.

## Start questions

At the start of v0.9.1, before any work: which of the carried items to take, and the owner's list of bugs. One round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults.
