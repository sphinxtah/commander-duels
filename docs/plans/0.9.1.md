# Plan for v0.9.1

Opened at the close of v0.9.0 on 2026-11-07 ([release notes](../releases/0.9.0.md)); closed the same night ([release notes](../releases/0.9.1.md)). The next plan is [v0.9.2's](0.9.2.md).

## Scope agreed for this version (2026-11-07)

The owner's new item: "Instead of the Details link for the card, just make the cards themselves clickable to pop up and show details." From the start questions, the owner took:

- **Clickable cards everywhere:** the opened pack (pictures, and a land pack's Every card) and the auction house's item panel.
- **King's Table's house rule on** in practice games.
- **Decks cleared before v0.9.0:** dropped, unrecoverable (below).
- **Campaign tuning:** the training hand and the lessons' moments, and the difficulty measured.
- No bugs beyond those; the Two-Headed Giant and King's Table browser journeys stay carried.

Added during the work (22:00): "double check to be sure the The One Ring special printing actually only has 1 total in library inventory, and that user Sphinx has it," the 001/001 Post Malone owns.

## Done

All of the above; see the release notes. **Decks cleared by a removal before v0.9.0** can't come back: v0.8.9's removal cleared `decks.sleeve`, its audit entry named only the sleeve, and games don't store sleeves, so nothing records which decks wore it. Their owners choose the sleeve again.

## Carried to v0.9.1

- **Browser journeys** of Two-Headed Giant with four people, and of King's Table, against the real engine.
- **The campaign's tuning in play**: difficulty against a reference deck suite, the barks' triggers, the lessons' moments, the finale's incarnation ([the v0.8.9 plan](0.8.9.md#what-v080-left-read-first) has the details), and the handoff's optional post-campaign challenge, a duel's restoration after a reconnect, and voice-over.
- **The music and the scroll lock in play.**

## Noted during v0.9.0

- **King's Table's Generals** press the attack by a plain rule (an untapped blocker that kills and survives keeps a creature home). If games still stall, weigh the King's defence and the whole board, or default practice games to the house rule on.
- **The defender choice** now offers only the defenders an attacker can attack (patch 0012, every format). Watch for a restriction XMage checks only after the declaration.
- **Decks cleared by a removal before v0.9.0** don't come back with a restore.
