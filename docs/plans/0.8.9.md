## What v0.8.0 left (read first)

- **The private images at the tag.** `commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.8.0` (now with the campaign finale's content in its plugin) and `commander-duels-web-sphinx:0.8.0` (now with song 21) must exist before `deploy.sh` can pull them: the private repository's two workflows, run (or scheduled) once the tag's run has published the public `0.8.0` images.
- **The campaign in play.** Play it through from a fresh account, on the live site (the private finale) and in the local stack (the public stand-in): the opening, every ward's scene, the seals, the chapter cards, a loss and its retreat, the endings. Then tune:
  - **Difficulty.** The handoff's difficulty contract is a target to measure, not a property of the order: a reference deck suite against each ward, win rates, the turn of the first threat and of the win, inactive turns. The bots' skill (1 to 6) and the decks are the levers; the handoff asks for no life or mana bonuses. Tibalt must not threaten lethal before the player's sixth turn in Learn Commander (T04); his deck has no early lethal line, but the skill-1 bot is not scripted.
  - **The barks' triggers.** Each is read from the views and the log ([the campaign](../campaign.md#at-the-table)); some are near signs of the handoff's events (a counterspell by "countered" in the log, a recursion by a card leaving the graveyard as the battlefield grows). Watch which fire early, late or never.
  - **The lessons' moments** in the first duel, and the opening hand: the handoff's training seven (three Plains, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Raise the Alarm, Pacifism) needs an engine that can stack a library; the seed only fixes the shuffles.
  - **The finale's incarnation**: its first crossing at 15 life, a countered first cast, removal by destruction, exile and bounce, the recast for {W}{U}{B}{R}{G}, Doubling Season and Deepglow Skate, the ultimate, and the bot's use of the reserve. `CampaignReserveTest` covers the crossing with a public planeswalker; the rest is the engine's rules, untested in a scripted game.
- **The spoils in play** (the owner's word during v0.8.0): each warden's deck in Decks and its cards in My library after the first win, the tokens on the profile, nothing twice, and what 16,383,000 tokens a campaign does to the shop and the auction house.
- **The rewards' cosmetics.** The card backs (Broken Writ, Unclaimed World, Broken Throne, Fractured Throne) and the titles (Unbowed, Witness of the Unbound, Concordance Breaker) are recorded and listed on the Campaign page; they do not yet show on the table's card backs or on profiles. The handoff's optional post-campaign challenge (and its badge) is not built.
- **Not built from the handoff:** a duel's mid-game restoration after a reconnect (the engine has none; a lost game returns to the ward's start), voice-over, and the separate AI profile that would defer the finale's immediate wins (the handoff's optional practice mode).
