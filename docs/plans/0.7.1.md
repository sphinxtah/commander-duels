# Plan for v0.7.1

Not planned: the owner asked for two more things on the evening of 2026-10-30, after v0.7.0 was tagged, as additions to it. This session's GitHub access could create a tag but not move one, and the owner chose to tag them as v0.7.1 rather than move `v0.7.0`. The music meant for v0.7.1 is now [v0.7.2](0.7.2.md).

## Scope agreed (2026-10-30)

1. **The bots' difficulty.** The owner: "The bot difficulty isn't spread properly. 6 difficulty is fine, but 1 difficulty is still very difficult... the 1 bot makes intelligent choices, when it should be playing like an amateur. Fix that, tag, push and officially close the version." XMage's skill changes little a player feels (its search is as deep at skill 1 as at 4; attacks, blocks, targets and choices are the same heuristics at every skill), and v0.6.5's flat share of passed priorities mostly delayed a spell to the next main phase. A `FairBot` now has a `Profile` for its skill, the habits of a weaker player, each a share that falls steeply with the skill and is gone at 6 ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#skill-and-habits-v071)). Skills 1 to 3 think one second per level. The search and its depth are unchanged: a depth below four would score a spell while it still sits on the stack, and the bot would stop casting.
2. **The auction house's sections fold.** The owner, with a hundred active listings: "Make the sections collapsible. I have to scroll a really long time to reach the sold cards. Default is collapsed." The transfer list's and the targets' sections fold, folded at first, with the count and Clear on the folded bar ([the auction house](../auctions.md#transfer-list)).

## Closed

Closed on 2026-10-31 with the tag `v0.7.1` on the close commit ([release notes](../releases/0.7.1.md)). Everything else open goes to [v0.7.2](0.7.2.md).
