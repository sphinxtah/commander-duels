# Plan for v0.6.5

Named by the owner on 2026-10-25, at the start of v0.6.4: "Make those changes, close the version, commit, and push to github. The next version will be v0.6.5 where we'll decide on it in the beginning of the next chat." A patch version. Its scope is the owner's to choose at the start, in a new chat; this lists what v0.6.4 left.

## Scope agreed (2026-10-25)

The owner, at the start: "We need to make the power 9 back to normal ratios again, instead of the 5x ratios. Fix that so they don't get over-pulled, close the version, commit, and push to github. Next version will be v0.7.0." Asked whether the ten dual lands boosted with them go back too, the owner chose both. So v0.6.5 removes v0.6.2's boost entirely (`api/shop.py` `BOOSTED`, `api/collation.py` `boost_sheet`): every card in a pack comes as often as MTGJSON's sheet says. Nothing else is in this version; the items below carry on to [v0.7.0](0.7.0.md).

## Carried over

v0.6.4 brought only the basic land packs, so everything [its plan](0.6.4.md) listed is still open:
- **"Add to deck" from My library** (the owner, 2026-10-24): each library card offers "Add to deck", with a pop-up to choose the deck.
- **A browser journey that plays Two-Headed Giant with four people** against the real engine.
- **Weathered Sentinels** ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md)): XMage's effect stops all of its controller's creatures from attacking, in every format, not only the Sentinels. Fixing it changes Legacy and Commander too.
- **From v0.6.2**, for the owner to keep or revisit: guided deck creation proposes only cards the engine plays and no search term finds stand-ins; old openings show every card they pulled; My library's filters start folded and reset; the alliance tile closes as soon as "Begin Alliance" is clicked; the boosted chase cards fill about 82% of Alpha's rare slots (taken in v0.6.5, below); Two-Headed Giant's choices for Invasion Plans, Contested Game Ball and Tahngarth, First Mate.
- **From v0.6.3:** unlimited auctions are Buy Now only and never end; the owner may want a limit on how many a player keeps up, or a way to lower the price without withdrawing.

## From v0.6.4

- **Basic land packs** ([the shop](../shop.md#basic-land-packs-v064)), for the owner to keep or revisit: the editions counted (core, expansion, masters, draft innovation, starter and Commander sets; not Secret Lair, other boxed sets, duel decks or promos), the straight-line scale by release rank (Alpha about once in 8,900 lands of its type), snow-covered basics and Wastes left out, and their place on a shelf of their own above the eras. A land pack opens at the counter as any pack, its 40 or 200 cards face down to turn over or "Reveal all"; a view made for big packs (counts by land and edition) may be wanted.
- **Not run locally in v0.6.4:** the browser journeys (hosted CI runs them), and on native Windows the two test files that hang there (`tests/test_matches.py`, `tests/test_tables.py`).

## Bug fixing

The owner's and the testers' reports (Administration → Reports) lead, then anything the owner names at the start.

## Start questions

At the start of v0.6.5, before any work: what the version is for, which carried-over items to take, and the owner's list of bugs. One round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults.
