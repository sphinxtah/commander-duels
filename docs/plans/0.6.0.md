# Plan for v0.6.0

Named by the owner on 2026-10-23, at the end of v0.5.1: "Next version will be v0.6.0. We will decide later what to work on for it." A minor version: new features. Its scope is the owner's to choose at the start, in a new chat; this lists what is carried over and what the backlog offers.

## Carried over from v0.5.1

What v0.5.1 left ([its release notes](../releases/0.5.1.md#known-not-done)):
- **Two-Headed Giant's team priority and team choices** (805.5–805.7) and attacks legal as a whole (805.10b): about a version. Teammates still receive priority and declare attackers, blockers and triggers one after the other. The format stays marked Early until then.
- **The rare cards the audit left wrong**, each with its reason in [the audit's review](../engine/two-headed-giant-audit.csv): Urabrask's draw replacement, nine cards that take the turn's holder as "the attacking player", four watchers keyed by the holder, Storage Matrix, Elkin Lair, Brine Elemental's double skip.
- **No browser journey plays a Two-Headed Giant game with four people against the real engine**; the tables, queue and team channel are checked through the API against the fake engine.
- **A bot cannot take control of a turn** (XMage refuses at the pin): keep the refusal, give bots a fixed rule, or patch XMage so an AI acts for the controlled player. Word of Command and Opposition Agent have no test of their own.

Decisions taken while building v0.5.1 that the owner may revisit ([the release notes](../releases/0.5.1.md#decisions-taken-while-building)): the tables' team seating and bots, the queue's pairing and invitations, results counting only the other team, the teammate's hand as a fan, the audit's step-trigger rule and 810.9d choice, the chat bar's behaviour at the table, and the one-of-a-kind and print-run rules.

## Decisions at the start of v0.6.0

The owner chose the scope on 2026-10-23: **Tivit's Auction House**, a market for cards between players that works like Madden Ultimate Team's transfer market, and **the official Magic card back**. Nothing else from the list above. The owner's rules:
- **Cards only.** Unopened packs cannot be sold, and an alliance's cards never can.
- **The market drives itself.** The seller sets the auction's length, its starting bid and the rest.
- **No fee.** A sale pays the seller 100% of what the buyer pays.
- **Editions are separate items,** which is why the library keeps them apart: an auction sells one card in one edition, and the editions are not worth the same.
- **The card back** is the official one (`magic.card.back.webp`, the owner's), wherever a card shows its back: tearing packs, at the game table, and everywhere else.
- **The page and the tile** (added by the owner the same day): `auction.house.background.png`, the owner's painting of Tivit at the auctioneer's desk, is the main menu tile's painting and the auction page's banner. "Try to make it look just like Madden Ultimate Team (obviously, keeping to the current theme of the app). I want all of the functionality of navigating the auction house."

Answers to the start questions (two rounds, every recommended option):
- **How a listing works:** Madden's way. A starting bid and an optional Buy Now price; lengths of 1 hour, 3 hours, 12 hours, 24 hours or 3 days; a bid is held from the bidder's balance and returned when someone outbids it; a bid in the last 30 seconds adds 30 seconds.
- **Finishes are separate too:** libraries record each edition's copies by finish (nonfoil, foil, etched), filled in for past packs from what their openings recorded; listings and searches name the finish. Decks do not care about the finish.
- **Any owned copy can be listed:** from packs, starter decks or the grandfathered decks; never an alliance's. Listing takes the copy out of the library at once, so a deck that used it is collection-locked until it is fixed; an unsold copy comes back.
- **Watched, not limited:** no limit on prices. SysOp has a log of every sale (buyer, seller, price) and can cancel an auction that is still running; every sale is in both players' token ledgers.
- **Withdrawing** is possible only until the first bid.
- **Where:** a main-menu tile of its own and its own page; "Sell" also on My library's cards.
- **The tools:** search filters (card name, set and edition, rarity, finish, colour, type, price, Buy Now, time left). With the owner's "all of the functionality of navigating the auction house", Madden's other screens come too: the transfer targets (auctions watched and bid on: active, won, lost), the transfer list (my listings: active, sold, unsold, to relist) and comparing prices with the same card's other listings.

## Added during v0.6.0

- **The main menu's greeting opens the profile** (the owner's request): the whole "Welcome back" tile is a link to the profile.
- **Lands are cards like any other** (the owner, 2026-10-23): "Lands are only acquired by getting decks or packs, and they can be auctioned. Make sure to keep to the exact print." Basic lands are owned by exact print and finish, and auctioned like any card. Asked how decks treat them, the owner chose **decks must own every basic**, print by print, like every other card; a deck short of basics is collection-locked until fixed.
- **A foil glow** (the owner): every card picture whose copy is foil or etched glows, throughout the app. Asked about the game table, the owner chose **outside games only**: decks still ignore the finish.
