# Plan for v0.9.2

Opened at the close of v0.9.1 on 2026-11-07 ([release notes](../releases/0.9.1.md)). Its scope is the owner's to give at the start, in a new chat: one round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults, then the work.

## The campaign's difficulty (v0.9.1's measurement)

[The first run](../campaign.md#difficulty-measured-v091) found the curve backwards: the wardens from ward 7 on (skill 4 to 6) won none of 24 games against the loaner at skill 3, and the early wardens won 4 of 18. Skill alone isn't the lever. Questions for the owner:

- **Targets.** The handoff's difficulty contract holds the win rates it wants by tier; the repository doesn't have them.
- **The lever.** Rework the later wardens' decks toward plans XMage's AI plays well (creatures, removal, fewer combo and five-colour pieces), or give the later bots a stronger opening (the handoff asks for no life or mana bonuses).
- **A wider run:** the starter decks as references, more games, and the reference at skill 2 and 4.
- **The finale:** its stand-in ran out of the engine's 2 GB heap once, and one game ended on turn 2 with both players at 40.
