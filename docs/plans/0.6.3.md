# Plan for v0.6.3

Named by the owner on 2026-10-24, during v0.6.2: "The next version will be v0.6.3. We'll decide on what the version will bring later." A patch version. Its scope is the owner's to choose at the start, in a new chat; this lists what v0.6.2 left and what it found.

## Carried over

- **"Add to deck" from My library** (the owner, 2026-10-24): each library card offers "Add to deck", with a pop-up to choose the deck. Left out of v0.6.2 at the owner's choice.
- **A browser journey that plays Two-Headed Giant with four people** against the real engine.
- **Weathered Sentinels** ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md)): XMage's effect stops all of its controller's creatures from attacking, in every format, not only the Sentinels. Fixing it changes Legacy and Commander too.

## From v0.6.2

Left open or decided along the way, for the owner to keep or revisit:
- **Stand-ins** ([catalog](../catalog.md#stand-ins-v062)): guided deck creation still proposes only cards the engine plays, and no search term finds stand-ins (for example `is:stand-in`). A card the staff suspended is still kept out of packs and libraries.
- **Old openings** (before v0.4.5) now show every card they pulled, tokens included, as the history no longer filters them.
- **My library's filters** start folded behind the Filters button and reset when the tab is left; the owner may prefer them open or kept.
- **The alliance tile** closes as soon as "Begin Alliance" is clicked; a refusal shows behind it.
- **Boosted chase cards**: the Power Nine and the dual lands now fill about 82% of Alpha's rare slots (the owner chose exactly five times each card's chance). Lowering it, or boosting other sets, is the owner's call.
- **Two-Headed Giant choices made in v0.6.2**: Invasion Plans and Contested Game Ball let the ability's controller choose either player of the attacking team (rule 805.10c as written); Tahngarth, First Mate may trigger twice when both opponents attack.

## Bug fixing

The owner's and the testers' reports (Administration → Reports) lead, then anything the owner names at the start.

## Start questions

At the start of v0.6.3, before any work: what the version is for, which carried-over items to take, and the owner's list of bugs. One round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults.
