# Plan for v0.6.4

Named by the owner on 2026-10-25, at the start of v0.6.3: "When that change is made, close the version, commit, and push to github. The next version will be v0.6.4." A patch version. Its scope is the owner's to choose at the start, in a new chat; this lists what v0.6.3 left.

## Carried over

v0.6.3 brought only the auction house's new lengths, so everything [its plan](0.6.3.md) listed is still open:
- **"Add to deck" from My library** (the owner, 2026-10-24): each library card offers "Add to deck", with a pop-up to choose the deck.
- **A browser journey that plays Two-Headed Giant with four people** against the real engine.
- **Weathered Sentinels** ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md)): XMage's effect stops all of its controller's creatures from attacking, in every format, not only the Sentinels. Fixing it changes Legacy and Commander too.
- **From v0.6.2**, for the owner to keep or revisit: guided deck creation proposes only cards the engine plays and no search term finds stand-ins; old openings show every card they pulled; My library's filters start folded and reset; the alliance tile closes as soon as "Begin Alliance" is clicked; the boosted chase cards fill about 82% of Alpha's rare slots; Two-Headed Giant's choices for Invasion Plans, Contested Game Ball and Tahngarth, First Mate.

## From v0.6.3

- **Unlimited auctions** ([the auction house](../auctions.md#a-listing)) are Buy Now only and never end. A listing nobody buys stays on the market, and its copy out of the seller's library, until the seller withdraws it. The owner may want a limit on how many a player keeps up, or a way to lower the price without withdrawing.
- **Not run locally in v0.6.3:** the browser journeys (hosted CI runs them). The new lengths have unit tests (pytest and Vitest), not a journey of their own.

## Bug fixing

The owner's and the testers' reports (Administration → Reports) lead, then anything the owner names at the start.

## Start questions

At the start of v0.6.4, before any work: what the version is for, which carried-over items to take, and the owner's list of bugs. One round of multiple-choice questions with recommended defaults.

## Scope chosen at the start of v0.6.4

The owner, 2026-10-25, gave the whole scope with the request, so no start questions were asked: basic land packs in Leng's shop (the Basic Land Pack, 200 lands for 200 tokens, and a pack of each basic land, 40 for 50 tokens), editions weighted from the newest down to Alpha, 1 to 5 foils per color, part of the weekly sale. Nothing carried over was taken: everything above moves to [the v0.6.5 plan](0.6.5.md). What was built: [the release notes](../releases/0.6.4.md).
