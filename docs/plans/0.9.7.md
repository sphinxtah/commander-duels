# Plan for v0.9.7

Closed on 2026-11-10: what was done is in [the release notes](../releases/0.9.7.md), what is left in [v0.9.8's plan](0.9.8.md). The owner's scope: the quest report below, a bug hunt, and catching up from this list; and, asked for during the work, the court's house rule ("Kings can attack anyone, except other kings, unless a general is defeated") and a turn limit of 40 turns for each player throughout the game.

Opened at v0.9.6's close on 2026-11-10. The scope is the owner's to choose at its start, with the usual round of questions; these are the candidates.

The quests should complete in any order, and be called "quests" or "achievements" instead of "Steps". - **Stand-ins**: the cards needing an engine piece of their own (Ertai's Meddling, Shaman's Trance, Magnetic Web, Ghostly Flame, Secret of Bloodbending, Illusionary Mask), the text changers (Sleight of Mind's kind), Spy Kit and Cryptic Spires; XMage's next release.

- **A duel's restoration after an engine restart**, voice-over.
- **The lobby's held seat for an invited friend**, the music and the scroll lock in play.
