# Plan for v0.9.6

Opened during v0.9.4 on 2026-11-10, ahead of time, for the owner's request below. v0.9.5 comes first ([its plan](0.9.5.md)); this version's scope is the owner's to confirm at its start, with the usual round of questions.

## The owner's request: a fifteenth ward, a King's Table finale (2026-11-10)

The owner's words, condensed:
- **Ward 14 changes.** The Sphinx no longer loses the duel. At the moment he would lose, he recalls Liliana and Nicol Bolas and flees, keeping his deck. The player is pulled into ward 15.
- **Ward 15 is a King's Table game** ([King's Table](../kings-table.md)):
  - Team B: The Sphinx is King B, with Liliana and Nicol Bolas as Generals B. All three are bots at the highest difficulty.
  - The Sphinx gifts Liliana and Nicol Bolas new decks, made to be cohesive with his own.
  - Team A: the player must be King A. Their Generals are their choice: two friends invited to join, two bots, or one friend and one bot.
- **The spoils.** Winning ward 15 gives the player all three decks: The Sphinx's, and Liliana's and Nicol Bolas's new ones.
- **Friends who help** get 10,000 tokens each.

## The owner's decisions (2026-11-10)

- **Ward 14 and ward 15 are one run.**
  - Losing ward 15 sends the player back to the start of ward 14.
  - Both wards pay only after ward 15 is beaten.
- **The payouts.**
  - Every ward's payout stays as it is: ward 14 keeps its 8,192,000 tokens and its seal, paid with ward 15's.
  - Ward 15 pays **double ward 14's tokens, 16,384,000**, and a seal of its own, "really amazing and unique, mythic".
- **The helpers.** 10,000 tokens **once per friend** (per player and friend, ever), and **only with a win**.
- **The house rules.** Kings cannot attack anyone (`kingAttacksAnyone` off). The rest are the defaults, with everyone at 40 life, in the Commander format.
- **The Sphinx keeps the High Priest:** he flees ward 14 with his deck.
- **Ward 15's match gets a new name** of its own.

**Clarified by the owner (2026-11-10, later):**
- **Apex Unbound** (v0.9.3's post-campaign challenge) becomes wards 14 and 15 as one dual level, run as the campaign's: losing ward 15 starts again at ward 14.
- **The incarnation in ward 15.** The Sphinx keeps it. He starts the King's Table match with it already on the battlefield, as if it were a free card.
  - When the planeswalker is killed, he goes back to his avatar image, and it comes back during the upkeep of his next turn.
  - Today's reserve rule in ward 14 is to cross at 15 life, cast for free once, then recast for its mana cost.
  - To confirm: whether the coming back is free and without limit, and whether it is cast (and can be countered) or simply put onto the battlefield.
  - To confirm: "starts the King's Table match with 40 life". Read as The Sphinx himself at 40 life, like every seat, with the incarnation at its printed loyalty. If the owner meant the incarnation with 40 loyalty, that changes the card in this ward only.
- **Players who beat ward 14 before v0.9.6** play ward 15 alone, as the one exception. Only SysOp has.
- Ward 15's match name: the owner's to give, or Claude's to propose.

- **Ward 14's end.**
  - **The trigger.** What "would lose" means: the moment the Sphinx's life reaches 0 or a lethal hit resolves, as the engine's result says, or a threshold before it.

  - **Ward 14's own rewards.** Does winning ward 14 still pay its 8,192,000 tokens and its seal? Or does that move to ward 15, with ward 14's "win" recorded as reaching the flight?
  - **Players who already beat ward 14.** Their deck and the High Priest stay theirs. Proposed: ward 15 opens for them directly.
- **Ward 15's rewards.**
  - Its tokens: proposed 16,384,000, continuing the doubling, or something else.
  - The seal and the chapter: a new chapter, or Godlike's?
  - The cosmetics: the Concordance Breaker title and Fractured Throne card back move from ward 14, or ward 15 gets new ones.
  - Whether its three decks come through v0.9.5's deck opening, as three openings.
- **The helpers.**
  - Paid 10,000 tokens once per friend: per ward-15 win, or once ever per friend and player?

  - Whether the Sphinx keeps the High Priest and the incarnation in ward 15. Proposed yes: the incarnation at 15 life, as today.

- **Apex Unbound** (v0.9.3's post-campaign challenge): does it become ward 15 again, or stay the duel?
- **The engine.**
  - A King's Table game where every seat of team B is a campaign bot at skill 6, and the reserve and incarnation rules on King B.
  - Campaign barks for three speakers at one table.
