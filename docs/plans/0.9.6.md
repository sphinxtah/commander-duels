# Plan for v0.9.6

Opened during v0.9.4 on 2026-11-10, ahead of time, for the owner's request below. v0.9.5 comes first ([its plan](0.9.5.md)); this version's scope is the owner's to confirm at its start, with the usual round of questions.

## The owner's request: a fifteenth ward, a King's Table finale (2026-11-10)

The owner's words, condensed:
- **Ward 14 changes.** The Sphinx no longer loses the duel. At the moment he would lose, he recalls Liliana and Nicol Bolas and flees, keeping his deck. The player is pulled into ward 15.
- **Ward 15 is a King's Table game** ([King's Table](../kings-table.md)):
  - Team B: The Sphinx is King B, with Liliana and Nicol Bolas as Generals B. All three are bots at the highest difficulty.
  - The Sphinx gifts Liliana and Nicol Bolas new decks, made to be cohesive with his own.
  - Team A: the player must be King A. Their Generals are their choice: two friends invited to join, two bots, or one friend and one bot.
- **The spoils.** Winning ward 15 gives the player all three decks: The Sphinx's, and Liliana's and Nicol Bolas's new ones.
- **Friends who help** get 10,000 tokens each.

## The owner's decisions (2026-11-10)

- **Ward 14 and ward 15 are one run.**
  - Losing ward 15 sends the player back to the start of ward 14.
  - Both wards pay only after ward 15 is beaten.
- **The payouts.**
  - Every ward's payout stays as it is: ward 14 keeps its 8,192,000 tokens and its seal, paid with ward 15's.
  - Ward 15 pays **double ward 14's tokens, 16,384,000**, and a seal of its own, "really amazing and unique, mythic".
- **The helpers.** 10,000 tokens **once per friend** (per player and friend, ever), and **only with a win**.
- **The house rules.** Kings cannot attack anyone (`kingAttacksAnyone` off). The rest are the defaults, with everyone at 40 life, in the Commander format.
- **The Sphinx keeps the High Priest:** he flees ward 14 with his deck.
- **Ward 15's match gets a new name** of its own.

Read as follows, to confirm at the start:
- Apex Unbound stays the post-campaign challenge it is.
- The incarnation stays with The Sphinx, at 15 life as in ward 14.
- Ward 15's name is the owner's to give, or Claude's to propose.
- Still open: whether players who beat ward 14 before v0.9.6 play ward 15 alone, or the 14–15 run again.
