# Plan for v0.4.3: pack artwork, and more fixes and features

Prepared on 2026-10-21, when v0.4.2 closed. The owner named v0.4.3: more bug fixing and other feature changes, starting with artwork for Leng's shop packs ("You'll need to source each pack design and implement in"). It starts in a new chat. This plan gathers what is known so far; it proposes, and the owner decides at the start.

## Start with: artwork for every pack in Leng's shop

Today every product on Leng's shelves is a text card (name, set code, date, booster type, price), and a pack opening shows a generic pack. The shop lists 392 products in 182 sets (`content/pack-prices.json`), and the weekly booster data job adds new sets at 500 tokens ([the shop](../shop.md#products)). Each needs a picture of its pack, on the shelf, in "Your packs" and at Leng's counter when it opens.

**Where the designs could come from**, for the start questions:
1. **The real pack designs.** Photos or renders of each sealed pack, as Wizards of the Coast published them (product pages, press kits) or as retailers and price sites show them (TCGplayer, which `content/pack-prices.json` already names per product, and its product images). Most faithful to Magic Duels; but each image belongs to Wizards or the photographer, and a retailer's photos are the retailer's: their terms decide whether they may be copied onto this site. Wizards' Fan Content Policy covers the game's own art and imagery for free fan content, not a retailer's photography. The sourcing would record each image's origin and licence in `docs/asset-provenance.csv`, and the site would serve its own copies, never hotlink.
2. **Designs made here, one per set and booster type.** A pack shape in the set's look: its name, its expansion symbol (Scryfall's set icon SVGs, which the catalog's sets already name), its era's frame (the shop's eras: the first years to Play Boosters), the booster type's band (Play, Collector, Set, Draft, Jumpstart and so on) and the set's key colours, made as SVG or WebP by a script. All 392, and every new set the job adds, would get one automatically, with nothing to license beyond what the site already uses.
3. **A mix:** the real designs where their licence allows, generated ones for the rest and for new sets.

**Start questions:**
1. Which source: real designs, generated, or the mix?
2. If real: may images from retailers' or price sites' product pages be used, or only Wizards' own?
3. Where they show: the shelf, "Your packs", the opening at Leng's counter (the pack that tears open), the opening history.
4. The starter decks' tab: their commanders' cards stay their pictures, or a deck box design too?
5. Sizes: one image per product in two sizes (about 160 and 480 px tall, WebP), loaded on demand and outside the installed app's precache, like the portraits.

## Also known

1. **The testers' reports**, in Administration → Reports, once the beta testers have played. Each is reproduced first (a test or a journey that fails), then fixed, and its report set to fixed or closed. None had come in when v0.4.2 closed.
2. **The starter decks' prices.** Each deck holds one Revised dual land, which is most of its market value, so the shop prices them at 35,000 to 94,000 tokens (on 2026-10-21; [the shop](../shop.md#starter-decks-v042)). Leaving the dual lands out of the price would make them about 2,500 to 3,800: the owner's call.
3. **What v0.4.2 could not check by hand,** on the live site after its deploy:
   - the engine's start line saying "at most 64 games at once", and `deploy.sh`'s line raising `CD_GAMES_MAX`;
   - the setup wizard for a new account from approval to its first game, on a phone too;
   - the Cards page's alliance tab for SysOp allied with The Sphinx and with another Planeswalker;
   - a starter deck bought in the shop, and the two free ones chosen without spending a token;
   - the welcome screenshots on the live site.
4. **Still open from v0.4.1 and v0.4.0** ([the v0.4.2 plan](0.4.2.md#start-with)): the Google side and a stranger's full path from request to first game; a screenshot over 750 KB once the TSH proxy's block for `/api/v1/reports` is in; "Delete account" on a real account and the first daily removal of requests never approved; many players at once; the custom cards in a real game seen from both sides; a human table between two real players with an away vote; a pack bought and opened against the live catalog; a real win's tokens and the back pay.
5. **Choices the builds made without asking,** for the owner to keep or change: the earlier ones in [the v0.4.2 plan](0.4.2.md#start-with) (item 5), and from v0.4.2: the wizard opens by itself once per visit rather than on every page; its welcome comes first even for an account that has already chosen its handle; a bought starter deck counts as a shop purchase in the token ledger ("Starter deck: <title>"); a deck's price is fixed in a content file, read again by a script rather than daily.
6. **Housekeeping.** The old git worktrees under `.claude/worktrees/` on the development PC, those of v0.4.1 included, still make `git gc` report "Permission denied". All their branches are merged.
