# Plan for v0.4.3: pack artwork, and more fixes and features

Prepared on 2026-10-21, when v0.4.2 closed. The owner named v0.4.3: more bug fixing and other feature changes, starting with artwork for Leng's shop packs ("You'll need to source each pack design and implement in"). Added the same day: a player search in the app's top bar, by username or email; friends, with who is online and in a game; and a notification bell. Chat comes in v0.4.4. It starts in a new chat. This plan gathers what is known so far; it proposes, and the owner decides at the start.

## Start with: artwork for every pack in Leng's shop

Today every product on Leng's shelves is a text card (name, set code, date, booster type, price), and a pack opening shows a generic pack. The shop lists 392 products in 182 sets (`content/pack-prices.json`), and the weekly booster data job adds new sets at 500 tokens ([the shop](../shop.md#products)). Each needs a picture of its pack, on the shelf, in "Your packs" and at Leng's counter when it opens.

**Where the designs could come from**, for the start questions:
1. **The real pack designs.** Photos or renders of each sealed pack, as Wizards of the Coast published them (product pages, press kits) or as retailers and price sites show them (TCGplayer, which `content/pack-prices.json` already names per product, and its product images). Most faithful to Magic Duels; but each image belongs to Wizards or the photographer, and a retailer's photos are the retailer's: their terms decide whether they may be copied onto this site. Wizards' Fan Content Policy covers the game's own art and imagery for free fan content, not a retailer's photography. The sourcing would record each image's origin and licence in `docs/asset-provenance.csv`, and the site would serve its own copies, never hotlink.
2. **Designs made here, one per set and booster type.** A pack shape in the set's look: its name, its expansion symbol (Scryfall's set icon SVGs, which the catalog's sets already name), its era's frame (the shop's eras: the first years to Play Boosters), the booster type's band (Play, Collector, Set, Draft, Jumpstart and so on) and the set's key colours, made as SVG or WebP by a script. All 392, and every new set the job adds, would get one automatically, with nothing to license beyond what the site already uses.
3. **A mix:** the real designs where their licence allows, generated ones for the rest and for new sets.

**Start questions:**
1. Which source: real designs, generated, or the mix?
2. If real: may images from retailers' or price sites' product pages be used, or only Wizards' own?
3. Where they show: the shelf, "Your packs", the opening at Leng's counter (the pack that tears open), the opening history.
4. The starter decks' tab: their commanders' cards stay their pictures, or a deck box design too?
5. Sizes: one image per product in two sizes (about 160 and 480 px tall, WebP), loaded on demand and outside the installed app's precache, like the portraits.

## Also in v0.4.3: finding other players (the owner, 2026-10-21)

"Search for finding other players. There should be a player search bar at the top center of the app. It should search by username or email address."

- **Where:** a search field in the middle of the top bar, on every page of the app (the top bar today holds the motto, the connection status, the update notice, "Report a problem" and the version).
- **What it finds:** active players by handle (the username) or by email address. Each result shows the handle, the alliance portrait and the tier; what opening a result leads to is a start question (a profile card, an invite to a table, and later the in-game messaging the setup wizard's handle step mentions).
- **Privacy, for the start questions.** Today an email address is seen only by its owner, administrators and SysOp ("only you can see this" on Profile), and the privacy policy says so. Searching by email can keep that true if an email matches only when typed in full, case aside, and results never show anyone's email; a partial email would let anyone guess addresses letter by letter. Whether players can opt out of being found by email, how many searches an account may make (as reports are limited to 20 a day), and the privacy policy's new words are the owner's choices. Pending, denied and deleted accounts are never found.
- **Start questions:** exact or partial email; what a result opens; an opt-out; who may search (every player, or administrators only for email).

## Also in v0.4.3: friends, who is online, and notifications (the owner, 2026-10-21)

"I also want you to be able to friend other players, and when they're online you'll get a notification. There should be a bell icon in the upper right corner of the app for all notifications. When visiting another player's profile, Add friend button appears. Then, you'll be able to see when each other is online, game status (eg in a game, idle, etc)."

- **Player profiles.** A player found by the search (above), or met at a table, has a profile page of their own: handle, portrait, tier and wins, alliance, and an **Add friend** button. Today only an account's own Profile exists.
- **Friends.** Adding a friend, and what the other player sees: a start question whether a friend request waits for the other player's yes (mutual friends, the usual choice, so no one is followed unasked) or is one-sided. Removing a friend, and blocking a player who keeps asking, belong with it.
- **Presence and game status.** Friends see each other's state: online, in a game (practice or at a table, perhaps which format), idle, offline. The API already knows a session's last activity and every running game's seats; presence would come from those, the app's open WebSocket or its once-a-minute refresh, never from anything the browser claims about others. Only friends see it (a start question), and the privacy policy says so.
- **Notifications and the bell.** A bell in the top bar's right-hand corner, with a count of unread notifications and a list: a friend came online, a friend request, a request accepted, and later table invites and messages. v0.4.1 chose counts inside the app only, no email or push; whether the browser's own notifications (the installed app's, with the player's permission) join the bell is a start question. Administrators' counts of waiting accounts and unread reports could move into the bell too.
- **Start questions:** mutual or one-sided friends; who sees presence; which states (and whether a player can appear offline); which notifications, how long they are kept, and browser notifications or the bell alone.

**Decided by the owner on 2026-10-21, before v0.4.3 started:**
1. **Friends are mutual.** The player who clicks Add friend sees "Pending" until the other either clicks Add friend on the first player's profile or accepts the request from the notifications.
2. **Notifications:** the bell pops out a bubble with the latest notifications and, at its bottom, a link to see them all: a page with the full history. (Browser notifications were not asked for.)
3. **Presence is for friends only by default;** a player may make it public in privacy settings, which come in this version too.
4. **A player may appear offline** to everyone except administrators and SysOp, who still see the real state, passively and silently.

## Also known

1. **The testers' reports**, in Administration → Reports, once the beta testers have played. Each is reproduced first (a test or a journey that fails), then fixed, and its report set to fixed or closed. None had come in when v0.4.2 closed.
2. **The starter decks' prices.** Each deck holds one Revised dual land, which is most of its market value, so the shop prices them at 35,000 to 94,000 tokens (on 2026-10-21; [the shop](../shop.md#starter-decks-v042)). Leaving the dual lands out of the price would make them about 2,500 to 3,800: the owner's call.
3. **What v0.4.2 could not check by hand,** on the live site after its deploy:
   - the engine's start line saying "at most 64 games at once", and `deploy.sh`'s line raising `CD_GAMES_MAX`;
   - the setup wizard for a new account from approval to its first game, on a phone too;
   - the Cards page's alliance tab for SysOp allied with The Sphinx and with another Planeswalker;
   - a starter deck bought in the shop, and the two free ones chosen without spending a token;
   - the welcome screenshots on the live site.
4. **Still open from v0.4.1 and v0.4.0** ([the v0.4.2 plan](0.4.2.md#start-with)): the Google side and a stranger's full path from request to first game; a screenshot over 750 KB once the TSH proxy's block for `/api/v1/reports` is in; "Delete account" on a real account and the first daily removal of requests never approved; many players at once; the custom cards in a real game seen from both sides; a human table between two real players with an away vote; a pack bought and opened against the live catalog; a real win's tokens and the back pay.
5. **Choices the builds made without asking,** for the owner to keep or change: the earlier ones in [the v0.4.2 plan](0.4.2.md#start-with) (item 5), and from v0.4.2: the wizard opens by itself once per visit rather than on every page; its welcome comes first even for an account that has already chosen its handle; a bought starter deck counts as a shop purchase in the token ledger ("Starter deck: <title>"); a deck's price is fixed in a content file, read again by a script rather than daily.
6. **Housekeeping.** The old git worktrees under `.claude/worktrees/` on the development PC, those of v0.4.1 included, still make `git gc` report "Permission denied". All their branches are merged.
