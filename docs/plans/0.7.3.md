# Plan for v0.7.3

Opened at the close of v0.7.2 on 2026-10-31 ([release notes](../releases/0.7.2.md)). Its scope is the owner's to give at the start, in a new chat; below is what v0.7.2 noted and left, and the rest carried over.

## Noted during v0.7.2

- **The payment prompt does not say why a cost is higher.** The owner cast Terminate on Skycat Sovereign with Bot 2's Jubilant Skybonder on the battlefield ("Creatures you control with flying have 'This creature can't be the target of spells or abilities your opponents control unless they pay {2}.'"), paid {B}{R}, and was asked for {2} more; Cast Down on Tide Skimmer earlier in the game was the same. The game was right, but the prompt read "Pay {2}" with nothing naming the Skybonder, and the game log records no payments. Worth adding: the `pay_mana` prompt's detail naming the cost as XMage adjusted it and the effects that changed it (XMage's cost-modification effects that apply to the ability, `game.getContinuousEffects()`), such as "Terminate costs {2}{B}{R} here: {2} more from Jubilant Skybonder." An engine change (`Prompts.java`), so CI's engine job is its first build.
- **The menu playlist** (the owner's twenty songs, [the v0.7.2 plan](0.7.2.md)): the files are not in the project folder, and the rights to commercial hits streamed from the live site are the owner's to settle first. When they are: the private repository holds the files (Git LFS or a release's assets rather than plain git, for ten times the gameplay track), the private web image serves them under `/music/`, Settings gets the tick boxes and a menu-music volume, and the first-visit sound pop-up from the owner's notes starts the music on the click the browser needs.
- **The music in play**, after more games: the default level (50, a quarter of full), the fades (2.5 s in, 1.5 s out), and the hand-off at a match's next game (the track begins again; the owner may prefer it to go on).
- **The scroll lock in play**: every pop-up on every page, with the window scrolled, in Chrome and Firefox; the chat bar and the toasts (`position: fixed`) sit 17 px further right while a pop-up is open on Windows, behind the backdrop.
- **The owner's PC**: `.local/demo-0.7.2.cmd` is superseded by `.local/start-demo.cmd` (`git -c gc.auto=0`); the stale `.git/worktrees/*` records that git's auto-maintenance cannot delete there (OneDrive holds a `logs` folder) want `git worktree prune` after a reboot.

## What v0.7.2 left (read first)

- **The private web image** must exist at a version's tag, like the private engine image, before `deploy.sh` can pull it: the private repository's `web.yml`, run after the tag ([the live site](../deployment.md#the-web-image-with-the-music-v072)). The VM's `.env` needs `CD_WEB_IMAGE` once.
- **The music's first deploy**: hear it on the live site; check that a muted player streams nothing (the browser's network panel) and that the track survives a reconnect.
- Everything v0.7.0 and v0.7.1 left that v0.7.2 did not take: the effects' restraint in play, the slain card's hold, a big spell's burst at resolution, the bots' habits at each skill, the folded auction sections' memory ([the v0.7.2 plan](0.7.2.md#what-v070-and-v071-left-read-first)).

## Carried over

v0.6.5 only returned the Power Nine and dual lands to their real pull ratios, v0.7.0 took only the special effects, v0.7.1 the bots and the auction sections, and v0.7.2 the music and the scroll lock, so the rest of [the v0.6.5 plan](0.6.5.md) is still open:
- **"Add to deck" from My library** (the owner, 2026-10-24): each library card offers "Add to deck", with a pop-up to choose the deck.
- **A browser journey that plays Two-Headed Giant with four people** against the real engine.
- **Weathered Sentinels** ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md)): XMage's effect stops all of its controller's creatures from attacking, in every format, not only the Sentinels. Fixing it changes Legacy and Commander too.
- **From v0.6.2**, for the owner to keep or revisit: guided deck creation proposes only cards the engine plays and no search term finds stand-ins; old openings show every card they pulled; My library's filters start folded and reset; the alliance tile closes as soon as "Begin Alliance" is clicked; Two-Headed Giant's choices for Invasion Plans, Contested Game Ball and Tahngarth, First Mate.
- **From v0.6.3:** unlimited auctions are Buy Now only and never end; the owner may want a limit on how many a player keeps up, or a way to lower the price without withdrawing.
- **From v0.6.4:** the basic land packs' editions and scale, and a view made for big packs (counts by land and edition).
- **Not run locally:** the browser journeys (hosted CI runs them), and on native Windows the two test files that hang there (`tests/test_matches.py`, `tests/test_tables.py`).
