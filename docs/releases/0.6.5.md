# v0.6.5: the Power Nine and dual lands at their real pull ratios

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-25 (tag `v0.6.5`). The owner reopened it the same day for one more fix, the missing card flips and foil glow, and it was closed again with the tag moved to the new close commit.

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-10-25: "We need to make the power 9 back to normal ratios again, instead of the 5x ratios. Fix that so they don't get over-pulled." Asked, the owner chose to return the ten dual lands boosted with them too ([the v0.6.5 plan](../plans/0.6.5.md)).

## Changed

- **No card is boosted.** Since v0.6.2, Alpha's and Beta's Power Nine (Black Lotus, the five Moxen, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, Timetwister) and ten dual lands came exactly five times as often as their sheet said, together about 82% of an Alpha rare slot. Now they, and every card, come as often as MTGJSON's booster sheet says, in boosters and starter decks alike: Black Lotus in about 0.9% of Alpha packs again, instead of about 4.3%. Print runs still hold. Packs opened before keep what they gave.
- In the code, `api/shop.py`'s `BOOSTED` and `api/collation.py`'s `boost_sheet` and `boosted` are gone, with `tests/test_boosts.py`. Details: [the shop](../shop.md).

## Fixed (reopened)

- **The pack's card flips and the foils' glow are back in every browser.** The owner, 2026-10-25: "The foil cards no longer have the glowing animation over them. Fix that for all browsers. My Chrome version of the game doesn't have the nice card flip animations for opening packs either." The cause was the system's reduced-motion setting: on the owner's PC Windows' animation effects are off, so Chrome (and Firefox, which read the same setting) told the site to reduce motion, and the site then stopped every animation and transition: the dealing and turning of a pack's cards, the foils' shimmer and breathing glow, and the table's animation. Now only the game's own **Settings → Reduce motion** stills them, on every page and at the table; it is off unless the player turns it on, and it follows the account to every device. The CSS of the flip and the glow was unchanged and checked working in Chromium and Firefox.
- In the code: `web/src/global.css`, `web/src/game/table/screen.module.css` and `web/src/social/social.module.css` lose their `prefers-reduced-motion` rules (each had the same rule for the setting beside it), `web/src/app/motion.ts` no longer reads the system's setting, and Settings no longer says system preferences are respected.

## Fixed (reopened again)

- **A big library's pictures and its lag.** The owner, 2026-10-25, with a library of 14,827 cards: some card art no longer showed (the card's text frame in its place) and the page was very laggy, also after the VM grew to 32 vCPUs and 64 GB. The cause was the page, not the server: My library drew a tile for every edition at once, thousands of them with every foil's glow, and each tile asked for its picture as it came near; the server fetches new pictures from Scryfall 4 at a time per account with 128 waiting, answered the rest "busy" with a Retry-After, and the page gave up after five tries. Now My library draws 120 editions at a time and the next 120 as its end comes near (or with "Show more"), and a picture the server is busy with is asked again for as long as it is on the page. Other failures still give up after five tries, and a card Scryfall has no picture for keeps its text frame, as before.

## Deploying 0.6.5

No `.env` change and no migration. The engine did not change, but `deploy.sh` pulls the private engine image at the tag, so `commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.6.5` is built too. Once the tag's run has published the `0.6.5` images and the private engine image exists, on the VM: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`.
