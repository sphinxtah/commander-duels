# v0.6.5: the Power Nine and dual lands at their real pull ratios

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-25 (tag `v0.6.5`).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-10-25: "We need to make the power 9 back to normal ratios again, instead of the 5x ratios. Fix that so they don't get over-pulled." Asked, the owner chose to return the ten dual lands boosted with them too ([the v0.6.5 plan](../plans/0.6.5.md)).

## Changed

- **No card is boosted.** Since v0.6.2, Alpha's and Beta's Power Nine (Black Lotus, the five Moxen, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, Timetwister) and ten dual lands came exactly five times as often as their sheet said, together about 82% of an Alpha rare slot. Now they, and every card, come as often as MTGJSON's booster sheet says, in boosters and starter decks alike: Black Lotus in about 0.9% of Alpha packs again, instead of about 4.3%. Print runs still hold. Packs opened before keep what they gave.
- In the code, `api/shop.py`'s `BOOSTED` and `api/collation.py`'s `boost_sheet` and `boosted` are gone, with `tests/test_boosts.py`. Details: [the shop](../shop.md).

## Deploying 0.6.5

No `.env` change and no migration. The engine did not change, but `deploy.sh` pulls the private engine image at the tag, so `commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.6.5` is built too. Once the tag's run has published the `0.6.5` images and the private engine image exists, on the VM: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`.
