# v0.4.2: the first fixes, and a setup wizard for new players

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-21 (tag `v0.4.2`). It fixes what the owner found on the live site after v0.4.1, and adds what the owner asked for during it: a setup wizard for new players, the starter decks in Leng's card shop, and a roster of only Planeswalkers a game can play. This follows [the v0.4.2 plan](../plans/0.4.2.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-21 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.2.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v042)):
- The 64-game limit on the live site, which still ran 6.
- The owner's report: the Cards page did not follow the alliance, and showed The Sphinx's custom cards to SysOp allied with another Planeswalker.
- The terms line under the sign-in button, and SysOp's custom card upload preview, both left from v0.4.1.
- Added by the owner during the version: a setup wizard (welcome, handle, two free starter decks, alliance); the starter decks in Leng's shop, priced by their cards; no Planeswalker without a playable card.
- Kept as they are: ended sessions until the account is deleted, and the defaults earlier versions chose.

## Changed

- **Up to 64 games at once, live.** After 0.4.1's deploy the VM's `.env` still said `CD_GAMES_MAX=6`, the value every kit from 0.1.1 to 0.4.0 wrote, so the engine ran 6 games. `deploy.sh` now raises that 6 to 64 when it deploys over one of those versions, and says so; any other value is the owner's own and stays, as does a 6 set later ([deployment](../deployment.md#the-vm)).
- **The Cards page follows the alliance** (the owner's report). Its third tab is the allied Planeswalker's, named for them ("Teferi's cards"), for every account, SysOp's too: only that Planeswalker's cards, which change when the alliance does (`GET /api/v1/me/alliance/cards`). For The Sphinx it shows the Godlike tier's custom cards. The custom card list (`GET /api/v1/custom-cards`) now holds only the allied Planeswalker's, so a Godlike account allied with anyone else, SysOp included, sees none on the Cards page or in the deck builder's finder; SysOp keeps building from everything else, and its upload list in Administration stays whole. Before, the "Godlike cards" tab listed every custom card to any Godlike account, and SysOp, who builds from the whole catalog, saw its alliance nowhere ([progression](../progression.md#alliances)).
- **The setup wizard** (the owner's request). A new account sets up its game in one dialog: a big welcome with four screenshots of the game (the table, the deck builder, Leng's card shop and the Planeswalker Alliance), then its handle, the name other players see at the table, in the lobby and later in a game's chat, then its two free starter decks, each with its commander's card, check boxes and a call to action, then an alliance, which may wait for later, and a last step pointing to a practice game. It opens by itself each time the site opens on an account that has not chosen its handle and its starter decks (a new sign-in or a reload), until both are done. "Finish later" closes it; every page then starts with a line and a "Continue setup" button, and Profile's "Set up your game" panel opens it too. The steps are the server's, as on Profile before ([progression](../progression.md#the-starter-decks)). The screenshots are in `web/public/welcome/`, outside the installed app's precache.
- **The starter decks in Leng's card shop** (the owner's request). The shop has two tabs, Packs and Starter decks. The first two decks are free, chosen in the wizard, and leave the 1,000 welcome tokens for packs; until they are chosen the tab shows every deck as free and opens the wizard. After them, each of the others sells once (`POST /api/v1/shop/decks`): it becomes a deck of the player's own, and every copy of its cards joins the library for good, as choosing it would have. Its price comes from the market value of the cards it grants, basic lands aside, at the current pack's rate (500 tokens for $6.07), from `content/starter-deck-prices.json`, which `scripts/starter_deck_prices.py` reads from Scryfall. On 2026-10-21 that is 35,000 to 94,000 tokens, most of it each deck's Revised dual land ([the shop](../shop.md#starter-decks-v042)).
- **The commander's picture** on a starter deck falls back to the card's own default printing when the catalog lacks the printing the owner chose.
- **Only Planeswalkers a game can play** (the owner's request). The ten whose every card the engine cannot play left the roster, with their portraits: Abian, B.O.B., Deb Thomas, Dungeon Master, Ersta, Luxior, Mr. Monopoly, Svega, Sifa and Thomil. 89 remain, The Sphinx included: Bronze opens 32 and Silver 23 more. Migration `0014_roster_playable` ends any alliance with them ([progression](../progression.md#wins-and-levels)).
- **What signing in accepts.** Under the sign-in button: "By signing in you accept the terms of use and the privacy policy.", each a link ([identity](../identity.md#before-the-app-the-landing-page-v030)).
- **SysOp's custom card upload** previews the image as a `data:` URL, which the site's Content-Security-Policy allows, instead of a `blob:` one it refused.

## Deploying 0.4.2

In this order:
1. **The private engine image.** The engine did not change, but `deploy.sh` pulls the private image at the version's tag: the private repository's CI builds `ghcr.io/sphinxtah/commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.4.2` once the tag's run has published the `0.4.2` images, as for 0.4.1.
2. **Deploy:** `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Nothing to change in `.env` by hand: `deploy.sh` raises `CD_GAMES_MAX=6` to 64 on the way and says so. Migration 0014 only changes data, so the previous version keeps working while it runs. Afterwards the engine's start line says "at most 64 games at once".
3. **Still open from 0.4.1:** the TSH proxy's `location = /api/v1/reports` block, for screenshots over 750 KB ([deployment](../deployment.md#on-the-tsh-proxy), step 5).
