# v0.9.6: The Unbound Court, the campaign's fifteenth ward

Opened and closed on 2026-11-10. The next version is v0.9.7 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.7.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner planned v0.9.6 during v0.9.4 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.6.md)): The Sphinx no longer loses ward 14; at the moment he would lose, he recalls Liliana and Nicol Bolas and flees, and the player follows him into a fifteenth ward, a King's Table game against his court. The owner's decisions of 2026-11-10 stood (one run for wards 14 and 15, both paid after ward 15, 16,384,000 tokens and a mythic seal, 10,000 tokens once ever per helping friend and only on a win, Kings who can't attack, 40 life, Commander, The Sphinx keeping the High Priest and his incarnation, Apex Unbound as the same dual level, the players who beat ward 14 before playing ward 15 alone). At the start the owner answered four questions:
- **The incarnation:** free, unlimited, put onto the battlefield. It starts the court on the battlefield and comes back at the beginning of his next upkeep each time it's gone, at its printed loyalty; never cast, so never countered. The Sphinx himself is at 40 life like every seat.
- **Ward 14 on the map:** an animation of The Sphinx shaking furiously, then fleeing, leaving a "Fled" marker; no seal yet.
- **Ward 15's own rewards:** a new title and card back, besides its tokens and seal. Ward 14 keeps Concordance Breaker and Fractured Throne, paid with ward 15's win. Three deck openings.
- **The match's name:** *The Unbound Court*.

During the work the owner added one item: the edition codes' "i" beside the set-code field. Claude's defaults for the rest: "would lose" is the engine's result (any way of losing); a cut scene between the wards; friends join a private King's Table table the campaign opens (one of the player's own Commander decks), a General's seat nobody takes being a bot's; helpers' own campaigns untouched, and the game counting nothing else for anyone.

## Added

- **Ward 15, The Unbound Court** ([the campaign](../campaign.md#the-unbound-court-ward-15-v096)): a King's Table game with Commander decks, everyone at 40 life, Kings who can't attack. The player is King A; team B is The Sphinx as King with Nicol Bolas and Liliana as his Generals, each a named bot at skill 6 with their own deck.
  - **The gifted decks:** *Nicol Bolas's Gift* (Grixis power, led by Nicol Bolas, the Ravager) and *Liliana's Gift* (mono-black power, led by Liliana, Heretical Healer), built for XMage's AI around the power cards of The Sphinx's own, every card checked against the engine's database.
  - **The Sphinx keeps his deck**, the High Priest and his incarnation with the owner's engine, or the public stand-in without.
  - **Generals: bots or friends.** With bots the court starts at once. With friends, the campaign opens a private table (`POST /api/v1/campaign/encounters/15/court`), the player seated as King A, the court's seats fixed and named, one or two friends invited to the Generals' seats; the table starts as any other.
  - **The helpers:** each friend who played a won court to its end is paid 10,000 tokens, once ever per player and friend.
  - **Barks for three speakers** (The Sphinx, Nicol Bolas, Liliana, and Kaya), read from the King's seat.
- **Ward 14's flight:** its win is The Sphinx's flight (the scene FC.E14.FLIGHT), which opens the court and pays nothing yet. The map shows ward 14 as fled: The Sphinx shakes furiously, flees, and leaves the Fled marker.
- **One run.** A lost court sends the player back to ward 14. A won court pays both wards: ward 14's seal, The Sphinx's deck and the Throne's chapter, then ward 15's seal (Sovereignty), its two decks, 16,384,000 tokens and its chapter, *The Unbound Court*: the **Courtbreaker** title and the **Unbound Court** card back. The three decks are opened one after another, then the court's victory scene and the ending.
- **The court's incarnation in the engine** (`CampaignReserve.applyCourt`, a reserve with `"mode": "court"`): on the battlefield from the first moment the game checks its state, back at each of his upkeeps whenever it's in the reserve, never cast, and sent to the reserve instead of anywhere else. `CampaignCourtTest` shows it with a public card standing in (Ball Lightning, sacrificed at every end step).

- **Edition codes beside the set code** (the owner's request during the version): an "i" next to the set-code field of Tivit's Auction House's search and of the Cards page's search opens a small pop-up listing every edition's code, name and year, oldest to newest, with a box to find one; choosing a code fills the field. It reads `GET /api/v1/sets?every=true`, every edition of the catalog's deck cards (without `every`, the sets with a card a game can play, as before).

## Changed

- **Apex Unbound** is wards 14 and 15 as one run: the challenge opens once the court is won, its ward 14 win is a flight again, a lost court sends it back to ward 14, and the court won earns the badge. The campaign's state says where it stands (`challenge.at`).
- **The campaign's state:** ward 14's status may be `fled`; each encounter has `court` (null for a duel) and `spoils.decks`; the game summary's `campaign.seat` is the player's seat, and only the campaign's own player sees that reference.
- **The court's table** in the lobby's page reads "Campaign · King's Table" with the court's title; its host can't change its settings or the court's seats (`409 campaign_table`).
- **The engine** takes a reserve in any Commander deck format, King's Table's included.

## For players who already beat ward 14

Only SysOp has. The court opens for them at once, losing it never sends them back, and its win pays ward 15's own rewards.

Nothing changes there. The owner's finale content (`campaign-finale.json`: the High Priest, the incarnation and the threshold) serves the court as it serves the duel; the API asks the engine for the court's mode. The private images are rebuilt for the version as usual.
