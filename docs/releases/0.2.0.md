# v0.2.0: your own decks and priority controls

A minor version. It brings the scope's M1: the Scryfall catalog mapped to the engine, and a deck builder for Legacy and Commander whose decks you play at the practice table. It also brings the rest of the priority controls (UI07). The work follows [the v0.2.0 plan](../plans/0.2.0.md). Behaviour is described where it lives: [the card catalog](../catalog.md), [decks](../decks.md), [the game screen](../game-screen.md#priority-controls-ui07-v020), [the human seat](../engine/human-seat.md#priority-controls), [games through the API](../games.md) and [the live site](../deployment.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-20 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.2.0.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v020)): Track A with Track D; English printings; unsupported cards shown and labeled but blocked from play; the whole of M1's deck builder, DECK01–DECK11.

Defaults taken from the scope where the owner left them open: a daily sync with a configurable time and jitter and an administrator's "run now"; playable means certified (the engine's implementation passed the automatic gates) and legal in the format; decks are private or shared by link with signed-in players, and notes are never shared; bots play starter decks or the player's own playable decks (AI06); the six fixture decks become starter decks to copy; SysOp keeps any-card deck lists, and its own decks may hold any card the engine implements.

One addition to the agreed datasets: Scryfall Tagger's `oracle_tags` (6 MB), for guided creation's card roles and the `otag:` search.

## New

### The card catalog

- **Imported from Scryfall** every day: `oracle_cards`, English `default_cards`, `rulings` and `oracle_tags`, streamed and validated before anything is written. Each import is a revision; nothing is ever deleted, and a failed import leaves the previous catalog in use (T20).
- **Mapped to the engine.** The engine image lists every card XMage implements and runs five automatic gates on each. A card is playable (certified) when it passed them and its mana cost, types and power and toughness match the Oracle card. Every other card is shown, labeled with the reason, and blocked from play (T18).
- **On 2026-10-20:** 32,089 playable card concepts; of the deck cards legal in Legacy, 31,711 playable, 351 not implemented and 67 in a layout this version does not know yet.
- **Search** in Scryfall's syntax (`t:`, `o:`, `c:`, `id:`, `mv:`, `r:`, `s:`, `f:`, `is:`, `k:`, `otag:`, `year:`, `or`, `-`, parentheses), with filters, sorting and pages, on the new **Cards** page.
- **Reviews.** A playable card whose Oracle text changes stays playable and waits for an administrator to accept it or suspend it.
- **Administration → Card catalog:** the sync's state and runs, "Run the catalog sync now", the engine's coverage per format, and the review list with accept, suspend and restore.

### Decks

- **The Decks page** lists your decks with their state ("Ready to play", "Legal, not playable yet", "Draft"), and archives, trashes and restores them. Starter decks can be copied.
- **The deck builder:** a card finder limited by default to what the deck can use, sections per location, printing choice, statistics with opening-hand odds and sample hands, notes, versions and export. It saves by itself and resolves edits made in two tabs.
- **Validation** for Legacy and Commander, with a remedy for every problem: card legality and releases, copy limits and their exceptions, deck sizes, commanders and their pairs, color identity, and the ten companion conditions (T03–T05).
- **Versions.** Saving a version stores an immutable revision. A game records the revision it started with, so later edits never change it.
- **Import and export** as text or as a lossless deck file, with a preview that asks about names it cannot resolve.
- **Guided creation:** a starting deck from a format, a commander or colors, a theme and an experience level, each card with its reason. The rules are written down; no language model is involved.
- **Sharing** by link with signed-in players, who can copy the deck. Notes stay private.
- **At the practice table** your decks are listed above the starter decks, for your seat and the bots'. "Play it at the practice table" opens the table with the deck chosen.

### Priority controls (UI07)

- **Keep passing:** a priority prompt also offers to pass until the stack resolves, the next main phase, the end of the turn, the next turn, the end step before your turn, or your next turn. New stack objects and combat stop a pass, as the seat's stops say.
- **Stop passing**, in the new Priority panel or with Escape, cancels a pass in progress.
- **Full control**, **hold priority after casting**, and **stops** per step for your turns and the others', with stops for new stack objects, attackers and blockers.
- The engine applies them through XMage's own player settings, with a new `set_priority` command that needs no open prompt.

## Changed

- **SysOp's card-name lookup** for deck lists moved from `GET /api/v1/cards` to `GET /api/v1/engine/cards`; the catalog's search took the old path.
- **The navigation's Decks and Cards** pages are real pages now, not "not available" placeholders.
- **The Profile page's session list** names each other session's button "End the session" with that browser in its accessible name, apart from this browser's "Sign out".

## Fixed while building

- **"Play it" across formats.** Opening the practice table for a deck of the other format switched the format and then cleared the chosen deck. The deck now stays chosen.
- **Two changes at once in the deck editor.** Notes save when the field loses focus, so ticking "Share by link" sent two changes together; an older reply arriving last could undo the newer one on screen. Only the newest reply shows now.
- **Guided Commander decks with a pair of commanders** had 101 cards, and a small card pool left a proposal short. The lands now fill what the spells leave, so a proposal is always 100 or 60 cards.
- **The first sync on a new installation.** A deploy starts the worker with the engine, which takes about a minute to load. An import before the engine answered would have left every card unchecked until the next day. While the catalog has never met the engine, the sync now retries with backoff until it does.

## For the live site

- **No `.env` change.** The deploy creates the worker's `catalog_data` volume; the first sync starts with the worker, downloads about 115 MB and takes about two minutes, and the database grows by about 450 MB. Until it finishes, the Cards page says the catalog is not imported yet, and the deck builder finds no cards. Migrations `0005_catalog` and `0006_decks` only add tables and nullable columns.
- **After the deploy:** Administration → Card catalog shows a succeeded run, and a deck of your own plays at the practice table ([the live checks](../deployment.md#checking-the-live-site)).

## Known limits

- **Companions, sideboards and maybeboards stay out of games.** Games use the main deck and the commanders; best-of-three sideboarding is later work (FMT05).
- **English printings only**, and no collections or owned status.
- **Nothing leaves the trash for good** yet.
- **Certification is automatic.** The gates and the characteristics check are not play-tested parity (M6). 92 cards whose engine text may predate today's Oracle wording are marked in their details.
