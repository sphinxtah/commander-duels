# v0.6.1: the top bar, Alpha and Beta, and Two-Headed Giant's team priority

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-24. This follows [the v0.6.1 plan](../plans/0.6.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-24 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.6.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v061)):
- The top bar's page links retire; the main menu's tiles lead everywhere, and the player search sits in the middle.
- Deleting a deck asks first.
- Alpha and Beta packs and starter decks in Leng's shop, priced from the market and following the real print runs.
- Two-Headed Giant's team priority, team choices and attacks legal as a whole.
- A bot can take control of a player's turn.

## Added

- **Two-Headed Giant: team priority and team choices** (rules 805.2, 805.3, 805.5 to 805.7). A team has priority: a player who passes hands it to a teammate who has not passed since the team last acted, and the other team gets it only once both have passed. A team puts all its players' triggered abilities on the stack in one order, chosen in one prompt by its primary player, or by its human player when the primary player is a bot. Mulligans go team by team, the starting team first. A seventh XMage patch (`0007-two-headed-giant-team-priority`); each prompt of a team decision says what it is to the team ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md#team-priority-and-team-choices-v061)).
- **A bot can take control of a turn** (rule 723, every format): Word of Command, Opposition Agent, Mindslaver and the like work for a bot. Its AI makes the controlled player's decisions, a human's included, who is asked nothing meanwhile and sees that the bot controls their turn; nothing hidden reaches any human seat. Until now XMage refused, and the control was lost. An XMage patch of its own (`0008-ai-turn-control`; [a bot in control](../engine/human-seat.md#a-bot-in-control-v061)).
- **Two-Headed Giant: one attack and one block a team, legal as a whole** (805.10b, 805.10d). Each player declares for their own creatures, a human player sees the team's declaration again when a teammate changes it, and the restrictions are checked against the whole attack or block: Mogg Flunkies may attack beside a teammate's creature, and two teammates may each block a creature with menace once.

## Changed

- **Two-Headed Giant is no longer marked "Early"**: the Play page says "Open to play" and the practice table drops the mark (`GET /api/v1/formats` says `early: false`). The 17 rare cards the card audit left wrong are still listed in [its review](../engine/two-headed-giant-audit.csv).
