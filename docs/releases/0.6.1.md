# v0.6.1: the top bar, Alpha and Beta, and Two-Headed Giant's team priority

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-24. This follows [the v0.6.1 plan](../plans/0.6.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-24 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.6.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v061)):
- The top bar's page links retire; the main menu's tiles lead everywhere, and the player search sits in the middle.
- Deleting a deck asks first.
- Alpha and Beta packs and starter decks in Leng's shop, priced from the market and following the real print runs.
- Two-Headed Giant's team priority, team choices and attacks legal as a whole.
- A bot can take control of a player's turn.

## Added

- **Alpha and Beta in Leng's shop** (the owner, 2026-10-24): their boosters and starter decks are on the shelves with Unlimited's, in the first years. From v0.4.0 they were kept for tournaments; a tournament may still give them as prizes later ([the shop](../shop.md#products)).
  - **Priced on the same curve** as every pack, from their market on 2026-10-24: the Beta booster from its ungraded sales ($8,464.17: 14,000,000 tokens); the Alpha booster ($24,183.37: 61,000,000) and both starter decks (Beta $36,275.05: 110,000,000; Alpha $96,733.52: 440,000,000) estimated from it, as no sale of them could be found ([pack prices](../pack-prices.md#alpha-and-beta-v061)).
  - **The real print runs:** all 597 Alpha and Beta printings are in `content/print-runs.json` with their copies printed: Alpha 1,100 of each rare, 4,500 of each uncommon and 15,900 of each common; Beta 3,200, 13,400 and 47,800; each basic land by how often it appears on each sheet. A printing stops being dealt once as many copies as were printed are in libraries ([print runs](../shop.md#print-runs-v051)).
  - **Pack artwork:** the four products' pictures from TCGplayer and the two sets' symbols.
