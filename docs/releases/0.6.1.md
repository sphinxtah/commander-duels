# v0.6.1: the top bar, Alpha and Beta, and Two-Headed Giant's team priority

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-24. This follows [the v0.6.1 plan](../plans/0.6.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-24 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.6.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v061)):
- The top bar's page links retire; the main menu's tiles lead everywhere, and the player search sits in the middle.
- Deleting a deck asks first.
- Alpha and Beta packs and starter decks in Leng's shop, priced from the market and following the real print runs.
- Two-Headed Giant's team priority, team choices and attacks legal as a whole.
- A bot can take control of a player's turn.

## Added

- **Alpha and Beta in Leng's shop** (the owner, 2026-10-24): their boosters and starter decks are on the shelves with Unlimited's, in the first years. From v0.4.0 they were kept for tournaments; a tournament may still give them as prizes later ([the shop](../shop.md#products)).
  - **Priced on the same curve** as every pack, from their market on 2026-10-24: the Beta booster from its ungraded sales ($8,464.17: 14,000,000 tokens); the Alpha booster ($24,183.37: 61,000,000) and both starter decks (Beta $36,275.05: 110,000,000; Alpha $96,733.52: 440,000,000) estimated from it, as no sale of them could be found ([pack prices](../pack-prices.md#alpha-and-beta-v061)).
  - **The real print runs:** all 597 Alpha and Beta printings are in `content/print-runs.json` with their copies printed: Alpha 1,100 of each rare, 4,500 of each uncommon and 15,900 of each common; Beta 3,200, 13,400 and 47,800; each basic land by how often it appears on each sheet. A printing stops being dealt once as many copies as were printed are in libraries ([print runs](../shop.md#print-runs-v051)).
  - **Pack artwork:** the four products' pictures from TCGplayer and the two sets' symbols.
- **Two-Headed Giant: team priority and team choices** (rules 805.2, 805.3, 805.5 to 805.7). A team has priority: a player who passes hands it to a teammate who has not passed since the team last acted, and the other team gets it only once both have passed. A team puts all its players' triggered abilities on the stack in one order, chosen in one prompt by its primary player, or by its human player when the primary player is a bot. Mulligans go team by team, the starting team first. A seventh XMage patch (`0007-two-headed-giant-team-priority`); each prompt of a team decision says what it is to the team ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md#team-priority-and-team-choices-v061)).
- **A bot can take control of a turn** (rule 723, every format): Word of Command, Opposition Agent, Mindslaver and the like work for a bot. Its AI makes the controlled player's decisions, a human's included, who is asked nothing meanwhile and sees that the bot controls their turn; nothing hidden reaches any human seat. Until now XMage refused, and the control was lost. An XMage patch of its own (`0008-ai-turn-control`; [a bot in control](../engine/human-seat.md#a-bot-in-control-v061)).
- **Two-Headed Giant: one attack and one block a team, legal as a whole** (805.10b, 805.10d). Each player declares for their own creatures, a human player sees the team's declaration again when a teammate changes it, and the restrictions are checked against the whole attack or block: Mogg Flunkies may attack beside a teammate's creature, and two teammates may each block a creature with menace once.

## Changed

- **The top bar holds no pages** (the owner's request): Home, Play, Tables, Decks, Cards and Shop left it, and the phone's bar of them at the bottom of the window went too. The main menu's tiles lead to every page, the mark at the top left leads home, and every other page's band has its "Main menu" button. **The player search is centred over the main menu's tiles**, on every page; the tokens, Friends, the bell and the account stay at the top right ([the menus](../menus.md#the-top-bar)).
- **Two-Headed Giant is no longer marked "Early"**: the Play page says "Open to play" and the practice table drops the mark (`GET /api/v1/formats` says `early: false`). The 17 rare cards the card audit left wrong are still listed in [its review](../engine/two-headed-giant-audit.csv).
- **A player whose turn another controls** reads that the controller "makes your choices while the control lasts" (it said "until it ends", wrong for Word of Command's short control).
- **Sign out is in the account's menu** at the top right of every page (the owner's request); *Sign out everywhere* stays on Profile ([identity](../identity.md)).
- **Profile is in tabs** (the owner's request: it scrolled on too long): Achievements first, then Alliance, Record, Tokens, Details, Privacy and Sign-ins ([the menus](../menus.md#profile-in-tabs-v061)).

## Fixed

- **Deleting a deck asks first** (the owner's report): "Delete <deck>? It goes to the trash, where you can restore it.", with Keep it or Delete, as conceding a match does ([decks](../decks.md)).
- **Your own auctions are on the market** (the owner's report: two cards listed, not found when searching). The market left the viewer's own listings out, as Madden's does, so a seller saw an empty market and counted no live auctions. They are now shown, badged "Your listing", and counted; they open without bidding ([the auction house](../auctions.md#search-the-market)).
