# v0.6.1: the top bar, Alpha and Beta, and Two-Headed Giant's team priority

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-24. This follows [the v0.6.1 plan](../plans/0.6.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-24 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.6.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v061)):
- The top bar's page links retire; the main menu's tiles lead everywhere, and the player search sits in the middle.
- Deleting a deck asks first.
- Alpha and Beta packs and starter decks in Leng's shop, priced from the market and following the real print runs.
- Two-Headed Giant's team priority, team choices and attacks legal as a whole.
- A bot can take control of a player's turn.

## Changed

- **The top bar holds no pages** (the owner's request): Home, Play, Tables, Decks, Cards and Shop left it, and the phone's bar of them at the bottom of the window went too. The main menu's tiles lead to every page, the mark at the top left leads home, and every other page's band has its "Main menu" button. **The player search is centred over the main menu's tiles**, on every page; the tokens, Friends, the bell and the account stay at the top right ([the menus](../menus.md#the-top-bar)).

## Fixed

- **Deleting a deck asks first** (the owner's report): "Delete <deck>? It goes to the trash, where you can restore it.", with Keep it or Delete, as conceding a match does ([decks](../decks.md)).
- **Your own auctions are on the market** (the owner's report: two cards listed, not found when searching). The market left the viewer's own listings out, as Madden's does, so a seller saw an empty market and counted no live auctions. They are now shown, badged "Your listing", and counted; they open without bidding ([the auction house](../auctions.md#search-the-market)).
