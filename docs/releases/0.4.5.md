# v0.4.5: the facelift

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-22 (tag `v0.4.5`). The menus and pages take the look of Magic Duels' front end, the placeholder pages and a test build's words go, every card the engine cannot play leaves the site, and whoever starts a table chooses which cards by legality its decks may hold. This follows [the v0.4.5 plan](../plans/0.4.5.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.5.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v045)):
- A home hub of large tiles under a game-style top bar replaces the sidebar; every page has a title band with a way back to the main menu. The owner's own art only: Planeswalker portraits, the banner, Leng's painting and the pack art.
- Campaign, Learn and Collection leave the menu; milestone eyebrows and "Testing preview" go; formats not built yet show as "Coming soon".
- New tables and practice games start casual: banned cards and cards not legal in the format are both allowed, each a separate option, with a "Tournament rules" shortcut. The lobby shows each table's rules as tags. Every deck is checked against them before it is seated and when the game starts.
- The catalog learns Scryfall's `prepare` layout; every other card the engine cannot play is removed from the site for everyone, except Administration's catalog review.
- Added during the version: SysOp resets an account to a fresh start, so its player sees the setup wizard again.

## Changed

- **Magic Duels' menus** (the owner's request). The sidebar gives way to a game-style top bar: the mark that leads home, the main menu's six pages (Home, Play, Tables, Decks, Cards, Shop), the player search, the account's tokens, Friends, Messages, the bell, and the account's own button, with its ally's portrait, handle, tier and wins, which opens Profile, Settings, Administration and Report a problem. On a phone the six pages move to a bar along the bottom ([the menus](../menus.md)).
- **A main menu.** The app now opens on a main menu of large tiles, as Magic Duels did: the account's ally stands in a pool of light beside the way to play, with its tier and the way to the next; the tiles lead to Play (or back to a game still running), Tables (with how many are open), Leng's Card Shop (with the week's sale), Decks, Cards and Friends (with who is online). The installed app starts there too.
- **Every page in the new look.** A title band with the way back to the main menu runs across every other page; titles are in Cinzel, in gold leaf; buttons are bevelled gold; panels have a gold frame. Pages rise in as they open, tiles lift and glow, embers drift behind the main menu, and the menus make generated sounds at the table's volume, all of which Reduce motion and the sound settings control as before.
- **A title screen.** Before sign-in, the owner's mark glows over the banner, dimmed far behind it, with embers rising; the sign-in stays below it.
- **No Campaign, Learn or Collection,** placeholders since v0.0.1; their old addresses open the main menu. **No test build's words:** "Testing preview", milestone labels and planned formats are gone; Two-Headed Giant shows as "Coming soon", and Settings lists what this version offers.
- **Prepare cards play.** The catalog reads Scryfall's prepare layout (Secrets of Strixhaven, Reality Fracture) as the engine plays it, an adventure under its front face's name: 66 of the 72 cards are in the game. XMage 1.4.62 lacks Whiplash Wordsmith and five Alchemy cards. A version that learns a layout imports again as soon as it deploys ([catalog](../catalog.md#prepare-cards-v045)).
- **Cards the engine cannot play leave the game, for everyone** (the owner's decision). Search, card pages, the deck builder, guided creation, imports, libraries, the alliance tab and SysOp's deck lists show only playable cards, and tokens leave search too; "Show cards not playable yet", the "Playable here" filter and the playability search terms are gone. Library copies of a removed card stay recorded but hidden, and come back if the engine learns it ([catalog](../catalog.md#only-playable-cards-are-in-the-game-v045)).
- **Decks follow.** A removed card leaves every deck's draft when the version deploys, after any sync that takes its playability away, and on a staff suspension; the deck says which cards left and why until its owner dismisses the note. Versions that games played keep their cards ([decks](../decks.md#cards-that-leave-the-game-v045)).
- **Leng's packs** draw another card from the same sheet instead of a removed one, keeping the pack's size and rarity odds, and the opening records which card was replaced ([shop](../shop.md#buying-and-opening)).
- **Administration's catalog review** lists the cards the engine's checks held back (12 on 2026-10-22, Un and playtest variants); accepting one puts it in the game for good, audited and kept through later syncs. A suspension now survives a sync, so it can be restored afterwards (it could not before).
- **Tables choose their cards** (the owner's request). Whoever opens a table decides whether its decks may hold banned cards and cards not legal in its format (Un-cards, playtest and digital-only cards). A new table is casual and allows both; Tournament rules allows neither; the format's deck rules always apply. Tables open at the upgrade became casual ([human tables](../tables.md#the-tables-rules-v045)).
- **Every deck is checked before it sits down.** Opening, joining and changing deck refuse a deck the table's rules do not take, naming its cards; every deck is checked again when the game starts, and the host learns only whose deck no longer fits. The lobby shows each table's rules as tags beside its seats ("Casual", "Tournament rules", or the one kind of card it allows), and so do the table's own page and its invite link.
- **No deck is illegal for its cards' legality any more.** A deck holding banned or not-legal cards is ready to play at the tables that allow them: the deck list tags it "Casual tables only", and the editor says which tables take it ([decks](../decks.md#validation-deck08-t03t05)). Practice games take the same rules, casual unless the player chooses others. The engine never checked our decks' legality, so it needed no change.
- **SysOp resets an account** (the owner's request, for a beta tester who wanted to try the setup wizard again). Administration → Accounts → "Reset *handle*'s account…", for SysOp alone (`admin.users.reset`): the account becomes a new one, so the setup wizard runs again. Its handle, alliance, library and packs are cleared, its decks go to the trash, and its wins go back to 0 and its tokens to 1,000 through the ledgers. Friends, messages, reports and settings stay; SysOp confirms by typing the handle, and the reset is audited (`admin.user_reset`). The player's app notices the next time its tab is used, and the wizard opens ([identity](../identity.md#resetting-an-account-v045)).
- **After a reset** the two free starter decks can be chosen again, and the shop sells each other starter deck once more. Grants and packs from before the reset stay recorded but no longer count.

Migrations `0018_card_removal` (a deck's note of cards that left, a pack card's replaced printing), `0019_table_rules` (a table's two options, true by default, and a deck's stored needs) and `0020_account_reset` (when an account was last reset) only add columns, so the previous version runs on them.

## Deploying 0.4.5

In this order:
1. **The private engine image.** The engine did not change, but `deploy.sh` pulls the private image at the version's tag: the private repository's CI builds `ghcr.io/sphinxtah/commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.4.5` once the tag's run has published the `0.4.5` images.
2. **Deploy:** `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Nothing to change in `.env` or on the TSH proxy.
3. **What happens by itself:** when the worker starts, it sees that the last catalog import set aside the prepare layout, which this version knows, and imports again at once from the files it already has, after the engine answers (a few minutes). Then every draft that holds a card the engine cannot play loses it, with a note on the deck. Open tables become casual.
4. **Afterwards:** the title screen and the main menu; a table's tags in the lobby; Cards no longer offers "Show cards not playable yet"; a prepare card (Abigale, Poet Laureate) found in Cards; Administration → Card catalog lists the held-back cards with Accept; Administration → Accounts shows "Reset … account" to SysOp alone.
