# v0.9.1: cards that open themselves, the training hand, the campaign measured, The One Ring kept

Opened and closed on 2026-11-07. The next version is v0.9.2 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.2.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-11-07: "Instead of the Details link for the card, just make the cards themselves clickable to pop up and show details." From the start questions: clickable cards everywhere, King's Table's house rule on in practice, the campaign's training hand and lessons, and its difficulty measured; the decks cleared before v0.9.0 dropped as unrecoverable ([the plan](../plans/0.9.1.md)). Added during the work: The One Ring 001/001 is to exist once, in Sphinx's library.

## Changed

- **Cards open their details.** In an opened pack's summary (and a land pack's Every card) the card itself is the button: a click, or Enter, opens its details, and it lifts under the pointer. The Details link is gone. In the auction house's item panel the card's picture opens its details, and the Card details button is gone ([the shop](../shop.md), [auctions](../auctions.md)).
- **King's Table practice games default the house rule on** ("King can attack anyone"), so a practice table of bots doesn't stall; tables keep it off unless the host turns it on (`kings_table.PRACTICE_DEFAULTS`, [King's Table](../kings-table.md)).
- **Learn Commander deals the training seven.** The loaner's opening hand is the handoff's three Plains, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Raise the Alarm and Pacifism, whatever the seed: `content/campaign.json` names it (`training_hand`), the API sends it as the human seat's deck list `opening`, and the engine puts those cards on top of the library after the game's first shuffle (never after a mulligan). Only a human seat's list may name an opening, at most seven cards the deck holds ([the campaign](../campaign.md)).
- **Kaya's lessons keep their moments.** A lesson whose moment came while another showed is looked at again when that one closes, so the mulligan's lesson and the first creature's are no longer lost behind the welcome; during the mulligan T02 comes first, then T01 after the keep; T11 also comes when the attackers offered would deal lethal damage unblocked.
- **The One Ring 001/001 stays one of a kind and Sphinx's** ([one of a kind](../progression.md#one-of-a-kind-v051)). Each worker start (each deploy) grants Sphinx the one copy when Sphinx's library holds none and no other account but SysOp's does: an account made after migration 0027, or emptied by a reset, gets it; a second copy is never made. SysOp's testing grants refuse the printing to every account, SysOp's own included (`409 one_of_a_kind`). Packs, auctions and other players' decks already refused it.

## Added

- **The campaign's difficulty, measured** ([the campaign](../campaign.md#difficulty-measured-v091)): `CampaignDifficulty` in the engine's tests (run only on request) plays a reference deck suite against every ward on the real engine, and `scripts/campaign_difficulty.py` sums it up. The first run, the loaner at skill 3, three games a ward, found the curve backwards: wardens 7 to 14 won none of 24 games. Skills were left as they are; the decks are the lever, for v0.9.2.
