# v0.7.0: the special effects layer

A minor version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-30 (tag `v0.7.0`). The music the owner planned for it on 2026-10-26 moved to [v0.7.1](../plans/0.7.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-10-30: "I want this session to be adding some special effects during gameplay when cool things happen … eg. a red jagged X being slashed over the top of fallen creature to signify it died in combat, right before it goes to the graveyard, with a slashing sound effect. … I want it to be nice, shiny, and wow-factored, but make sure it's not excessive where it's distracting to the actual gameplay; we want it to enhance gameplay with a new layer of effects." Nothing else was in the version ([the v0.7.0 plan](../plans/0.7.0.md)).

## Added

- **The special effects layer** over the table, under the emblem, drawn by the WebGL scene for the moments worth a flourish. Like the animation and the sound, it is planned from the events between two committed views and never from the log, so nothing a seat may not see can show in it ([the game screen](../game-screen.md#the-table)):
  - A **creature slain in combat** (attacking or blocking in a combat step when it left): a jagged red X slashed over the card in two strokes, a red flash over its face and sparks as the second stroke lands, with a blade sound; the card waits under its X before it flies to the graveyard, as the owner asked.
  - A creature that **died another way**: grey ash rising off it.
  - A **commander cast**: a gold burst at the stack. A **spell of six or more mana taking effect**: a burst in the colours of its cost where its card lands (told by the card it became, since the stack shows no cost; a hidden card is never big).
  - A **creature of power five or more** entering: a dust ring at its foot.
  - **Attacks**: embers off the attackers. **Blocks**: a steel frame closing on the blockers. Six of each at most.
  - **Five or more life lost at once**: a red shock ring off the portrait and sparks, and for your own life (or your team's) the screen's edges flaring red once; ten or more makes it bigger. **Five or more gained**: green glitter.
  - A **player eliminated**: the portrait's dark shards flung out, with the crack the table already drew.
  - **Victory**: confetti across the view. **Defeat**: ash drifting down. **Your turn**: a gold shine along the band.
- **Settings › Special effects**: Full, Subtle (the marks and rings without any particle or edge flare) or Off, on the Settings page under Display & accessibility and in the table's Options drawer, kept on the device like the other table settings (`cd.table.v1`, `effects`). Reduce motion turns the layer off whatever it says; the plain table has none.
- **Four sound cues**: `slash` (a combat death, in place of `die`), `arcane` (a commander cast in place of `cast`, and a big spell taking effect), `impact` (a blow of five or more life, in place of `damage`) and `shatter` (an elimination, which had no sound). They follow the sound settings.
- **Three facts in the table's events** for all of this: `leave.combat`, `cast.commander`, and `resolve.big` with `resolve.colours`. The thresholds are in `web/src/game/table/notable.ts`.

## Changed

- `DURATIONS` (`web/src/game/scene/timeline.ts`) gains the effects' lengths; they scale with the animation speed as the other motion does.
- The scene takes an `effects` level (`setEffects`), and `SceneHost` passes it from the settings.
- The e2e fake table's quiet settings turn the effects off: the journeys check the table's state, not its sparks.

## Not changed

- The server: no API route, WebSocket frame, engine message, migration or `.env` setting. The VM does nothing more for the effects; they are a few `Graphics` strokes and sprites of three small textures made once in the browser, batched by PixiJS, with no filters and fixed, small particle counts.
- The animation and sounds the table had: the layer is added over them.

## Deploying 0.7.0

No `.env` change and no migration. The engine did not change, but `deploy.sh` pulls the private engine image at the tag, so `commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.7.0` must be built too. Once the tag's run has published the `0.7.0` images and the private engine image exists, on the VM: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`.
