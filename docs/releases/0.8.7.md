# v0.8.7: King's Table, Play for the bots, and passive bots for administrators

A patch version, opened and closed on 2026-11-03. The next version is v0.8.8 ([the plan](../plans/0.8.8.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-11-03, at the start: "Lets do v0.8.7. I want to add Emperor, except with my own custom rules." The owner sent a developer specification, then renamed the mode the same evening: "Lets rename it from Emperor to King's Table." The owner also asked for two changes to the menus and one to passive bots:

- **King's Table** to the owner's specification. Asked at the start, with every recommended default:
  - **Where it's played.** At tables, with humans in any seats and bots filling the rest, and on the practice table, one player and five bots. Not in the public queues.
  - **Seats.** Each player takes an open seat. The host moves players and places bots, and any change clears readiness.
  - **Bots.** They never deploy creatures.
  - **Pay.** As at other tables: each human on the winning team is paid, a General eliminated earlier included. A draw pays nobody.
- **Play is for the bots.** "Find a table" belongs in Tables, and the giant header with the Varragoth image and "Gather your cards. Make your mark." goes. "The Play section should just be for vs bots; Tables section is where people go to play with other humans."
- **Administrators can switch on passive bots during play**, as SysOp can.
- **Not taken:** the plan's carried items (the reports, the campaign's tuning in play, its spoils and cosmetics, the v0.7.3 notes), which stay in [the v0.8.8 plan](../plans/0.8.8.md).

## Added

### King's Table

Six players in two teams of three, each team with a King and two Generals: Magic's Emperor variant (809) with the owner's settings and house rule. A team wins when the other team's King is out ([King's Table](../kings-table.md)).

- **The settings**, chosen by the host before anyone readies. All twelve combinations play, and they hold for the whole game, reconnects and rematches included.
  - The deck format for all six players, Legacy or Commander, with that format's own legality and banned list.
  - The starting life: everyone 20, everyone 40, or Generals 20 and Kings 40.
  - The house rule "King can attack anyone".
- **Commander decks.** Every player has their own commander, the command zone and commander tax, and loses to 21 combat damage from one commander at any starting life.
- **Legacy decks.** They never use Commander's rules, and keep their sideboard as Legacy does.
- **Seats around the table.** Team A's Left General, King and Right General, then team B's Right General, King and Left General. Each General faces the opposing General beside them.
- **Turns.** Each player takes their own turns. A random King starts and draws, and the first mulligan is free.
- **Teammates.** They see each other's hands and are never opponents.
- **Range of influence.** One seat each way for a General, two for a King. It is set for everyone as each turn begins, and spells, abilities and effects keep to it.
- **Attacks, with the house rule off.** A player may attack only an opponent beside them and in their range, so neither King can attack at the start. A player leaving opens new neighbours at once, and new range at the next turn.
- **Attacks, with the house rule on.** Either King's creatures may attack any opponent, the other King included, as a whole combat: the defender blocks, damage is dealt both ways, and both players' abilities see the combat. Spells and abilities still keep to range, and the permission goes with the attacking player, so a King's creature that a General controls attacks as the General's.
- **Deploying creatures** (804): "{T}: Target teammate in your range gains control of this creature. Activate only as a sorcery." Players only: the bots never deploy.
- **Winning and losing.**
  - A General who loses leaves the game, and the team plays on.
  - A King who loses or concedes loses the game for the whole team. Both Kings at once is a draw.
  - The team's result counts for all three of its players: the record, the win count and the pay.
  - With every human out, the bots play on to the team's result.
- **At a table**:
  - **Opening one.** The settings, your seat, and which seats bots take.
  - **The table's page.** The six seats as two facing teams, with roles and starting life, and the rules spelled out above the seats.
  - **The host's panel.** It changes the settings, gives a seat to a bot or back to the players, and moves a player. Each change clears readiness.
  - **Readying.** It checks your deck against the deck format.
- **On the practice table**: the three settings, your seat by team and role, a deck of the deck format, and five bots.
- **At the table during play**:
  - **The layout.** A six-seat arrangement shows all five other players at once, in order around the table.
  - **Each player's plate.** It shows the player's role (a crown for a King), team, life and starting life, and separate marks for "In range" and "Can attack". Attacks can be dropped only on players you can attack.
  - **The settings.** The game menu shows them.
  - **The result.** It names the winning team, or a draw ([the game screen](../game-screen.md#kings-table-v087)).
- **In the engine.**
  - Two game types, one for Legacy decks and one for Commander decks, share the rules.
  - XMage patch 0011 adds allies who keep their own turns, a game's own attack rules, combat across range, and each player's own starting life, which the 17 cards that read "your starting life total" now use. Every default keeps other formats as they were ([the patches](../../engine/xmage/patches/README.md)).
  - The seat views carry each player's team, role, starting life, range and attack reach ([the seat-view contract](../../contracts/engine-seat-view.schema.json)).
- **In the API.**
  - `POST /api/v1/tables` and `POST /api/v1/practice-games` take `kings_table`, `seat` and (tables) `bot_seats`.
  - The new `PATCH /api/v1/tables/{code}` is the host's.
  - Summaries carry the settings and each seat's role, team and starting life, and the recorded result carries `winner_team`.
  - Migration 0036 ([tables](../tables.md#kings-table-v087), [games through the API](../games.md)).

### Play for the bots

- **Play** has a plain "Battle the bots." heading in place of the owner's banner and "Gather your cards. Make your mark.". It holds the practice table and the formats, and links to Tables for playing other people ([menus](../menus.md)).
- **Tables** begins with "Find a game", the public queues. `#find` opens it, and the main menu's Tables tile shows a place in a queue and leads back to it ([queues](../queues.md)).

### Passive bots for administrators

- **Administrators** get the switch SysOp has had since v0.8.6, in their own practice game or campaign duel with no other human seat, through a new permission, `games.passive_bots` ([identity](../identity.md)).
- **Where it is.** An administrator's switch sits at the foot of the Priority controls drawer, since only SysOp has the Inspection drawer, and SysOp's stays there ([the game screen](../game-screen.md#passive-bots-v086-administrators-v087)).
- **Unchanged.** A player's game isn't an administrator's to see or switch, and a game played with passive bots still pays nothing out.

## Deploying 0.8.7

**Migration 0036.**

- It adds `kings_table` columns to games and tables.
- It widens their format checks.
- It replaces the tables' seat check: King's Table seats six with at least one player, and other tables keep two to four seats with two players.

v0.8.6 keeps serving while it runs. There's no `.env` change.

**The engine image** compiles XMage again, because patch 0011 changes the XMage layer.

**The private images must exist at the tag first.** Run the private repository's engine and web workflows once the tag's run has published the public `0.8.7` images. The private engine image builds on the public one, and patch 0011 only adds to XMage's interfaces, so the private card plugin needs no change.

**Then, on the VM, as always:** `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`.
