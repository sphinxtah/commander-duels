# v0.4.3: pack artwork, and finding other players

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-22. It gives every pack in Leng's card shop its picture, and adds what the owner asked for when v0.4.2 closed: a player search in the top bar, player profiles, mutual friends, who is online and in a game, privacy settings, and a notification bell. This follows [the v0.4.3 plan](../plans/0.4.3.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-22 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.3.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v043)):
- Pack artwork: the real pack designs, from Wizards' and retailers' product images, served from this site; a generated design for every pack with none, and for new sets.
- The starter decks' prices stay as they are.
- The player search, profiles, friends, presence, privacy settings and the notification bell, as the owner decided before the version started.
- The Python test run that hung in CI twice: a stack dump and a time limit, so a hang fails in minutes.

## Changed

- **Finding other players** (the owner's request). A search in the middle of the top bar, on every page of the app, finds players by handle, in part, as it is typed, or by email address, only typed in full and sent with Enter; no answer ever shows an email. A result opens the player's profile ([finding players](../social.md#the-player-search)).
- **Player profiles.** `#player/<id>`: the handle, portrait, tier, wins and first month, the presence when shared, and **Add friend** ([profiles](../social.md#profiles)).
- **Friends, mutual** (the owner's decision). A request shows **Pending** until the other player accepts it or adds back; either may withdraw, decline or end it. A new **Friends** page lists requests, friends by what they are doing, and requests sent. Players can **block** each other ([friends](../social.md#friends), [blocking](../social.md#blocking)).
- **Presence.** Friends see each other in a game (with its format), online, idle or offline, from the app's own check-in every 30 seconds and the server's running games ([presence](../social.md#presence)).
- **Privacy settings** on Profile: presence for friends (the default) or everyone; appear offline, which administrators and SysOp see through, silently; being found by email; and the blocked players ([privacy settings](../social.md#privacy-settings)). The [privacy policy](../../web/public/pages/privacy.html) says so, effective 2026-10-22.
- **The notification bell** (the owner's request), top right on every page: a count, a bubble with the newest notifications and a link to the full history; friend requests answered from it; friends coming online pop up for a moment ([notifications](../social.md#notifications-and-the-bell)).
- **Deleting an account** also removes its friends, requests, blocks and notifications.
- **CI.** pytest prints every thread's stack when one test runs over 5 minutes (`faulthandler_timeout`), and the verify job stops after 30 minutes and the engine job after 60, so a hang like the two of 2026-10-22 fails in minutes instead of holding a runner.

Migration `0015_friends` adds the tables and columns; it only adds, so the previous version runs on it.
