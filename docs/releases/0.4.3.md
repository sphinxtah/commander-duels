# v0.4.3: pack artwork, and finding other players

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-22. It gives every pack in Leng's card shop its picture, and adds what the owner asked for when v0.4.2 closed: a player search in the top bar, player profiles, mutual friends, who is online and in a game, privacy settings, and a notification bell. This follows [the v0.4.3 plan](../plans/0.4.3.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-22 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.3.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v043)):
- Pack artwork: the real pack designs, from Wizards' and retailers' product images, served from this site; a generated design for every pack with none, and for new sets.
- The starter decks' prices stay as they are.
- The player search, profiles, friends, presence, privacy settings and the notification bell, as the owner decided before the version started.
- The Python test run that hung in CI twice: a stack dump and a time limit, so a hang fails in minutes.

## Changed

- **Every pack in Leng's shop has its picture.** 365 of the 392 products show their real sealed pack, copied once from TCGplayer's product pages (the owner's choice, accepting the licence risk of a retailer's images) and served from this site; the other 27 (the six-card boosters, and a few with no usable picture), and every set the booster data job adds later, are drawn in the browser from their set: its colours, era, name, expansion symbol and booster type. The pictures show on the shelves, among "Your packs", in "Packs you opened" and at Leng's counter, where the pack that tears open is the product's own. They load when shown and stay out of the installed app's precache. The starter decks keep their commanders' cards. `scripts/pack_art.py` makes them again ([the shop](../shop.md#pack-artwork-v043)).
- **Leng's counter is the owner's painting**, at the top of the shop and, blurred and dimmed, behind every pack opened; it replaced the v0.4.0 placeholder ([the shop](../shop.md#lengs-counter-v043)).
