# v0.8.0: the Campaign

A minor version, opened and closed on 2026-10-31. The owner opened it for the Campaign section of the game, planned since v0.7.3 ([the v0.7.3 plan](../plans/0.7.3.md#scope-agreed-for-this-version-2026-10-31)), and asked for the close, the commit and the push in the same breath. The next version was v0.8.1, a hotfix ([release notes](0.8.1.md)), then v0.8.2 ([the plan](../plans/0.8.2.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-10-31: "Lets do v0.8.0. This will be the campaign addition. We'll need to add a Campaign tile to the main menu. In the project root is a folder named campaign. Look through the documentation to understand the full scope. I want there to be cut scenes between battles as you progress through the campaign. I want you to use the planeswalkers' images to show up on the screen during the dialogue moments with speech bubbles for their lines. I want the campaign to have a rich story line that's also immersive from the cut scene special effects and using images to provide immersion. Also, I've added song 21 in the music folder, which will need to be added to the playlist and made available to pull from the VM via Github. Read through the documentation and build it." And: "When finished, close the version, commit, and push to Github."

While it was being built, two more (2026-10-31): "As you advance in the campaign, beating a planeswalker also means the player wins the planeswalker's deck. It will show up in the players deck inventory. Each planeswalker defeated also grants tokens, starting with 1,000 tokens for beating the first, 2,000 for the second, and keep doubling it each time." And: "From the main menu, shorten up the profile tile on the far left to make room for the Campaign tile underneath it. Make the profile tile about 70% of the left space and 30% goes to the Campaign tile. Also attached is the background image to use for the background for the Campaign tile." Then, on the finale's deck coming with the public stand-in: "I want it changed. The Sphinx is the final boss and he needs that High Priest custom card as the Commander. make the needed changes to allow it. The High Priest will be awarded with the deck to the player if they win." And the last change for the version: "When you add mana to your pool directly, later when casting something the pooled mana should show at the top of the list for the first choice."

The folder holds the owner's handoff: the campaign guide (version 1, 2026-10-31: the story, every scene's dialogue, the tutorial, the encounter briefs, the finale's rules, the implementation and art direction and the acceptance checks), the fourteen deck builds, and the final boss's decklist and playbook. It names the Godlike tier's custom cards, so the folder is gitignored, as `Planeswalkers images/` and `the sphinx mtg/` are.

Decisions taken in building it, from the handoff where it decides and otherwise the nearest the site's rules allow:
- **The story is the handoff's, word for word**: all 247 lines under their scene keys, the barks and their triggers, the tutorial's twelve prompts. The staging is ours: seventeen sceneries drawn in SVG and CSS, the effects, the portraits' places. The encounter briefs' settings are narrated to the player in the second person ("You descend beneath the fairground"); their story notes become the journal's "What the seal revealed", and their counterplay Kaya's advice, both with the designer's asides taken out ("Midpoint reversal:", "Tezzeret must not read hidden player cards").
- **The portraits are the owner's** (`web/public/portraits`): all thirteen wardens, Kaya, Liliana and The Sphinx have one; the player stands as their ally's portrait. The High Priest, a survivor and a player without an ally (the Wayfarer) are drawn figures, since the High Priest's own art is a custom card's and stays private. The Sphinx is a silhouette in gold light, nameless, until the finale, as the handoff reserves his portrait and title for it.
- **The finale's private cards stay private.** The Sphinx's commander and his incarnation reach the duel from the owner's engine image (its plugin carries the finale's content; the public engine serves a plugin's content to the API), never from this repository. The incarnation's rules are the engine's, written for any card ([the campaign](../campaign.md#the-finales-incarnation)); an engine without the owner's content plays the finale with a public stand-in commander (Morophon, the Boundless) and no incarnation.
- **The campaign's loaner deck** ("Complete the 100-card loaner during production") is Adeline's Vanguard: Adeline, Resplendent Cathar, 62 simple creatures, removal, tokens, tricks and equipment, 37 Plains, no combinations.
- **The bots' skill rises with the tiers**, 1 to 6 ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#skill-and-habits-v071)), over the handoff's decks, which carry the difficulty.
- **Campaign duels count no wins, pay no win's tokens and advance no quests**, and account wins gate no ward (the handoff's progression separation). The rewards are the seals, the card backs and the titles, recorded once each, and since the owner's word the spoils: a warden's first defeat gives their deck (a deck of the player's own, every card granted to the library) and tokens, 1,000 doubling with each warden to 8,192,000 for The Sphinx (16,383,000 in all). The Sphinx's deck comes with the commander he played, the owner's High Priest, a Godlike custom card, granted to the winner for good: they see and play it whatever their tier (without the owner's engine content, the public stand-in) ([the spoils](../campaign.md#the-spoils)).
- **Learn Commander's training seed** is the disclosed seed `20261031` for the duel's shuffles; the handoff's exact opening seven is not arranged (the engine stacks no library), so the hints follow the hand dealt.
- **Song 21** (Falling In Reverse ft. Jelly Roll, All My Life) is in the private repository's `music/` folder and its manifest, as the twenty before it, so the VM gets it with the private web image ([the live site](../deployment.md#the-web-image-with-the-music-v072)).

## Added

- **The Campaign** ([the campaign](../campaign.md)): the main menu's Campaign tile, under the greeting in the left column (the greeting about 70% of it, the tile the rest, over the owner's painting of the throne: `web/public/menu/campaign-640.webp` and `-1280.webp`), and the Campaign page (`#campaign`): the map of the fourteen wards by tier with their seals, the refuge, the journal, the story settings, the rewards and the rules. The opening cinematic on the first visit; each ward's scene before its duel, then the deck (Learn Commander, the loaner or the player's own); after it the victory's scene with the seal claimed and a chapter's rewards, or the opponent's line and the retreat with Retry and the defeat's review; the endings, standard or early; the replay's framing.
- **The cut scenes**: the scenery behind (seventeen places, layered and drifting, with phases), letterboxed and graded; the speakers' portraits at either side, the one speaking lit and forward; speech bubbles typed out; title, narration, seal, chapter and end cards; twenty-two effects; Next, Auto, the text speed and Skip, Reduce motion and screen readers honoured.
- **The voices at the table**: the opponents' and Kaya's barks by their portraits, Kaya's lessons in the first duel, and The Sphinx's crossing in the finale.
- **The spoils**: each ward's deck and tokens, on the map, the duel's setup, a card after the first win, Decks, My library ("Won in the campaign") and the profile's tokens ("Campaign: beat Tibalt, The Laughing Gate").
- **The API**: `GET /api/v1/campaign` (each encounter's `spoils`), `POST /api/v1/campaign/encounters/{n}/start`, `POST /api/v1/campaign/scenes`, `PUT /api/v1/campaign/tutorial`; a game summary's `campaign`, and a campaign opponent's name and portrait on its bot seat. Migration 0032: `campaign_progress`, `campaign_games`, `campaign_rewards`; the token ledger's kind `campaign`, the library grants' source `campaign`, `campaign_games.prize_card` and `custom_card_grants` (a custom card granted for good).
- **The engine**: a deck list's `reserve` and the `CampaignReserve` rule (the incarnation crossing at its threshold, returning to its reserve, cast again from there), and `GET /v1/service/content/<name>` for a plugin's content.
- **Content**: `content/campaign.json` (the encounters, the thirteen decks and the finale's public 99, the loaner, the chapters and rewards); `web/src/campaign/script.ts` (the script and its staging).

## Changed

- **The playlist** has twenty-one songs: the private repository's `music/` holds track 21 and its manifest names it (1708e1b there); the owner's local `music/playlist.json` beside `compose.yaml` names it too.
- `#campaign` opens the campaign; Learn and Collection's old addresses still open the main menu.
- A practice game's bot seat may carry a name and portrait (a campaign opponent's); the table draws a bot's portrait when its summary gives one.
- **Paying mana**: mana already in your pool is the first choice of a payment, ahead of the sources to tap (the engine lists it first, and the prompt panel puts it first whatever the order).
- **A custom card won for good** (the finale's High Priest) is open to its holder whatever their tier: their library, validation, the deck builder, the engine and its image.
- **The main menu's left column**: the greeting is about 70% of its height, the Campaign tile the rest (on a narrow window both stack above the tiles).
- `docs/menus.md`, `docs/game-screen.md`, `docs/deployment.md`, `docs/tokens.md`, the README and `.gitignore` (the owner's `campaign/` folder).

## Not changed

- Practice games, tables, queues, matches, the shop, the auction house, quests and achievements: a campaign duel is a practice game the campaign reads instead of them. The economy's rules for wins are unchanged; the campaign's tokens are its own entries.
- The gameplay track and the menu playlist's player.
- The private repository's other cards and their rules: the finale uses the owner's two custom cards as they are.

## Deploying 0.8.0

The migration adds four tables and lets the token ledger and the library grants take the campaign's entries (their checks widened; the downgrade removes those entries); no `.env` change. The High Priest is granted by the custom card SysOp uploaded under the name the owner's engine serves: it must be uploaded (Administration → Custom cards) for the finale's deck to come with it. The private images must exist at the tag first: the private repository's engine workflow (it now carries the finale's content) and web workflow (it now carries song 21), run (or scheduled) once the tag's run has published the public `0.8.0` images. Then, on the VM: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Without the private engine image the campaign works to its end with the public stand-in finale.
