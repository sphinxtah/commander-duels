# v0.9.2: Find a game, Open a table, friends invited

Opened and closed on 2026-11-09. The next version is v0.9.3 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.3.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner asked first why King's Table is in Open a Table but not in Find a Game. It was a decision of v0.8.7: King's Table seats its players by role and setting, so it has tables but no public queue (`api/queues.py` refuses it, and the web leaves it out).

Then the owner, 2026-11-09: "Find a Game (public queue) section should show every game table waiting for players (detailed cards), and there should be filters (checkboxes that live-update the queue when selected/unselected). Open a Table should be a button at the top that pops up a big box that you fill out with the details of the kind of game you want to run on the table. … When opening a table, you should also be able to invite your friends to it as well, individually selectable. The cards should be limited to showing 20, 50, or 100 cards/tables with a dropdown to change how many to list per page."

The start questions settled:
- **The public queues stay**, as "Quick match", a pop-up beside "Open a table".
- **Filters**: format, rules, seats and bots (seats open, no bots, best of three), and friends' tables.
- **An invitation** is a notice in the bell with a link to the table, which also lets the friend find a private table. No seat is held, and the table's page can invite more friends later.

Since every table, King's Table's included, is now a card on Find a game, King's Table is found there without a queue of its own.

## Changed

- **Tables is Find a game** ([tables](../tables.md#finding-a-game-and-inviting-friends-v092)). Every table waiting for players is a card: format, Private and Yours badges, host and how long ago it opened, seats, rules and settings, players, open seats, bots, who sits where, who invited you, and your friends there. Checkboxes filter the cards the moment they change: format, Casual or Tournament rules, Seats open, No bots, Best of three, Friends' tables. 20, 50 or 100 a page, with Previous and Next; your own table leads. The browser keeps the filters and the page size.
- **Open a table** is a button at the top, its form in a pop-up, unchanged but for the friends to invite at its end.
- **Quick match** is a button beside it: the public queues, unchanged, in a pop-up. `#find` opens it, so the main menu's tile and the bell's Two-Headed Giant invitations still lead there. While you wait in a queue, the page says so with a link back to it.

## Added

- **Friends invited to tables.** The host picks friends one by one when opening a table, and any of its players invites more from the table's page (Invite friends, beside the invite link). Each friend gets a notice in the bell ("ada invites you to their Commander table.") that leads to the table, and sees the table in the lobby even when it's private. Inviting again sends nothing new; a table may invite 50 players in all.
- **API:** `GET /api/v1/tables/browse` (filters and pages), `POST /api/v1/tables/{code}/invites`, `invite` on `POST /api/v1/tables`; tables carry `open_seats`, and in the lobby's pages `friends` and `invited_by`; notifications of kind `table_invite` carry `table` ([routes](../tables.md#routes)).

## Migration

`0039_table_invites`: the `table_invites` table and the notification kind `table_invite`. It only adds; going back deletes those notices and drops the table.
