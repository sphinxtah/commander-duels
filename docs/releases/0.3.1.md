# v0.3.1: the Planeswalker Alliance

Opened on 2026-10-20. Levels earned by wins open the owner's 98 Planeswalker portraits as alliances, and each alliance brings its Planeswalker's cards into the player's library. Players build from their own library, which starts with two of ten new Commander starter decks. The bots stop seeing their opponents' cards. This follows [the v0.3.1 plan](../plans/0.3.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-20 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.3.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v031)):
- An alliance grants a playset of its Planeswalker's own Planeswalker cards.
- Copies count in the library, as in Magic Duels.
- New and existing players pick two of the ten Commander starter decks, and existing players keep their saved decks' cards.
- A player may switch alliances at any time, and the alliance's portrait is the player's picture.
- Placeholder badges are metal crests.
- Commander bots play the ten new decks.
- The Godlike tier's custom cards are uploaded, hidden and viewable in this version, and played in a later one.
