# v0.3.1: the Planeswalker Alliance

A patch-numbered feature version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-20 and closed on 2026-10-21 (tag `v0.3.1`). Levels earned by wins open the owner's 98 Planeswalker portraits as alliances, and each alliance brings its Planeswalker's cards into the player's library. Players build from their own library, which starts with two of ten new Commander starter decks. The bots stop seeing their opponents' cards. This follows [the v0.3.1 plan](../plans/0.3.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-20 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.3.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v031)):
- An alliance grants a playset of its Planeswalker's own Planeswalker cards.
- Copies count in the library, as in Magic Duels.
- New and existing players pick two of the ten Commander starter decks, and existing players keep their saved decks' cards.
- A player may switch alliances at any time, and the alliance's portrait is the player's picture.
- Placeholder badges are metal crests.
- Commander bots play the ten new decks.
- The Godlike tier's custom cards are uploaded, hidden and viewable in this version, and played in a later one.

## Changed

- **Levels from wins** ([the Planeswalker Alliance](../progression.md#wins-and-levels)). Every game a player wins counts once, in the same transaction that records the game's result, in an append-only progression ledger (PROG03–PROG05). Six tiers: Bronze, Silver at 10 wins, Gold at 100, Platinum at 1,000, Mythic at 10,000 and Godlike at 100,000. Games won before v0.3.1 count. SysOp starts at 100,000 wins and may set any account's wins, with a reason, audited and kept apart from counted wins; administrators see each player's wins, level and alliance.
- **Alliances.** Each tier opens more of the owner's 98 Planeswalkers, as the owner ranked them. A player allies with one at a time and may switch whenever they like; the alliance puts a playset of that Planeswalker's own Planeswalker cards in their library, and its portrait becomes their picture on the profile, in the sidebar and at the table. Each tier has a placeholder badge, a metal crest, until the owner's own arrive.
- **Players build from their own library** ([the library](../progression.md#the-library)). Copies count, as in Magic Duels: a deck may hold no more copies of a card than the library does. A deck holding more is collection-locked: legal and saved, but kept from games until fixed (DECK08). Every card in search and details says how many copies the library holds; the deck builder's finder starts on the library, and the Cards page has an "In my library" filter and a library view. Administrators and SysOp build from the whole catalog.
- **Ten Commander starter decks** from the owner's workbook, one per two-colour pair, each with its description, how to play it and what it teaches. Every player chooses two after choosing a handle; both become decks of their own and every copy of their cards joins the library. Players who joined before v0.3.1 choose theirs at their next visit, and every card in their saved decks joined their library, so nothing they built stopped working. The six old starter decks are no longer offered to players: they stay the engine's test decks, offered to SysOp.
- **The bots play fair** ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md)). A bot no longer plans with its opponents' hands or any library's order: each search re-deals what its seat cannot see, and every search has a time budget with a legal fallback (AI02, AI05). The practice table's notice that the bots read your hand is gone. Commander bots play the ten starter decks, a different one each.
- **The Godlike tier's custom cards** ([custom cards](../progression.md#the-godlike-tiers-custom-cards)). SysOp uploads the owner's own cards in Administration; they live outside the card catalog, and only accounts at the Godlike tier can see them. An alliance with The Sphinx puts them in the library; playing them comes in a later version, with their engine implementations in a private repository.

## Deploying 0.3.1

No `.env` change. Migration 0007 carries existing accounts over: it counts the games each player has won, gives SysOp its 100,000 wins, puts the cards of every player's saved decks in their library, and asks every player to choose their two starter decks at their next visit. After the deploy, SysOp can upload the custom cards in Administration (each image is fitted to the card size in the browser before it is sent).
