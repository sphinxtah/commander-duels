# v0.9.5: quest boards, bigger achievements, opening a warden's deck

Opened and closed on 2026-11-10. The next version is v0.9.6 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.6.md)): the owner's fifteenth ward.

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner opened v0.9.5 with "start on v0.9.5, read the notes and do the needful". Claude's defaults (the streak shown on each board, Arizona's days and Sunday weeks, the streak kept in the server's records, the old quests' history kept, a Quests page with the main menu's panel leading there, board achievements on the ×4 curve) stood, and four questions settled the rest:
- **Scope:** the quest boards and achievements ×4 (the headline), the warden deck opening, campaign tuning, and the classic stand-ins batch 2. The text changers, Spy Kit and Cryptic Spires wait.
- **Quest pay: "Rich"**: daily steps 150, 250, 350, 500 and 750; weekly goals 250, 400 and 650 by tier.
- **Every quest soloable**: every daily quest can be done against bots, and no weekly goal asks for a friend, a human table or a buyer. All twenty weekly goals show at once.
- **The opening's reveal**: the warden's portrait on a deck box, commons to mythic rares, the commander last (the High Priest for The Sphinx); an unopened deck waits on the map with a bell notice.

During the work the owner asked Claude to sidestep and fix the v0.9.4 private engine image, which failed (below).

## Added

- **Quest boards** ([quests](../quests.md#quest-boards-v095)):
  - **Daily: five quests a day**, one per step from the easiest to the hardest (more wins, stronger bots, bigger tables), from a pool of 57, paying 150 to 750 (2,000 a day).
  - **Weekly: twenty goals a week**, fourteen outside the games (Leng's card shop, Tivit's Auction House, decks, card sleeves, the campaign) and six in them, from a pool of 200 in 72 families, at 250, 400 and 650 by tier (about 8,350 a week).
  - **Board bonuses with streaks:** 400 for a full daily board, 100 more each day in a row up to 1,000; 2,000 for a full weekly board, 200 more each week in a row up to 5,000. A missed board starts over. The streak lives in the server's records (`quest_boards`).
  - **No swap.** Each quest completes and pays once, through the ledger (`quests-v2`).
  - **Farming rules**: only real exchanges count. An auction won from another account counts at 50 tokens or more; a listing counts once it stayed up an hour or sold; a pack counts when bought with tokens, and as a sale pack at the sale's price; a deck counts when it is legal and complete and lasted a day; a sleeve counts while it is kept.
  - **Where it shows:** the main menu's panel holds the five daily quests and a line for the week; the **Quests page** (`#quests`) holds both boards with their streaks and the rules. The bell names each quest and each board.
- **Six achievements for the boards**: Full Board, Seven Days Strong, Board Regular, Goal Getter, A Week's Work and Month of Mastery (400 to 1,600 tokens).
- **Opening a warden's deck** ([the campaign](../campaign.md#the-spoils)): after a warden's first defeat, the cut scene with its seal is followed by a pack opening of their 100-card deck from a deck box with their portrait, commons first and the commander last, then the spoils card. It is recorded with the spoils, gives nothing itself, waits on the map until opened ("Warden decks", with a bell notice), and can be watched again.
- **Twelve more classic cards play as printed** (XMage patch 0015, [the catalog](../catalog.md#the-second-batch-of-classic-cards-v095)): Frankenstein's Monster, Reflecting Mirror, Suffocation, Equinox, Silhouette, Backdraft, Quarum Trench Gnomes, North Star, Arcum's Whistle, Winter's Chill, Spoils of War and Ogre Enforcer.

## Changed

- **Achievements pay four times as much** (the owner): First Victory 400, Seasoned Planeswalker 800, Legendary Planeswalker 4,000, and so on, 25,800 tokens for the first 29 (`achievements-v2`). **Players who had already earned achievements are paid the difference when v0.9.5 reaches the live server**: migration 0041 credits each achievement earned since the account's last reset with the new reward less what it paid, once, as "<achievement>: reward raised to <n> tokens".
- **A game's end settles every player's quest boards**, the losers' too (a loss counts for "play games"), so every player's account is now locked in the game's end, in one order.
- **Campaign tuning** from the 30-game run ([the campaign](../campaign.md#difficulty-on-githubs-runners-v094)): stronger decks for Daretti (Wurmcoil Engine, Goblin Welder, Sol Ring, Thought Vessel, Vandalblast), Oko (Mystic Snake, Rhystic Study, Hullbreaker Horror) and Geyadrone Dihada (Dockside Extortionist, Mana Drain); weaker for Dovin (Kor Skyfisher, Divination, Arrest in place of Sun Titan, Sphinx's Revelation, Swords to Plowshares) and Ob Nixilis (Vampire Nighthawk, Charging Monstrosaur, Butcher of Malakir, Bonesplitter in place of Sheoldred, Inferno Titan, Grave Pact, Skullclamp). Skills stay with their tiers. Ward 13's row of the v0.9.4 run came in: Liliana 33% (target 20%), left as is; the finale's stand-in is left too, as the live finale adds the High Priest and the incarnation. The difficulty workflow runs again from `difficulty/v0.9.5`.
