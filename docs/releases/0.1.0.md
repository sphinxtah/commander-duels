# v0.1.0: the testing interface and the live site

The first version you can play. A signed-in, approved player starts a practice game against bots on the Play page and plays it to the end in the browser, by mouse or by keyboard alone. Legacy is against one bot; Commander is against one to three bots at a four-seat table. Cards show their printed images, and the screen shows every prompt the engine types and nothing the seat may not see. SysOp's inspection, deck lists and observing are on screen too. The Play page now opens with the owner's banner. The site runs live at https://duels.thesphinx.co, on a VM on Hathor behind the owner's TSH nginx proxy, and every later version deploys there. The screen is described in [the game screen](../game-screen.md) and the hosting in [the live site](../deployment.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-19 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.1.0.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v010)):
- **Formats on screen:** Legacy, and Commander up to four seats (the owner chose the four-seat layout).
- **Card faces:** card images (the owner's choice), looked up by name from Scryfall, cached by the API and served from the site itself.
- **Who plays:** approved players, approved by SysOp, until the site opens to the public at v1.0.0; a notice about AI02.
- **Priority controls:** pass priority with XMage's default stops.
- **The live site,** the owner's condition for v0.1.0:
  - `duels.thesphinx.co` on a Debian 13 VM on Hathor, behind the TSH nginx proxy;
  - CI builds the images and the VM pulls them;
  - this computer deploys over SSH.

## Included

- **The practice table** on the Play page:
  - format, your deck and each bot's, the number of bots, bot skill and a turn limit;
  - SysOp's deck lists with card-name lookup;
  - your running games (resume, close) and recent results; for SysOp, every player's games.
- **The game screen** (`#game/<id>`):
  - **Layout (UI02).** Opponent panels (three side by side at four seats), the stack and combat panels (UI05), your battlefield and hand, and a side panel with the card preview, the prompt and the log.
  - **Zone browsers (UI03).** Graveyard, exile and the command zone.
  - **Commander.** Commanders with their zone, tax and commander damage.
  - **Sizes (UI01).** 1920 x 1080 first; 1366 x 768 with smaller cards; one column with the prompt docked at the bottom below 900 px.
- **Prompts (UI04, UI08).** Every typed prompt kind:
  - **Where to answer.** On the board (click to select, click to target, with a menu when a card has several options) and in the prompt panel.
  - **Forms.** Amounts for damage assignment and X; piles for Fact or Fiction.
  - **Unsupported choices** say so and offer to concede.
  - **The keyboard (UI04).** Every option is reachable: arrow keys within a zone, Z to zoom a card, P for the primary option.
- **Card images.**
  - **Where they come from.** By exact name from Scryfall, fetched once by the API, kept on its own disk (`CD_CARD_IMAGE_DIR`) and served from the site, with the printing and artist recorded in `card_images` (migration `0004_card_images`).
  - **Spacing.** Scryfall's API is asked at most once every 120 ms, and one name is fetched once even under concurrent requests.
  - **Safety.** Only Scryfall's image host is ever downloaded from, at most 2 MB of JPEG per image.
  - **The frame.** A text frame from the engine's view always sits underneath, and card state sits beside the image, never on it.
  - **Notices.** The zoom credits the artist and Scryfall, and the footer carries Wizards of the Coast's Fan Content notice.
- **The connection.**
  - **Protocol.** The hello with the CSRF token, resynchronizing by revision, and reconnecting with backoff.
  - **Resends (T14).** A command whose reply was lost is resent with the same action ID once the tab holds the lease again.
  - **Two tabs.** A second tab watches until "Play here".
- **SysOp's inspection panel.** Every hand and library in order, refreshed with each revision, and audited as `game.inspection_opened`.
- **The banner.** The owner's banner leads the Play page, with its logo half on phones. `scripts/brand_assets.py banner` makes the sizes.
- **The live site:**
  - **Production configuration.** `CD_ENVIRONMENT=production` refuses development credentials, plain HTTP, missing proxies, a missing service token or Google client, and a catch-all SysOp network.
  - **The kit (`deploy/`).** The production Compose file; the VM bootstrap (Docker from Docker's repository, generated secrets, a nightly backup timer); `deploy.sh`, which backs up, migrates, restarts and checks; `push.sh`, which deploys from this computer; and the TSH proxy's site file.
  - **Access to the VM.** The web container lets in only the proxy and loopback, and nothing else is published.
  - **CI.** It publishes the images to `ghcr.io/sphinxtah/commander-duels/{api,web,engine}`: every push to `main` as `sha-<commit>` and `main`, and every version tag as its version.
- **Tests:**
  - **Browser journeys** against the real engine (`web/e2e/game.spec.ts`, run by CI's engine job): a Legacy game by mouse and one by keyboard, a four-player Commander table, and the second tab with a lost reply. Every WebSocket frame is checked for concealed cards (T13 in the browser).
  - **Card-image tests:** 11.
  - **A production-settings test.**

## Not included

- Human opponents, lobbies and matchmaking (NET02–NET03); recovery journals (NET08–NET09). A deploy interrupts running games.
- The rest of UI07 (stops, passing to the end of turn, full control); timers; animation, sound, help and gamepads (UI09–UI12).
- Printing-exact images (M1's catalog), drag and drop, and token grouping.
- The AI hidden-information audit (AI02): the bots still read the human's hand, and the table says so.
