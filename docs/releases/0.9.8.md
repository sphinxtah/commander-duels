## The owner's report: the King could not be attacked

"I used Liliana's Contract, and had 4 demons in play, the 2 opposing generals (Liliana and Nico Bolas) died at my upkeep, and the King (Sphinx) lived. During that turn, when trying to attack I couldn't select the Sphinx's planeswalker card or the player, so I couldn't attack."

- **What happened.** The court's guard had fallen with the Generals, and the combat did name The Sphinx and his planeswalker. The choice of whom to attack did not: XMage's `TargetDefender` took its candidates from the attacking player's range of influence, which is set as a turn begins, and at King's Table a King's range ends at the Generals. With two things to attack and neither in range, the choice offered nothing.
- **Who else it reached.** Any King the house rule let a human King attack across range, whenever there was a second thing to attack: since v0.8.7 the faraway King beside the Generals when the rule plays without its guard, or a King with a planeswalker or a battle. With one thing to attack nothing is asked, which hid it.
- **Fixed** in the engine ([patch 0016](../../engine/xmage/patches/README.md)): the choice offers every defender the combat names, whatever the range. Range still holds for spells, abilities and effects ([King's Table](../kings-table.md#attacking)).
- **Tested** by playing the report through a human seat: Liliana's Contract with four Demons, both Generals out at the upkeep, and The Sphinx and his planeswalker both offered in that turn's combat. The test fails on the engine as it was.

## Asked for during the version

### Deck packs

"When buying deck packs at Leng's shop, after revealing the cards, the player should get an option to save as a new deck or if they want to disassemble the deck and put them in to the library as individual cards. If it's a legacy pack, it should offer to create a legacy deck, and if its a commander deck pack, it should ask to build it in to a commander deck."

- **The choice comes after the reveal.** The summary asks "Keep this deck together?", with two buttons. The cards are in the library either way.
- **Legacy deck packs** are the Starter Decks (60 cards) and the Tournament Packs and Tournament Decks (75): 22 products. "Save as a Legacy deck" makes a deck named for the product, with every card the pack gave. It is saved once for each opened pack, and the offer stands whenever the pack is looked at again under "Packs you opened", which also shows the deck a pack was saved as.
- **Commander deck packs** are the ten starter decks. They used to become a deck the moment they were bought. Now the deck's box tears open as a pack does, its hundred cards are revealed with the commander last, and the summary offers "Build it into a Commander deck" or "Keep as individual cards". A player who keeps the cards loose, or closes the page first, can still build the deck on the Decks page, under Starter decks.
- **The two free starter decks** of the setup wizard are built at once, as before: a new account needs decks to play.
- **A random sixty is checked like any deck.** It can hold five copies of a card, or a card Legacy bans; the deck is saved all the same and the deck builder says what it needs.
- Details: [the shop](../shop.md#deck-packs-v098). Migration 0043 adds `decks.opening_id`.

### The tutorial's way into How to play

"When you click the link 'More in how to play: .....' it opens a new tab and sends the player in a loop. Those links should just have a popup box explaining more details on the subject."

- **What happened.** The guided game runs while an account is still in its setup, and the link opened How to play in a new tab. A new tab for an account in its setup opens the welcome again.
- **Now** it is a button that opens the topic in a pop-up over the guided game: the same words, cards and examples as the guide's page, with nothing in it that leads away. "Play on its own" waits while the pop-up is open. Close, Escape or a click beside it goes back to the game where it was.
- Tested in a browser at a desktop's and a phone's size: one tab, the same address, no links.

## The court's memory

Carried from v0.9.7: a bot's search at the court ran the engine out of memory in two of six measured games. The live engine exits when that happens, which interrupts every game it runs.

- **Two causes, both in XMage's AI**, found with a probe that counted the live copies of the game as a court game went: 365, then about 890, 3 GB, and the game died at turn 39.
  - When a tutor or a fetch land resolved in a search, the AI copied the game once for every card it could find in every library in range, before scoring the first.
  - When a trigger had targets to choose, it copied the game for every way to choose them: 870 copies for two targets among thirty permanents.
- **Now** a search tries a search effect's cards one at a time, one for each name, from the searching player's own library, and twelve of a trigger's options ([patch 0017](../../engine/xmage/patches/README.md)). A search that is running also ends when the heap fills, and the bot plays its best line so far; until now the heap was looked at only as a search began.
- **Measured.** The same court game here held at most 38 copies and finished at turn 46. On GitHub's runners six court games finished with no out-of-memory error, their heap peaks 3.0 to 4.5 GB of 10 GB; the live engine has 12 GB. The wards' duels peaked at 0.7 to 1.2 GB of 2 GB and run several times faster: thirty games of a ward took 6 to 21 minutes.
- Details: [fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#a-searchs-memory-v098).

## A court closed or left idle is lost

- Closing the court, or leaving it idle until the engine closes it, now counts as losing it: the player is back at ward 14. Until now a player losing the court could close the game and keep their place.
- **The game says so first.** The table's Close button asks "Close the court and lose it?", and the ward's setup says it.
- **A player alone at the court has an hour** before an idle game closes, as a table has. A duel keeps its quarter hour, and closing or leaving a duel still changes nothing.
- **Not a loss:** a court the turn limit ends, one an engine restart interrupts, and one closed by SysOp or an administrator who is not its player.
- Details: [the campaign](../campaign.md#the-unbound-court-ward-15-v096).

## Bots that keep their boards

Found while tuning the campaign, with the difficulty harness's trace.

- **Daretti lost 59 of his last 60 measured duels** because the bot sacrificed its own artifacts to his commander, one after another, for nothing. Slobad, Goblin Tinkerer reads "Sacrifice an artifact: Target artifact gains indestructible until end of turn"; XMage's AI sacrificed each artifact to make that same artifact indestructible. Two versions of stronger decks had changed nothing.
- **Liliana's bot did the same** with Yawgmoth, Thran Physician: it sacrificed Phyrexian Obliterator, then Archfiend of Depravity, for a -1/-1 counter and a card each.
- **Now a bot's search leaves such abilities alone**: any ability paid for by sacrificing another permanent. Everything else a card does stays, including a permanent that sacrifices itself (Mind Stone, Evolving Wilds), mana paid for by a sacrifice, and spells with a sacrifice among their costs.
- **What it costs.** Bots at tables and in practice no longer use Viscera Seer, Goblin Bombardment or the Thallids' Saproling abilities. Three starter decks hold such cards (black-red, white-black, black-green); in a bot's hands they play as creatures and tokens.
- Details: [fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#sacrifice-outlets-left-alone-v098).

## Small things

From v0.9.7's hunt, carried:

- **One way to write a count.** Every quest and goal writes a count below ten in words and one from ten in digits: "Win three Commander games", "Win 12 games". The weekly goals from the games wrote digits and the others words.
- **A table goal says how it is done alone.** "Finish a game at a table" shows a hint until it is done: "Alone? Open a King's Table table and give every other seat to a bot." Every other table needs two players' seats.
- **The court's host stays King A.** The host could take a General's seat and leave the King's to a bot. The lobby no longer offers the moves the server refuses at the court.
- **A warden's deck, once opened,** says "Back to the campaign" where it said "Back to the shop", and "Opening a deck" where it said "Opening a pack".
- **The friends box is as it was.** Nothing in it was broken. It floats over the page's lower right corner, so at any window size something scrolls under it, and every page keeps room at its end to scroll clear of it (checked on the main menu and the Quests page at ten window sizes). ## Not in this version: the third batch of stand-ins

None of the three could be made to play as printed here, and the owner was told during the work.

- **The text changers** (Sleight of Mind, Magical Hack, Alter Reality, Mind Bend, Crystal Spray, Whim of Volrath, Artificial Evolution, Trait Doctoring, Spectral Shift, Glamerdye, Swirl the Mists, Balduvian Shaman). XMage's rules for a card are Java code, not text, so there is nothing for "replace red with blue" to rewrite in general. XMage has no text-changing layer of its own; its one creature-type changer only changes the creature's type line. The owner's decision: a best-effort changer that says when it cannot change a card, or stand-ins for good.
- **Spy Kit** is exact only if every name comparison in the engine knows a creature can have many names: about 120 places in XMage's card code.
- **Cryptic Spires** can be exact, and it is the one that comes in ordinary packs. It needs a colour pair chosen for each copy and stored with the deck, which touches the deck builder, validation, import and export, and the engine. It is proposed for v0.9.9's deck workshop foundation.
