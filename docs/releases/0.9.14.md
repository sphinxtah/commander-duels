# v0.9.14

Three things the owner met in one four-player Commander game on the live site on 2026-11-14, the day v0.9.13 was released. v0.9.13 was not deployed; this version holds all of it ([its notes](0.9.13.md)), and its deploy notes apply here.

## The bots answer within a round

Late in that game every action waited for the three bots: 20 seconds each from turn 18, and at turn 27 "a full minute for me to cast a spell". With the bots made passive the wait was gone, which named the cause: their searches.

A bot may think twelve seconds for a decision at skill 4, the table's default (15 at skill 5, 18 at 6), and every priority it gets starts a search. Early on a search ends in milliseconds; on a late board each runs to its limit, and three bots answer one spell in turn.

A bot now has its whole thinking time only for its own turn with nothing on the stack. Any response, to a spell or an ability, or a priority in another player's turn, has its share of one six-second round divided among the table's bots: two seconds each with three bots ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#a-round-of-responses-v0914)).

- A search that finishes inside its share is unchanged; only one that would have run on is cut off, and it plays its best line so far.
- A game of two players keeps the whole time for everything, and so does a table's only bot, so duels, the campaign's among them, play and are measured as they were.
- **The Unbound Court** has five bots, which now share a round. Its difficulty was measured before this and has not been measured again.
- The operator's numbers count such searches as `response_searches`.

## The library is a button while a card shows its top

The owner: "make the library (deck) itself clickable during the time the card allows ... it should pop up a box just like when opening the graveyard."

While a card lets a player look at the top of their library (Bolas's Citadel, Mystic Forge) or has them play with it revealed (Future Sight), the library's pile is ringed in gold and is a button. It opens the same browser as a graveyard does, on what is shown: "Your library: 1 card shown, of 63". A card the prompt offers to play or cast answers there as it does on the table. The prompt column no longer lists such a card, except while a choice other than priority is asked of it, so that the choice cannot be missed ([the game screen](../game-screen.md)).

## Bolas's Citadel: the top card can be cast

The owner: "when seeing my top card of my library with Bola's Citadel, I can't even cast it."

The engine offered the cast all along. What was missing was the card: XMage lists a looked-at card only for the moment it tells its client and clears the list as it asks for priority, so the table had the card for an instant and there was nothing to click. The engine's seat view now shows a library's top for as long as the card that shows it holds, and says whose library it is (`library_seat` on a group of revealed or looked-at cards). The engine's test plays it: with Bolas's Citadel on the battlefield the top card is shown at every priority, offered to cast, and cast for its mana value in life (`LibraryTopTest`).

## Upgrading

```
cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh
```

- **From v0.9.12 this runs v0.9.13's migration too.** `0050_economy_removed` drops a table and a column that v0.9.12's API reads: deploy when nobody is playing, and going back to v0.9.12 needs the dump taken before it ([v0.9.13's notes](0.9.13.md#upgrading)). v0.9.14 adds no migration of its own.
- **The engine changed**, and with it the private engine image. Games running at the upgrade end interrupted.
- **Nothing in `.env` changes.**
