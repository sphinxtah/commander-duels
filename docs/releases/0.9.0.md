# v0.9.0: sleeves everywhere, restoring sleeves, Weathered Sentinels, auctions and land packs, Generals who fight

Opened and closed on 2026-11-07. The next version is v0.9.1 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.1.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-11-07, started with a bug and then took five items, choosing the recommended answer to every start question ([the plan](../plans/0.9.0.md#scope-agreed-for-this-version-2026-11-07)):

- **The bug:** "I chose one of the default deck sleeves, and my deck is still showing the default MTG card back design. Every time that deck is being used it should always show every card with the sleeve design on the back."
- **A campaign back in a deck's sleeve, checked on every read**, and **restoring a removed sleeve** (both left by v0.8.9).
- **Weathered Sentinels**, fixed in every format.
- **The v0.6.2 to v0.6.4 notes:** unlimited auctions and the basic land packs' views changed; the v0.6.2 choices kept.
- **King's Table's stall:** the bot Generals never attacked one another, so with the house rule off the Kings could never attack. (The owner's first report, a King who could not choose an attacker, was that rule working as written: the owner had turned the house rule off.)

## Fixed

- **A deck's sleeve on its library.** The table's canvas drew the library pile, and the pile of backs at a long hand's end, with the viewer's own back instead of the owner's sleeve; face-down cards already wore it. Both now wear each owner's sleeve. The plain table without WebGL (left by v0.8.9) does the same: its face-down cards, library piles and magnified backs wear their owner's sleeve ([card sleeves](../sleeves.md)).
- **Weathered Sentinels** (patch 0012, every format). At the pin its limit fell on every creature of its controller and read its watcher the wrong way round, so in a first combat it limited nothing: once any opponent had attacked you, the Sentinels could attack every player. Now the Sentinels alone may attack, as though it had no defender, only a player who attacked you during their last turn; the hint names those players. A seat choosing where an attacker goes is offered only the defenders it can attack, for every restriction, so with one choice none is asked ([the patches](../../engine/xmage/patches/README.md), `WeatheredSentinelsTest`: a Legacy duel either way, and a Commander game of three where the Sentinels may attack only the player who attacked).

## Changed

- **Campaign backs on every read.** A deck wearing a campaign back its owner no longer has (after a campaign reset), and an account's chosen back, show the default at the table; the deck keeps its choice and wears the back again once it is earned. The deck editor says so (`seat_sleeves` takes the campaign; `picture_of` checks the earned backs).
- **Restoring a removed sleeve.** Administration › Reports shows **Restore to every table** on a removed sleeve (`POST /api/v1/admin/sleeves/{id}/restore`, `reports.manage`, audited as `sleeves.restored`). A removal no longer clears the decks that wore the sleeve: they show the default while it is removed and wear it again when it is restored. A restore may pass the owner's 20 sleeves; a sleeve its owner deleted since stays gone (`409 sleeve_deleted`). Decks from removals made before v0.9.0 were cleared then and need choosing again.
- **Unlimited auctions** ([auctions](../auctions.md)). The seller changes an unlimited listing's Buy Now price in place, up or down, from its panel (`PUT /api/v1/auctions/{id}/buy-now`, audited as `auction.repriced`). A buyer's Buy Now names the price they saw, and a price changed since buys nothing (`409 price_changed`): the panel shows the new price. A player may have at most 50 unlimited listings up at once (`409 too_many_unlimited`).
- **Basic land packs' summary** ([shop](../shop.md)). Opened, a land pack sums up by land and edition: each land in WUBRG order with its count and foils, its editions newest first, and the five oldest pulls; "Every card" shows the usual pictures. Pulled cards carry `set_name` and `released_at`.
- **King's Table's bot Generals press the attack.** After XMage's AI chooses, a General sends each creature it kept home at the opposing General beside it with the least life, unless that player has an untapped creature that could block it, kill it and live. Kings, and bots in every other format, play as before (`FairBot.pressAsGeneral`, `KingsTablePressTest`).
