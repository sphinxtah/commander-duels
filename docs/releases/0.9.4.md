# v0.9.4: toward no stand-ins, the live lobby

Opened and closed on 2026-11-10. The next version is v0.9.5 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.5.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner opened v0.9.4 asking how stand-ins work and whether the game can have none, then set the rule and left the decision to Claude: "I'm a purist. I want the experience of opening a pack to be genuine and as close to realistic as possible… the players want the cards to play the game, so the goal is purity and fun." The decision ([the catalog](../catalog.md#toward-no-stand-ins-v094)): packs stay genuine, every card a pack can deal should play as printed, and stand-ins stay only as the casual fallback for a card not yet taught to the engine.

The start questions settled the rest:
- **Stand-ins:** upgrade XMage, teach the engine Conspiracy's draft-matters cards and a first batch of classic cards (the text-changing cards later).
- **Leftovers:** the testers' reports, the campaign's difficulty with wider samples, the finale's edge cases and a duel's restoration after a reconnect, and a live lobby.
- **Ante** (Darkpact, Contract from Below, Demonic Attorney and the rest) stays a stand-in for now.
- Nothing new besides. During the work the owner asked for daily and weekly quest boards in v0.9.5 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.5.md#the-owners-request-quest-boards-2026-11-10-noted-during-v094)).

## Added

- **XMage 1.4.63** (from 1.4.62). About 280 more Commander-legal cards play as written: Star Trek's before its packs arrive (2026-11-13), Marvel's, Mystery Booster Commander's and Reality Fracture's among them. The twelve patches were rebased; three needed a hand ([the patches](../../engine/xmage/patches/README.md)).
- **Pack cards the engine learned** (patches 0013 and 0014):
  - Conspiracy's draft-matters cards, played as printed outside a draft. There is no draft here, so nothing is noted, and by the cards' rulings their other abilities do nothing (Lurking Automaton enters with no counters, Garbage Fire deals 0, Regicide has no legal target, Paliano adds no mana). The draft text still shows. Eighteen cards, with Grenzo's Rebuttal played in full.
  - Undergrowth and Taste of Paradise (Alliances), Chromatic Armor and Barbarian Guides (Ice Age), and Whippoorwill (The Dark).
- **Cards found under XMage's spelling:** the catalog no longer misses a card for its punctuation, so Ratonhnhaké꞉ton, "Set Phasers to . . .", "I'm a Doctor, Not a . . ." and "With Great Power . . ." play as written once the engine is deployed.
- **The live lobby.** Find a game updates by itself: a change to a table, its seats or its invitations tells every open tab over the chat's WebSocket, and the page asks for its cards again at once ([human tables](../tables.md#the-live-lobby-v094)). The page polls only every 30 seconds while that socket is up.
- **The campaign's difficulty on GitHub's runners**: `.github/workflows/campaign-difficulty.yml` plays every ward at once, one job a ward, and writes the table into the run's summary ([the campaign](../campaign.md#difficulty-on-githubs-runners-v094)).

## Changed

- **An interrupted game's screen leads back.** A game the engine lost to a restart says so, as before, and now has the way back; a campaign duel adds that it counts for nothing and can be started again.

## Looked at, not built

- **A duel's restoration after an engine restart.** XMage keeps a game in memory with its players' threads; restoring one needs a state snapshot a new process can resume, or a replay of the game from its seed and every answer, with the bots' time-dependent choices recorded too. Either is a version of its own ([the v0.9.5 plan](../plans/0.9.5.md#the-campaign)).
- **The testers' reports**: Administration › Reports on the live site was empty.
