# v0.4.4: chat between friends and at the table

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-22. It brings chat: friends write to each other, and the players at a human table talk to the whole table, send emotes, make alliances and write to their allies alone. It also fixes card pictures that did not load when many came at once, which the owner reported during the version. This follows [the v0.4.4 plan](../plans/0.4.4.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-22 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.4.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v044)):
- Allies at a free-for-all table are agreed during the game: offered, accepted, ended by either.
- Friends' chat lives on a Messages page and in small docked windows; new messages count on the bell and pop up beside it.
- Administrators read a message only when a player reports it, with the 20 before it.
- Quick emotes at the table; no mute, word filter, or typing and seen marks.
- Stated defaults the owner kept: friends only, live delivery, SysOp reads every chat (audited), no chat with bots or in practice games, players out of the game still chat, messages kept a year (friends) or 30 days (tables).

## Changed

- **Messages between friends** (the owner's request). **Message** on a friend's profile or on the Friends page opens a small chat window at the bottom right, which stays open from page to page (up to three; on a phone, one). The new **Messages** page in the navigation, with the count of unread messages, lists every conversation, the latest first, and shows one beside the list with its history. Messages arrive at once over a connection the app keeps open; one that cannot be sent says why and offers **Try again**, and lands once however often it is sent. A conversation shown in a tab in use is read, in every tab. New messages from a friend are one notification on the bell while unread ("Ada sent you 3 messages."), and each pops up beside the bell for a moment unless its conversation is open. Only friends write to each other: an ended friendship or a block stops new messages, and what was written stays readable ([chat](../chat.md#friends-messages)).
- **The table's chat** (the owner's request). At a human table's game, the players talk to the whole table, in text or with nine preset emotes, and a player out of the game still does. A player offers an alliance from another player's portrait; it starts when they accept, everyone sees it, and either may end it. Allies may then write to each other alone, and no other player's connection ever receives those lines; the API tests read every frame to make sure. Practice games have no chat ([chat](../chat.md#the-tables-chat)).
- **Reporting a message.** Every message from another player has **Report** in its menu. The report keeps a copy of it and of the 20 messages before it that the reporter saw; Administration → Reports marks it "Message from …" and shows the copy, the reported message highlighted. Administrators read chat only so ([reports](../reports.md#reporting-a-chat-message-v044)).
- **SysOp's reader.** Administration → **Chats**, for SysOp alone (`chat.read_all`): the latest conversations and table chats, or one player's; opening one is recorded in the audit trail ([chat](../chat.md#sysops-reader)).
- **Limits and rules.** At most 500 characters and 13 lines; no control characters; invisible direction overrides dropped; 5 messages in 5 seconds and 30 a minute, friends and tables together. Every message shows as plain text ([chat](../chat.md#rules-for-the-text)).
- **Privacy policy and terms**, effective 2026-10-22: what is kept of messages and for how long, who reads them (friends, the table, allies; administrators only when reported; the operator any, audited), and what may not be written.
- **Deleting an account** also removes its conversations and its lines in tables' chats; the worker's daily job deletes friends' messages after a year and tables' chats after 30 days.
- **Card pictures that did not load** (the owner's report: a new pack drew 8 of its 14 pictures, and the Cards list missed some too). Every download from Scryfall's image host took a share of the per-account queue meant for Scryfall's rate-limited name search, 8 an account, so the 9th new card onwards was refused at once; and the Cards page, the pack opening and the starter decks' pictures never asked again, unlike the table's. Downloads now wait their turn (4 at once an account, 16 in all), and every card picture tries again with backoff ([the game screen](../game-screen.md#cards)).

Migration `0017_chat` only adds tables and columns (and the notification kind `message`), so the previous version runs on it.

## Deploying 0.4.4

In this order:
1. **The private engine image.** The engine did not change, but `deploy.sh` pulls the private image at the version's tag: the private repository's CI builds `ghcr.io/sphinxtah/commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.4.4` once the tag's run has published the `0.4.4` images.
2. **Deploy:** `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Nothing to change in `.env` or on the TSH proxy: the chat's WebSocket is under `/api/`, whose upgrades both proxies already pass.
3. **Afterwards:** Messages in the navigation; Message on a friend's profile; the chat panel at a human table; Chats in Administration for SysOp.
