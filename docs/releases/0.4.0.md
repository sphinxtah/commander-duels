# v0.4.0: tokens, human tables and Leng's card shop

A minor version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-21 (tag `v0.4.0`). Wins earn tokens, players can sit down together at human tables, and tokens buy booster packs in Leng's card shop, from Revised to the current set, with real pull ratios. This follows [the v0.4.0 plan](../plans/0.4.0.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-21 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.0.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v040)):
- Tokens for wins: 5 to 20 per defeated bot by its skill, and 50 to 500 per defeated human on a smooth curve by their wins; a welcome grant of 1,000 and back pay for past wins.
- Human tables in this version: a lobby and invite links, Legacy and Commander, bots filling empty seats, a vote to remove an absent player, and a daily limit of 3 paid wins over the same opponent.
- Leng's card shop: every booster type from Revised to the current set, Unlimited, Arabian Nights and Antiquities included, priced from market prices; Alpha and Beta stay for tournaments.

## Changed

- **Tokens for wins** ([tokens](../tokens.md)). Every win pays its winner for each seat beaten: 5 to 20 tokens per bot by its skill, and 50 to 500 per player on a curve by that player's wins. Tokens are paid once, in the same transaction that records the result, in an append-only ledger; wins over the same player pay for the first 3 a day, and SysOp may cap the tokens bots pay in a day (off by default). Every account starts with 1,000 tokens, and every win before v0.4.0 was paid back. The profile shows the balance and the latest entries; SysOp gives or takes back tokens on any account, with a reason, audited.
- **Human tables.** Players sit down with each other ([human tables](../tables.md)). A Tables page after Play lists open tables; a player opens one (Legacy for two, Commander for two to four, bots in the seats left over, public or by invite link only), others join with a deck of their own and get ready, and the host starts one game with a seat for each player. Each seat has its own token, views, lease and concession, and others see a player's handle and portrait, never their deck. There are no timers: a player away for 2 minutes may be voted out by a majority of the others still in the game, and the game goes on. Wins over players count and pay tokens on the curve, and the result screen shows what a game paid. A concession that leaves one player now ends the game at once (the engine wakes that player for XMage). Migration `0009_tables`.
- **Long choices at the table** (the owner's report, 2026-10-21). Three Tree City stopped the game: its creature type is one of about 300, and the table offered only choices of up to 64 entries, so the request was "unsupported". Choices of up to 1,000 entries are now listed, with a filter box for long lists, and naming a card (Pithing Needle, Cabal Therapy, tens of thousands of names) is typed, with names suggested from the catalog and the cards the player can see ([prompts](../engine/commander-tables.md#prompts)).

- **The Godlike tier's custom cards are playable** (the owner's request, 2026-10-21). Accounts at the Godlike tier put them in their decks from the deck builder, where they show with their images, and play them in every game, practice games and human tables alike; bots never do. They are legal in Legacy and Commander, may lead a Commander deck by the usual rule, and follow the library's copies. A game holding one starts only where the running engine has loaded it: the owner's private engine image, which adds them as a plugin; the public engine says "Not loaded by this engine". At the table every seat sees them with their images; everywhere else they stay hidden, from shared decks and search to image routes. An account SysOp lowers below Godlike keeps its decks, collection-locked, showing only a hidden "Godlike card". The public engine image puts `/app/plugins` on its classpath for such plugins ([progression](../progression.md#playing-them-v040), [decks](../decks.md#the-godlike-tiers-custom-cards-v040)).
- **Leng's card shop** ([the shop](../shop.md)). A new Shop page sells booster packs for tokens: 392 products from Unlimited to the current set, every booster type MTGJSON describes, grouped by era with filters by year and type, a search by set name or code, and sorting by date or price. Prices follow each pack's market price on a curve that makes a current pack 500 tokens and a Revised pack 150,000; Alpha and Beta are not sold. Packs follow their set's real booster: a weekly worker job reads MTGJSON's booster data (MIT licence), resolves every card to the catalog, and keeps a versioned collation per product; a product with cards the catalog lacks is not sold until it can be. Buying spends tokens once per request, and opening draws the pack on the server with secure randomness, records it, and puts its cards in the library for good. Packs open at Leng's counter in the Magic Duels style: the pack tears open, the cards turn over one by one (click or Space), rares and mythic rares glow, foils shimmer, with generated sounds, a "Reveal all" and a summary with new cards marked; reduced motion makes every step immediate. Administration's new Card shop tab lists the prices, lets SysOp and administrators change a price or take a product off sale (each with a reason, audited), shows the booster data job's runs with "run now", and lets SysOp give packs to any account. Leng is placeholder art until the owner's.

## Deploying 0.4.0

- **Migration 0011** lets a deck version's entry be a custom card. It only adds a nullable column and a check, so the previous version keeps working while it runs.
- **The private engine image, once** ([deployment](../deployment.md#the-engine-with-the-custom-cards-v040)). For the custom cards to play on the live site, before deploying v0.4.0 on the VM:
  1. If Docker on the VM is not logged in to `ghcr.io` any more, log it in with a classic personal access token with only `read:packages`, typed at the prompt: `docker login ghcr.io -u sphinxtah`.
  2. Add `CD_ENGINE_IMAGE=ghcr.io/sphinxtah/commander-duels-engine-sphinx` to `/srv/commander-duels/.env`.
  3. Make sure the private repository's CI has published that image with the tag being deployed (`0.4.0`). `deploy.sh` pulls it with the others and stops, replacing nothing, if it cannot.

  Without these steps v0.4.0 runs the public engine, and the custom cards say "Not loaded by this engine".
- **Leng's card shop** needs no `.env` change. Migration 0010 adds its tables. The worker's first booster data run starts on its own once the worker is up (it waits for the catalog's first import on a new installation), or at once from Administration's Card shop tab ("Run the booster data job now"). It downloads 241 MTGJSON set files and the set list (94 MB) into the catalog volume, one at a time, and on a copy of the development database it took 2 minutes 31 seconds and resolved all 392 products against the full catalog; the shop sells nothing until it has run. Later runs are weekly and fetch only files that changed: a second run there downloaded nothing (all 242 files "not modified") and made no new collation, in 2 minutes 47 seconds, most of it the half-second pause between requests.
