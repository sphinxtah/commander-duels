# v0.4.0: tokens, human tables and Leng's card shop

A minor version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-21. Wins earn tokens, players can sit down together at human tables, and tokens buy booster packs in Leng's card shop, from Revised to the current set, with real pull ratios. This follows [the v0.4.0 plan](../plans/0.4.0.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-21 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.0.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v040)):
- Tokens for wins: 5 to 20 per defeated bot by its skill, and 50 to 500 per defeated human on a smooth curve by their wins; a welcome grant of 1,000 and back pay for past wins.
- Human tables in this version: a lobby and invite links, Legacy and Commander, bots filling empty seats, a vote to remove an absent player, and a daily limit of 3 paid wins over the same opponent.
- Leng's card shop: every booster type from Revised to the current set, Unlimited, Arabian Nights and Antiquities included, priced from market prices; Alpha and Beta stay for tournaments.

## Changed

- **Human tables.** Players sit down with each other ([human tables](../tables.md)). A Tables page after Play lists open tables; a player opens one (Legacy for two, Commander for two to four, bots in the seats left over, public or by invite link only), others join with a deck of their own and get ready, and the host starts one game with a seat for each player. Each seat has its own token, views, lease and concession, and others see a player's handle and portrait, never their deck. There are no timers: a player away for 2 minutes may be voted out by a majority of the others still in the game, and the game goes on. Wins over players count and pay tokens on the curve, and the result screen shows what a game paid. A concession that leaves one player now ends the game at once (the engine wakes that player for XMage). Migration `0009_tables`.
- **Long choices at the table** (the owner's report, 2026-10-21). Three Tree City stopped the game: its creature type is one of about 300, and the table offered only choices of up to 64 entries, so the request was "unsupported". Choices of up to 1,000 entries are now listed, with a filter box for long lists, and naming a card (Pithing Needle, Cabal Therapy, tens of thousands of names) is typed, with names suggested from the catalog and the cards the player can see ([prompts](../engine/commander-tables.md#prompts)).
