# v0.5.0: quests, queues, best of three and Two-Headed Giant

A minor version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-22 and closed on 2026-10-23 (tag `v0.5.0`). Players get daily quests, achievements and a record; public queues find them a game without an invite, and a rematch plays it again; Legacy is played best of three with sideboards; Two-Headed Giant arrives as an early practice game on a patched XMage; and a player's library holds its cards by edition. This follows [the v0.5.0 plan](../plans/0.5.0.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-22 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.5.0.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v050)). The owner chose all four candidates:
- Daily quests from results, achievements as badges that pay once, totals on every profile and a history for its player and SysOp.
- Public queues for Legacy and for Commander of two, three or four, with bots after a minute unless a player waits for players only; casual only; rematches.
- Best-of-three Legacy with sideboards, every game won counting and paying as a game does.
- Two-Headed Giant: the spike on a patched XMage, and a practice game against bots if it held.
- Fixed with it: the top bar's tokens after buying a pack; the library shows its cards, with a list a switch away.
- Added during the version: the library by edition, and decks locked to the editions a player owns.

## Added

- **Daily quests** (PROG04). Three a day from a pool of 16, drawn at the day's first look (Arizona time), each paying 100–250 tokens once, from the server's own record of each game: win a Commander game, win with a red deck, beat a skill-4 bot, win against another player. One swap a day. They stand on the main menu, under its tiles, and the bell says when one is done ([quests and records](../quests.md)).
- **Achievements** (PROG07). 29 badges, each paying its tokens once (6,450 in all); a player pins up to three to show on their profile. A player's history from before v0.5.0 earns what it already deserves at its first check. The original Magic Duels achievements are not inventoried, so none is mapped; the Commander and Legacy ones are marked as additions.
- **A record on every profile** (PROG08). Games, wins, losses, draws and games with no result, in all, by format, against bots or players, and best-of-three matches once each; the share won; the pinned badges. Anyone who may open the profile sees them. The game-by-game history (date, format, opponents, own deck and revision, result, turns, length) is its player's and SysOp's alone.
- **Public queues** (NET01). Find a game, first on the Play page: Legacy, one game or best of three, and Commander for two, three or four, each its own queue. Players are seated in the order they joined; after a minute with too few, bots take the empty seats unless a player chose players only. Two players or more meet at a table's game, with its chat, alliances, away votes and tokens; a player alone with bots plays a practice game. The app follows into the game from any page ([queues](../queues.md)).
- **Rematches** (NET01, AI06). A practice game plays again at once with the same request; a table's or a queue's game offers its players the same seats and decks, and starts when everyone still there accepts within two minutes. A best-of-three match's rematch is a new match, offered once the match is over.
- **Best-of-three Legacy** (FMT05–FMT07). At Legacy tables, at the practice table and in its own queue. Between games each player sideboards in private, then the loser chooses to play or draw; the engine starts the seat chosen. Every game won counts and pays as a game does; the match is recorded for the record. "Concede the game" and "Concede the match" are separate; a match is capped at five games. A Legacy deck's sideboard goes to the engine, where wishes find it ([matches](../matches.md)).
- **Two-Headed Giant, early** (FMT08–FMT10, T09). The spike held: three small XMage patches give shared team turns, a shared life of 30, team poison at 15 and team results, and leave Legacy and Commander as they were. The practice table plays the player and a bot teammate against two bots, with Legacy decks that share no card but basic lands (Magic Tournament Rules 8.4 and 9.5); the teammate's hand is shown to its player (rule 810.5). It is marked "Early": the cards known to misbehave are listed in [the spike's report](../engine/two-headed-giant.md), with the revised estimate for the rest (four humans, team chat, the full card audit).

## Changed

- **The library by edition** (the owner's request during the version). A player's library now holds its cards by edition, and a deck may hold only the editions its player owns, copy by copy (the owner's choice) ([the library by edition](../progression.md#the-library-by-edition-v050)).
  - **Grants by edition.** A pack grants each card in the edition pulled; a starter deck in the editions it holds; the alliance's playset in each card's default edition. Basic lands stay unlimited in every edition, and administrators and SysOp build from every edition. Migration 0025 gives every earlier grant its edition: packs from the cards opened, starter decks from their lists, earlier decks' cards from the deck that granted them, otherwise the card's default edition.
  - **My library** lists each card once per edition, with that edition's picture and name ("Revised Edition (3ED #150)"), and its list view an Edition column.
  - **A card's details** opened from a pack show only that card's edition; from My library, only the editions owned; from All cards, every printing, each marked with the copies owned (the owner's report, with Goblin King's 27 printings).
  - **Decks.** Each edition's copies count against the copies of that edition; the copy limits per card still count every edition together. A deck holding an edition its player does not own is collection-locked, with the remedy ("You own Goblin King from Revised Edition: switch this copy to it"). Saving a draft gives copies that name no edition an owned one; the deck editor offers only owned editions, adding a card picks one, imports and guided creation take owned editions.
  - **Existing decks switched once, at deploy.** The worker moves every library player's draft copies to editions they own where the library allows, and notes on the deck what it switched ("Editions switched to the ones you own"); what it cannot match leaves the deck collection-locked until fixed. Versions games played are never changed ([decks](../decks.md#editions-switched-at-deploy-v050)).
  - **A pack's card is new** when its edition is new to the library.

## Fixed

- **The top bar's tokens** stayed at the old total after a pack or a starter deck was bought, until the page changed (the owner's report). A page that changes the balance now tells the top bar to ask again.
- **The library shows its cards** (the owner's request): My library shows the cards' pictures, each with its copies and where they came from, and a Pictures/List switch, the same on every Cards tab and remembered in the browser, turns any tab into a list ([the card catalog](../catalog.md)).

## Decisions taken while building

Taken by the subagents and me while building, for the owner to revisit ([the v0.5.1 plan](../plans/0.5.1.md#carried-over-from-v050)):
- **Quests** pay at once, with no claim button; every quest is about wins, so none can be farmed by losing; the day's three are drawn at its first look, and wins earlier that day count. Quest and achievement tokens ignore SysOp's daily cap on bots.
- **Achievements:** the 29 and their rewards are ours; SysOp's set wins earn none; a friend made counts at the next check, not at once.
- **Statistics:** a concession, an elimination or a vote-out is a loss; the history is not audited; it starts again after a reset, for SysOp too; the teammate in Two-Headed Giant is listed among the opponents.
- **Queues:** bots play at skill 4 with a turn limit of 60; a player alone with bots plays a practice game; after the minute every waiting player who takes bots is seated together; a place whose app has not looked for 150 seconds is dropped; queue and table exclude each other; joining and leaving are not audited.
- **Rematches:** a table's offer lasts two minutes; removed players are not asked and bots take their seats; leaving the result screen declines.
- **Best of three:** a draw or a game with no result adds a game, up to five; a closed game closes the match; sideboarding lasts at most five minutes; the loser chooses play or draw, a bot always plays first; only main deck and sideboard swap; "Concede the match" on the Play page acts without a confirmation; nobody can be voted out between games. A waiting next game asks for a slot every 5 seconds (30 while the engine does not answer).
- **Two-Headed Giant:** teammates' decks share no card but basic lands (the Magic Tournament Rules' current text; Wizards' format page still says four copies between teammates); Erayo is allowed at casual rules; a win pays for both opposing bots; the bots' decks are dealt in a fixed order and red and green beat white and blue in 25 of 26 measured games; the patches are not offered upstream yet; queues and tables do not offer it.
- **The library by edition:** copies naming no edition take the owned edition with the most copies; an unowned edition is switched only at deploy; the maybeboard keeps what it names; the deploy note lists only copies moved from a named edition.

## Deploying 0.5.0

The owner deploys as usual, once the tag's run has published `0.5.0` and the private engine image `0.5.0` exists: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Nothing in `.env` changes.
- **The API, the engine and the worker go together.** The API sends the engine a best-of-three game's starting seat and a Legacy deck's sideboard, which an older engine refuses; migration `0025_library_editions` is not expand-only (an older API cannot write a grant without its edition). `upgrade.sh` deploys them together.
- **Migrations 0021 to 0025** run on the way up: quests and results (0021 enters every past game's results, paying nothing), queues and rematches (0022), matches (0023), Two-Headed Giant's format (0024) and the library by edition (0025, which splits earlier grants by edition).
- **At the worker's first start** every library player's decks are switched to owned editions, once (`decks.editions_switched`). A deck it cannot match shows as collection-locked, with what to switch.
- **Achievements** a player's history already deserves are paid at their first look at Profile or their next win, so tokens appear then, not at the deploy.
- **The privacy page** says profiles now show totals and badges.
