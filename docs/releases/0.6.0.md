# v0.6.0: Tivit's Auction House

A minor version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-23 and closed on 2026-10-24 (tag `v0.6.0`). It adds Tivit's Auction House, a market where players auction cards to each other as in Madden Ultimate Team, and puts the official Magic card back on every card shown face down. This follows [the v0.6.0 plan](../plans/0.6.0.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-23 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.6.0.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v060)):
- Tivit's Auction House: cards only, never an alliance's or an unopened pack; a starting bid, an optional Buy Now and a length the seller chooses; no fee; each edition and finish its own item; Madden's screens (the market's search, transfer targets, the transfer list, comparing prices); a menu tile and a banner from the owner's painting.
- Libraries record finishes (nonfoil, foil, etched) per edition.
- The official Magic card back wherever a card's back shows.
- Added during the version ([the plan](../plans/0.6.0.md#added-during-v060)): the main menu's greeting opens the profile; basic lands are owned by exact print, auctioned, and needed by decks like any card; foil and etched cards glow outside games.

## Added

- **Tivit's Auction House** (the owner's request), on a main-menu tile and page of its own under the owner's painting of Tivit at the auctioneer's desk, laid out as Madden Ultimate Team's transfer market in the app's colours ([the auction house](../auctions.md)):
  - **Search the market:** filters by name, set, rarity, finish, colour, type, bid and Buy Now prices, Buy Now only and time left, with sorting and pages; card tiles with live countdowns, Winning and Outbid badges and a watch star.
  - **The item panel:** the bid stepper in 5% steps, Make bid, Buy Now (confirmed with the price), Watch, Compare price (the same edition and finish's live listings and recent sales) and Card details.
  - **The transfer list** (my listings: active, sold, unsold; withdraw before the first bid, relist, clear) and **the transfer targets** (watched and bid on: active, won, lost).
  - **Selling:** any card the library holds, in its exact edition and finish, from My library's Sell or the page's Sell dialog: a starting bid, an optional Buy Now, a length of 1 hour to 3 days, and up to 20 copies at once. Never an alliance's card, the one of a kind, a custom card or an unopened pack.
  - **The rules:** no fee, the seller paid all the buyer pays; a bid is held from the bidder's tokens and returned when outbid; a bid in the last 30 seconds adds 30 seconds; a listed card leaves the library until it sells or comes back unsold. Auctions settle on time, through the worker and on every read.
  - **The bell** says outbid, won, sold, unsold and cancelled. **Administration** has an Auctions tab: the log of every auction and sale for SysOp and administrators given `auctions.manage`, and SysOp's Cancel, with a reason, audited.
- **Libraries by finish:** each edition's copies are counted as nonfoil, foil and etched, filled in for past packs from what their openings pulled (migration `0028_auction_house`); decks still ignore the finish ([the library by finish](../progression.md#the-library-by-finish-and-basic-lands-v060)).
- **The official Magic card back** (the owner's) on every card shown face down: tearing packs at Leng's counter, and at the game table in both painters ([the game screen](../game-screen.md)).
- **The foil glow:** every card picture whose copy is foil or etched glows, outside games: My library, card details, the auction house and pack openings.
- **The main menu's greeting** opens the profile.

## Changed

- **Basic lands are cards like any other** (the owner's decision): packs and starter decks grant them in the printing and finish dealt; decks may hold only the basics the library holds, print by print; they can be auctioned. Migration 0028 grants every player the basics their past packs pulled and their starter decks held; **a deck holding more basics than that** (one built before v0.6.0 from free basics, or a grandfathered v0.3.1 deck) **is collection-locked** until it is fixed, as any deck short of a card is.
- **The token ledger** has three more kinds: `bid`, `bid_return` and `sale`; the tokens panel sums what the auction house gave or took.

## Decisions taken while building

The owner may revisit these ([the v0.6.1 plan](../plans/0.6.1.md#from-v060)): basic lands granted at deploy only from past packs and starter decks, so decks built before from free basics are collection-locked until fixed; an account reset or deleted while leading another's auction leaves it running with no leader; SysOp lists from its unlimited library and alone cancels, administrators with `auctions.manage` read the log; Buy Now in Tivit's purple.

## Deploying 0.6.0

No `.env` change. Once the tag's run has published the `0.6.0` images and the private engine image `0.6.0` is built, on the VM: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Migration `0028_auction_house` runs at start: it splits past packs' copies by finish and grants every player the basic lands of their past packs and starter decks. Afterwards: the main menu shows Tivit's Auction House; a card listed by one account appears on another's market; Administration → Auctions lists it.
