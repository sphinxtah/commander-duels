# v0.5.1: fixes and Two-Headed Giant for everyone

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-23. It fixes what the owner found in v0.5.0 and brings the rest of Two-Headed Giant: human tables with teams, a queue, team chat and the teammate's hand, and the card audit. During the version the owner added a chat bar in place of the Messages page, paintings behind the main menu's tiles, The One Ring 001/001 for Sphinx alone, and print runs that packs follow. This follows [the v0.5.1 plan](../plans/0.5.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-23 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.5.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v051)):
- A click on a card on the Cards page shows its picture big, on every tab, the Godlike tier's custom cards included.
- Every card the table draws has rounded corners.
- The player search by email: the cause found on the live site's data, read-only, and fixed.
- "Concede the match" asks first; the Two-Headed Giant bot decks are re-paired.
- Two-Headed Giant at human tables and in a queue (solo or with a friend), with team chat, the teammate's hand on the table and the full card audit. Kept as v0.5.1 by the owner's choice.
- Added during the version ([the plan](../plans/0.5.1.md#added-during-v051)): the chat bar, the menu's paintings, The One Ring 001/001 and print runs.

## Added

- **Two-Headed Giant at human tables** (FMT10). A table can be opened for Two-Headed Giant: four seats in two teams, four players or players with bots as teammates or opponents. Players choose their team, ready up and start by team; each deck is Legacy, and a team's two decks follow Unified Deck Construction, checked as a player gets ready and again at the start. One player's concession loses the team (810.8b); an away vote's removal is a concession. Both winners are counted and paid, each with their own daily limits, quests and achievements; a rematch keeps the teams ([human tables](../tables.md#two-headed-giant-v051)).
- **A Two-Headed Giant queue**, beside Legacy and Commander: alone, paired with another player whose deck shares no card with yours, or with a friend, invited through the bell; a pair is never split; bots after a minute as in every queue ([queues](../queues.md#two-headed-giant-v051)).
- **The team's channel.** At a Two-Headed Giant table the table chat's Allies tab is the team's, fixed from the start; the other team never reads a word of it ([chat](../chat.md#the-teams-channel-v051)).
- **The teammate's hand on the table** (810.5): a face-up fan along the bottom of the teammate's area, in both painters, magnified on hover or focus and never playable. The table for two teams: the teammate seated first, team pills, team poison, "Your team's turn", the result by team, SysOp's "Team 1/Team 2" ([the game screen](../game-screen.md#two-headed-giant-v050-v051)).
- **The card audit** (three more XMage patches): "each player's upkeep" and other step triggers for both players of the active team, "your turn" and "not your turn" for the team, skips, extra steps and turn control as the team's, can't-lose and can't-win for the team, life totals set or exchanged by the team's rules, poison checks on the team's counters, Assist counting the teammate's mana, "the attacking player", and an AI that attacks and blocks with its teammate in mind; 82 cards patched, and 836 card-and-pattern verdicts recorded ([the card audit](../engine/two-headed-giant.md#the-card-audit-v051)). The format stays marked Early: team priority (805.5–805.7) and a few rare cards are not done.
- **A card's picture, big** (the owner's request): a click on a card's picture on any tab of the Cards page, the Godlike cards included, shows it alone as large as the screen allows; its details are a button away ([the card catalog](../catalog.md#on-screen)).
- **The chat bar** (the owner's request during the version): friends' messages live on a bar along the bottom of every page, with a friends list that folds, shows who is online and reopens any conversation; the Messages page is gone, and its old links open the bar. At the game table the bar folds into a band diamond and comes out over the table on demand, covering nothing until then ([chat](../chat.md#friends-messages)).
- **Turn control** (the owner's decision after the audit found that it hung a game in every format): a player who controls another player's turn (Mindslaver, Emrakul, Sorin Markov and the like) makes that player's decisions in their own prompt panel, marked "for bob", and sees what that player may see while the control lasts (723): their hand, their face-down objects, the cards they looked at, their teammate's hand in Two-Headed Giant. The controlled player keeps their own view, reads that "ada controls your turn", and may still concede. Who controls whom is public; every leak check allows the controller's view exactly while control lasts ([turn control](../engine/human-seat.md#turn-control-v051)).
- **The main menu's paintings** (the owner's): Tables, Decks, Cards and Friends lie over the owner's paintings, as Play and the shop do ([the menus](../menus.md)).
- **The One Ring 001/001** (the owner's request): the only copy ever printed, in the Black Speech of Mordor (LTR #0), is Sphinx's alone, granted by migration 0027; no pack deals it and no other deck may hold it, SysOp's aside ([one of a kind](../progression.md#one-of-a-kind-v051)).
- **Print runs** (the owner's request): packs never deal a serialized printing more often, across every account, than it was printed: all 298 serialized printings, from /77 to /900, each count sourced from Wizards of the Coast; every other edition stays in packs ([print runs](../shop.md#print-runs-v051)).

## Changed

- **The Two-Headed Giant bots' pairing:** red and white against green and blue, the most even of the three pairings measured (46% over 100 bot games, against 62% for v0.5.0's red and green); the bots' decks are dealt by a search, so a game is refused only when no dealing exists ([fixture decks](../engine/two-headed-giant.md#fixture-decks)).
- **"Concede the match" on the Play page asks first**, as the match page's does (the owner's change to a v0.5.0 decision).
- **Two-Headed Giant's shared cards are named only to players who stay teammates:** the check reads each seat's bound deck, never a draft of cards its player does not own, and once cards are named the two stay on that team at that table ([human tables](../tables.md#two-headed-giant-v051)).
- **A history's opponents** in Two-Headed Giant are the other team; the teammate is listed with them.

## Fixed

- **The player search by email** found no one for a whole gmail.com address (the owner's report). The live data showed why: that player had no handle yet (approved but not through the setup wizard's first step, or reset). The search now says so, and **Add friend** sends a request that waits, unseen, until they choose a handle ([finding players](../social.md#a-player-with-no-handle-yet-v051)).
- **Card corners on the table** (the owner's report): Scryfall's JPEGs fill a card's rounded corners, so the WebGL table drew them square. Each picture is cut to the card's corners once, as it is decoded, and the glows follow ([the game screen](../game-screen.md#cards)).
- **Found by the version's reviews and fixed:** a friend's queue invitation that never appeared on Find a game until a reload; an invitation outliving the friendship, and two friends inviting each other at once; team rows locked in different orders (a PostgreSQL deadlock); the bots' fixture decks dealt first-fit, refusing valid games; a bot teammate shown as "skill-?" in the history; a rematch skipping the format's own banned card; a chipped teammate taking an attack drop; at the table, Escape in a chat Report dialog freezing the table; the chat bar's background reads keeping a session from ever reaching its idle limit; windows and drafts remembered for the browser rather than the account; an ex-friend left among "Your friends"; and the rest listed in the docs each fix names.

## Decisions taken while building

Taken by the agents and me while building, for the owner to revisit:
- **Tables:** the host's team is Team 1; bots fill the host's teammate seat first, then the other team's last seats; a deck or team change unreadies the teammate; in Two-Headed Giant the teammate votes on removing an away player, and a removal loses their team.
- **Queue:** players alone are paired only when their decks share no card, and two who cannot be teammates play on opposite sides with a bot each after a minute; a friend team's place starts at acceptance and takes the inviter's "players only"; an invitation has no time limit of its own.
- **Results:** only the other team counts as opponents, also in Two-Headed Giant practice games (bots 2, not 3).
- **The teammate's hand:** a fan by the teammate's board rather than a drawer; "+N" past 15 cards; the teammate always sits in the left column; SysOp observing sees the watched seat's teammate's hand; the response timer still runs on a teammate's spell.
- **The audit:** "each player's" step triggers decided from their rules text (that player, they, their), with hand-made exceptions; "the active player" is the turn's holder when an ability picks none; the team's first player chooses whose life total is set (810.9d); a bot asked to Assist pays all it can; the AI's team play is heuristic; turn control for human seats is left undone (below).
- **The chat bar:** on a desktop the friends list stays open after a conversation opens; at the table messages are counted and spoken, their pop-ups wait for the pages; the bar at the table is a diamond and a floating panel, not a strip.
- **Turn control:** the controller sees the controlled player's hand, face-down objects, looked-at cards, their teammate's hand and their commander's hidden zone, never their deck's name or sideboard (outside the game, 723.4); the controller passes each priority one at a time (no pass modes for the controlled player), the response timer never runs on decisions made for them, their hand cannot be dragged, and their personal messages reach the controller too; if the controller leaves, control ends at once and an open bot decision gets the least-impact answer; the emblems read "You Control This Turn" and "Controlled Turn".
- **One of a kind and print runs:** the ring is granted by handle, never by email (the repository is public); a reset would take it from Sphinx's library; administrators who build from every card may not hold it, SysOp may; a print run counts every copy ever granted, a reset aside; a printing whose count no source states is not limited.

## Known, not done

- **A bot cannot take control of a turn:** XMage refuses it at the pin ("is AI and can't take control"), so a bot's Mindslaver is lost and the player plays their own turn; nothing hangs, and no bot deck holds such a card today. Three ways on are open (keep the refusal, a fixed rule, or an AI acting for the controlled player, which needs an XMage patch). Word of Command and Opposition Agent go through the same routing as Mindslaver but have no test of their own.
- **Two-Headed Giant's team priority and team choices** (805.5–805.7) and attacks legal as a whole (805.10b), and a handful of rare cards the audit lists: about a version.
- **No browser journey plays a Two-Headed Giant game with four people** against the real engine; the tables, queue and team channel are checked through the API against the fake engine, and the table on the fake table.
