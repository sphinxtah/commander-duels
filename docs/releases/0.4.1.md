# v0.4.1: ready for the beta testers

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-21. It gets the live site ready for about 20 beta testers to sign in with Google and request an account. The fixes for what the owner and the testers find come in v0.4.2. This follows [the v0.4.1 plan](../plans/0.4.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-21 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v041)):
- Public About, Privacy and Terms pages, linked in one small line below the landing page's sign-in button; the About page is the home page given to Google. Contact: `info@thesphinx.co`.
- An in-app "Report a problem" form with an optional screenshot, and a Reports tab in Administration.
- Counts of waiting approvals and unread reports in Administration's navigation and the tab's title.
- "Delete account" for SysOp and administrators, keeping game results under "Deleted player"; requests never approved are removed after 30 days.
- Up to 64 games at once.

## Changed

- **The Sphinx's portrait** (the owner's request, 2026-10-21). The Godlike tier's Planeswalker has its portrait, from the owner's `the.sphinx.png`, made into the same three sizes as the other 98 by `scripts/planeswalkers.py portraits`. It shows wherever an alliance's portrait does: the profile, the sidebar, the alliance picker and the table. Only the portrait is public: the custom cards stay in the owner's private repository, and nothing about them is in this one.
- **Up to 64 games at once** (the owner's limit). `CD_GAMES_MAX` and the engine's `CD_ENGINE_MAX_GAMES` go up to 64 instead of 8, and the engine keeps at least as many finished games readable as may run, so games that end together all record their results.
  - **Bots under load.** Every bot in the engine shares one pool of search threads, which had five, as XMage's own does, and a decision's time budget ran from the moment the bot asked for a thread. With many games at once, searches waited in line, ran out of budget before they began, and the bots passed. The pool now has a thread per processor (`CD_ENGINE_AI_THREADS` overrides it), and the budget starts when the search does; a search that finds no thread within 60 seconds is skipped and the bot passes ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#decision-budget-ai05)). The engine logs each bot's searches when its game ends.
  - **The API's connections to the engine.** Every human seat holds a long poll to the engine, up to four a game. The API's HTTP client allowed 100 connections, so with many human tables a player's command could wait behind them; it now has no such cap.
