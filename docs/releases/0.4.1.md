# v0.4.1: ready for the beta testers

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-21 (tag `v0.4.1`). It gets the live site ready for about 20 beta testers to sign in with Google and request an account. The fixes for what the owner and the testers find come in v0.4.2. This follows [the v0.4.1 plan](../plans/0.4.1.md).

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-21 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.4.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v041)):
- Public About, Privacy and Terms pages, linked in one small line below the landing page's sign-in button; the About page is the home page given to Google. Contact: `info@thesphinx.co`.
- An in-app "Report a problem" form with an optional screenshot, and a Reports tab in Administration.
- Counts of waiting approvals and unread reports in Administration's navigation and the tab's title.
- "Delete account" for SysOp and administrators, keeping game results under "Deleted player"; requests never approved are removed after 30 days.
- Up to 64 games at once.

## Changed

- **Public About, Privacy and Terms pages** ([identity](../identity.md#the-public-pages-v041)). https://duels.thesphinx.co/about, /privacy and /terms are plain pages that need no sign-in and no JavaScript, in the site's look and readable on a phone. About says what the site is: a free, unofficial beta with sign-in by approval and virtual tokens only. It is the home page Google's branding check asks for, because Google refuses a home page that is only a sign-in. The privacy policy says what the site keeps, why, who sees it, for how long and how to have it deleted, as the code does it; the terms cover the beta, virtual items and conduct. Contact: `info@thesphinx.co`. Both are plain-language drafts for the owner to review. The landing page stays wordless, with one small line below the sign-in button: About · Privacy · Terms; the app's footer has the same links. The installed app never answers these addresses from its cache.
- **The waiting screen, for people who have never heard of the site.** A pending request says it is in, that the owner reviews each request by hand, and that the page opens the site by itself once approved. A refused one says to write to `info@thesphinx.co` if it is a mistake, and that a request not approved is deleted after 30 days.
- **Report a problem** ([problem reports](../reports.md)). Every page of the app, and the table's Game menu, has "Report a problem": a summary, what happened, and an optional screenshot chosen as a file (a phone's photo library), pasted or dropped. The report also carries, as the form says, the page, the version, the game and its turn, the browser, the window's size and the IDs of the player's latest requests, which match the API's log. The server re-encodes the screenshot as WebP from its pixels alone, so no location or other metadata survives, and refuses anything over 5 MB or 40 million pixels. 20 reports a day per account. Administration's new Reports tab, for administrators and SysOp, lists them with their screenshots, and each status change (new, seen, fixed, closed) is audited. Migration `0012_reports`; Pillow 12.3.0 joins the API's dependencies.
- **Counts of waiting accounts and unread reports.** For SysOp and administrators, Admin in the navigation shows a badge with them, Administration's tabs show them, with a button for the waiting accounts, and the browser tab's title starts with their total, for example "(3) Commander Duels". They refresh every minute while the page is shown, without counting as activity, so an open tab still signs out on time.
- **Delete account** ([identity](../identity.md#deleting-an-account-v041)). SysOp and administrators may delete a player's account, with a reason, after typing its handle. The account's row stays, as "Deleted player", because the ledgers and other players' games refer to it. Its Google identity (subject and email), sessions, role grants, decks, problem reports, handle, name, avatar and alliance go. A revision a game played keeps its cards for that game, without its title or notes. The same Google account signing in later starts again as a new request. An account playing a game is deleted once its game ends. Administrators cannot delete administrators, and nobody can change a deleted account. Audited as `admin.user_deleted`. Migration `0013_account_deletion`.
- **Requests never approved are deleted after 30 days.** A daily worker job deletes, the same way, every account still pending or denied 30 days after it signed in first, and records how many in the audit trail (`account.unapproved_deleted`).
- **The Sphinx's portrait** (the owner's request, 2026-10-21). The Godlike tier's Planeswalker has its portrait, from the owner's `the.sphinx.png`, made into the same three sizes as the other 98 by `scripts/planeswalkers.py portraits`. It shows wherever an alliance's portrait does: the profile, the sidebar, the alliance picker and the table. Only the portrait is public: the custom cards stay in the owner's private repository, and nothing about them is in this one.
- **Up to 64 games at once** (the owner's limit). `CD_GAMES_MAX` and the engine's `CD_ENGINE_MAX_GAMES` go up to 64 instead of 8, and the engine keeps at least as many finished games readable as may run, so games that end together all record their results.
  - **Bots under load.** Every bot in the engine shares one pool of search threads, which had five, as XMage's own does, and a decision's time budget ran from the moment the bot asked for a thread. With many games at once, searches waited in line, ran out of budget before they began, and the bots passed. The pool now has a thread per processor (`CD_ENGINE_AI_THREADS` overrides it), and the budget starts when the search does; a search that finds no thread within 60 seconds is skipped and the bot passes ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#decision-budget-ai05)). The engine logs each bot's searches when its game ends.
  - **The API's connections to the engine.** Every human seat holds a long poll to the engine, up to four a game. The API's HTTP client allowed 100 connections, so with many human tables a player's command could wait behind them; it now has no such cap.

## Deploying 0.4.1

In this order:
1. **The private engine image.** The engine changed, so the private repository's CI builds `ghcr.io/sphinxtah/commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.4.1` once the tag's run has published the `0.4.1` images, as for 0.4.0. `deploy.sh` stops, replacing nothing, if it cannot pull it.
2. **`.env` on the VM:** change `CD_GAMES_MAX=6` to `CD_GAMES_MAX=64`. The engine's new 12 GB heap and 16 GB limit come with the kit; the VM's 32 GB hold them ([sizing](../deployment.md#the-vm)).
3. **Deploy:** `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Migrations 0012 (problem reports) and 0013 (account deletion) run on the way; both only add, so the previous version keeps working while they run. The worker schedules the daily removal of requests never approved by itself.
4. **The TSH proxy**, for screenshots over 750 KB ([deployment](../deployment.md#on-the-tsh-proxy), step 5): copy the `location = /api/v1/reports` block from `deploy/nginx/duels.thesphinx.co` into the live file's HTTPS server block, then `sudo nginx -t` and reload. Until then, the page shrinks a larger screenshot to 750 KB and sends it again.
5. **Google's consoles**, once the pages are live ([Google sign-in](../deployment.md#google-sign-in)): verify `thesphinx.co` in Search Console with a DNS TXT record; fill in Branding (the name, `web/public/brand/google-logo-120.png`, the home page `/about`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, the authorized domain); Audience → Publish app; then Verify branding.
