# v0.6.2: the real card in every pack

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened on 2026-10-24. This follows [the v0.6.2 plan](../plans/0.6.2.md). Hosted CI is recorded here at close-out.

## Scope agreed for this version

Agreed on 2026-10-24 (details in [the plan](../plans/0.6.2.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v062)):
- A pack gives the card it pulled, always: no more cards given in place of one the game cannot play. Casual games play such a card as a stand-in with its printed cost, types, power and toughness; tournament rules refuse it. Packs opened since v0.4.5 give their players the cards that were replaced.
- An alliance's cards on a tile before choosing it, with "Begin Alliance with <name>".
- Granular filters in My Library.
- A deck shown as its cards by default, with the list as the other view.
- The rare cards the Two-Headed Giant audit left wrong.
- Added mid-thread by the owner: Alpha's and Beta's Power Nine and dual lands five times as likely, and Alpha, Beta, Unlimited and Arabian Nights at a quarter of their price.

## Added

- **The actual card in every pack: stand-ins** (the owner: "when tearing packs, I want the actual card to be there. No more replacing cards."). A pack now gives the card it drew, whether the engine can play it or not ([the shop](../shop.md), [stand-ins](../catalog.md#stand-ins-v062)).
  - **Back in the game.** Cards the engine has no rules for yet are back for everyone as stand-ins. Search, card pages, libraries, decks and the pack summary show them with a "Stand-in" badge.
  - **Played in casual games.** At casual tables, practice games and the queues, a stand-in plays with its own picture and its printed cost, types, subtypes, supertypes, power, toughness and loyalty; its rules text does nothing. The engine builds it itself (`StandInCard`) from what the API sends with the deck list.
  - **Refused by tournament rules.** A new table option, "Stand-ins", is on by default and off under Tournament rules. A deck holding a stand-in is tagged "Casual tables only: Plays stand-ins".
  - **Past packs.** Packs opened from v0.4.5 to v0.6.1 whose card was swapped now also grant the card drawn, in its finish, beside the one given (migration `0029_stand_ins`). Their openings show both; the swapped-in card is marked "Extra".
  - **Still never in a pack:** tokens, art cards and the other objects no deck holds, cards Scryfall no longer lists, and cards the staff suspended.
- **An alliance's cards before choosing it.** In Profile's alliance picker, and in the setup's choice after the starter decks, a Planeswalker opens a tile with their portrait and every card the alliance brings. Each picture opens large on a click, and "Begin Alliance with <name>" chooses them. Locked Planeswalkers show their cards too, with the wins that open them (`GET /api/v1/planeswalkers/{id}/cards`). The Sphinx's custom cards stay private ([progression](../progression.md)).
- **My library's filters.** A Filters button beside the library's search filters its cards by set, type, mana value, rarity, finish and colour (any, all or exactly), and sorts them by name, mana value, newest edition, rarity or copies.
- **The deck as its cards.** Opening a deck shows its cards' pictures by default, grouped as the list is (commander first, then by type and mana value), each with its copies. A click opens the card as the list does; the list is a switch away, remembered in the browser ([decks](../decks.md)).

## Changed

- **Boosted chase cards** (the owner, mid-thread). In Alpha and Beta boosters and starter decks, each of the Power Nine (Black Lotus, the five Moxen, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, Timetwister) and the ten dual lands is exactly five times as likely as before. The sheet's other rares share the rest, so these 19 cards fill about 82% of Alpha's rare slots, and Black Lotus comes in about 4.3% of Alpha packs instead of 0.9%. The real print runs still cap them ([the shop](../shop.md)).
- **The first sets at a quarter** (the owner, mid-thread). Alpha, Beta, Unlimited and Arabian Nights boosters and starter decks cost a quarter of their curve price: Alpha booster 15,000,000 tokens (was 61,000,000), Alpha starter deck 110,000,000, Beta booster 3,500,000, Beta starter deck 28,000,000, Unlimited booster 950,000, Unlimited starter deck 4,000,000, Arabian Nights booster 650,000. A price staff set by hand still wins ([pack prices](../pack-prices.md#the-first-sets-at-a-quarter-v062)).
- **Booster data on deploy** (on main since v0.6.1's tag): the worker queues a booster data run as it starts when products have none yet.

## Fixed

- **Two-Headed Giant's rare cards** (carried over from v0.5.1). A ninth XMage patch, `0009-two-headed-giant-rare-cards`, fixes 16 of the 17 cards the card audit left wrong; the 17th, Elkin Lair, already played correctly ([Two-Headed Giant](../engine/two-headed-giant.md)).
  - "The attacking player" is one player of the attacking team, chosen by the ability's controller (rule 805.10c: Invasion Plans, Contested Game Ball).
  - Ellie, Brick Master, Emberwilde Captain and Tahngarth, First Mate trigger for each attacking player of the team. Tenuous Truce, Master Warcraft, War's Toll and Territorial Hellkite read each creature's controller's attack. O-Kagachi, Weathered Sentinels, Arboria and Invasion of Fiora's Marchesa remember who attacked, or whose turn it was, player by player.
  - Storage Matrix asks each player of the team in the team's untap step. Urabrask's draw replacement replaces only its own opponent's draw. Brine Elemental makes the opposing team skip one untap step, not two (805.8).
  - Outside Two-Headed Giant every card plays as before.

## Known, not done

- **Weathered Sentinels** has an XMage defect in every format: it stops all of its controller's creatures from attacking, not only the Sentinels. Fixing it would change Legacy and Commander, so it is documented and left.
- **Guided deck creation** still proposes only cards the engine plays; there is no search term for stand-ins yet.
- **The library's filters** start folded and reset when the tab is left.
- **Carried to later:** "Add to deck" from the library, and the four-player Two-Headed Giant browser journey.

## Deploying 0.6.2

No `.env` change. One migration (`0029_stand_ins`), which `upgrade.sh` runs. The engine changed (patch 0009 and the stand-in card), so the private engine image `0.6.2` must be built too. Once the tag's run has published the `0.6.2` images and the private engine image exists, on the VM: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Afterwards: packs of Alpha show the new prices; a past pack whose card was swapped shows the card drawn and the "Extra"; a library holds stand-ins with their badge.
