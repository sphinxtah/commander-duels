# v0.9.3: the campaign's curve, Apex Unbound, team tables to the end

Opened and closed on 2026-11-09. The next version is v0.9.4 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.4.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner opened v0.9.3 asking what was left from earlier versions, for a recommendation, and to catch up: "I have lots of tokens left." The start questions settled ([the plan](../plans/0.9.3.md#the-owners-answers-at-the-start-2026-11-09)):
- **Everything recommended**: the campaign's difficulty; the finale and the campaign's tuning (the incarnation's test, the barks, the post-campaign challenge); browser journeys of Two-Headed Giant with four people and of King's Table; the quick wins (Find a game's order and host search, deleting decks from the trash for good, the backlog's stale lines).
- **The lever**: rework the late wardens' decks (7 to 14) toward plans XMage's AI plays well.
- **The targets**: from the owner's handoff (a forgiving first ward, a clear rise in every chapter, a finale beaten only rarely), set as reference win rates by tier.
- **Bugs**: the testers' reports on the live site, of which there were none.

During the work the owner added: every warden's deck is won whole on its first defeat; rework the finale's 99 too; players who already won a reworked warden keep the deck they won.

## Fixed

- **Sleeve pictures above about 0.7 MB would not upload** (the owner's report: a 3.4 MB picture, "The application service is unavailable"). Both proxies kept nginx's 1 MB request limit for sleeves, which travel in base64; they now take 8 MB there, as for reports, and the page says plainly when a picture is too large. The page also makes a large picture smaller before sending it, cropped to a card's shape and encoded under 0.7 MB, so it fits even where a proxy keeps its old limit ([sleeves](../sleeves.md)). **The TSH proxy's site file changed**: copy `deploy/nginx/duels.thesphinx.co` to the proxy and reload nginx (the page's shrinking works without it).

## Changed

- **The late wardens' decks** (7 to 13) are rebuilt around their commanders, colours and themes as decks XMage's AI pilots: dense creature curves, removal, evasive threats and few loops, the power rising by tier ([the campaign](../campaign.md#difficulty-measured-again-v093)). Each still produces its ward's bark triggers (Tezzeret's artifact recursion, Ashiok's library pressure, Ob Nixilis's sweep). Liliana's lost the loop fodder that made her turns crawl.
- **The finale's 99** (the owner's Apex list, which the live finale plays with the High Priest) keeps its unrestricted power (the Power Nine, fast mana, Force of Will, Mana Drain, Teferi, Opposition Agent, Hullbreacher, Grand Abolisher, and Doubling Season, Deepglow Skate and Vorinclex for the incarnation) and trades the combo lines the AI never assembled for threats, removal and protection.
- **Bronze's skills**: Daretti's bot is stronger (skill 3) and Dovin's weaker (skill 2), which the measurement asked for; Bronze's decks are as the handoff wrote them.
- **A team's concession ends the game at once.** At Two-Headed Giant one player's concession takes their team out (810.8b) and the other team wins; at King's Table a King's concession loses the game for their team. Until now the engine applied such a concession only at a winner's next answer, so the game waited on the winners ([human tables](../tables.md#two-headed-giant-v051)).
- **The bots stop losing on their own**: a bot's own Demonic Consultation names a card its library holds instead of a random one, and a bot holds a Pact until it could pay the Pact's upkeep. The finale lost on turn two at 40 life to both ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md)).
- **Heavy games search shallower.** A search whose copy of the game takes more than 200 ms, or that starts while the heap is more than 70% full after a collection, looks two plies ahead. The finale's combo turns had made each copy take seconds; a cancelled search could not stop, and the heap ran out, which in production would stop every game the engine runs ([fair bots](../engine/fair-bots.md#light-searches-for-heavy-games-v093)).

## Added

- **Apex Unbound**, the handoff's post-campaign challenge: once the finale is won, the Campaign page offers it, the finale again with Kaya's aids off, for a badge of its own, once. It never gives the seals, spoils or tokens again, and the ending won stays. The badge shows under Rewards and on the profile ([the campaign](../campaign.md#apex-unbound-v093)).
- **Delete for good** in the Trash tab, and **Empty trash**, each asking first. A deck whose versions no game used goes entirely; one a game played keeps those versions for the game, hidden from every list and outside the 250-deck limit ([decks](../decks.md#deleting-for-good-v093)).
- **Find a game's order and host search**: Newest, Most players or Fewest open seats, kept with the filters; a Host box narrows the cards by the host's handle or name ([human tables](../tables.md#finding-a-game-and-inviting-friends-v092)).
- **The spoils are whole**: a warden's card the catalog cannot resolve is logged by name instead of dropped quietly. All fourteen decks and the loaner resolved card for card in the live catalog.
- **Tests and tools**: `IncarnationRemovalTest` (the incarnation back to its reserve from every removal), `FairBotNamingTest` (Consultation and Pacts), `TwoHeadedGiantTest.aTeamThatConcedesLosesWhileTheWinnersPromptIsOpen`, and the difficulty harness's `-Dcampaign.trace` (a game's log). Browser journeys of Two-Headed Giant with four people and King's Table with two people and four bots (`web/e2e/team-tables.spec.ts`), in CI's engine job with two more generated players.
- **API**: `DELETE /api/v1/decks/{id}/forever`, `POST /api/v1/decks/trash/empty`; `sort` and `host` on `GET /api/v1/tables/browse`; `challenge` on a campaign duel's start, its game summary and the campaign's state; `badges` on profiles.
