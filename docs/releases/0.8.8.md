## Added

### SysOp's grants for testing

SysOp sees its own account first in Administration › Accounts. Its row offers the grants other accounts' rows do, and "Testing grants…" opens four more ([identity](../identity.md#sysops-grants-for-testing-v088)).

- **Tokens and wins.** "Give yourself tokens" and "Set your wins" are the grants SysOp already gave other accounts (v0.4.0, v0.3.1).
- **Wins by format.** Commander, Legacy, Two-Headed Giant or King's Table, 1 to 100 at a time, with the deck's colours and the opponents (bots of a skill, or players).
  - **What they count towards.** They count towards the day's quests and the achievements, checked and paid at once as after a game won. The account's wins rise by as many, as a setting of wins.
  - **What they never count towards.** The profile's statistics and the match history, and no win pays tokens.
- **Packs.** Any product with booster data, 1 to 100. The API has offered this since v0.4.0; now there's a form for it.
- **Cards.** Found by name, 1 to 99 copies of the card's default edition, nonfoil or foil, into the library for good. They may be sold at the auction house. SysOp's own library is the whole catalog, so a grant to itself only gives it copies to list.
- **Campaign progress.** "Won through" an encounter gives:
  - each encounter's seal;
  - the next encounter opened;
  - each completed chapter's card back or title.

  It never gives the spoils (decks and tokens). A number below what's won starts the campaign over first, and "None" starts it over.
- **Records.**
  - Every grant takes a reason and is audited (`testing.results_granted`, `testing.cards_granted`, `testing.campaign_set`, besides the earlier `economy.tokens_granted`, `shop.packs_granted` and `progress.wins_set`).
  - Only SysOp grants. Another root account is never a target, and nobody else ever sees SysOp's account in the list.
- **In the API.**
  - `POST /api/v1/admin/users/{id}/results`, `…/cards` and `…/campaign`.
  - `GET /api/v1/admin/users` lists SysOp's own account first, for SysOp only.
  - Migration 0037 lets a result be granted (no game, `granted_by`) and a library grant's source be `grant`.

### Add to deck

Every card in My library offers "Add to deck", on its tile and in its row ([decks](../decks.md#add-to-deck-v088)).

- **The pop-up.** Choose one of your decks, how many copies, and where:
  - main deck, sideboard or maybeboard;
  - for a Commander deck that the card can lead, the command zone.
- **Saving.**
  - The copies join the deck's entry for that card and edition, or a new one, and the draft saves.
  - If another tab saved the deck meanwhile, the deck is read again once and the copies added to it.
  - The library page says whether the deck is ready to play.

### The campaign's cosmetics

The card backs and titles the campaign's chapters reward (v0.8.0) can now be used ([the campaign](../campaign.md#cosmetics-v088)).

- **Choosing them.** The Campaign page's Rewards list now has "Your rewards in use":
  - the card backs you've earned, each drawn small, beside the official back;
  - a choice of title.
- **The card backs.**
  - Four designs, drawn for this version: Broken Writ, Unclaimed World, Broken Throne and Fractured Throne.
  - The one you choose replaces the official back on your own table: your library, your opponents' hands and every face-down card, on the canvas and on the page.
  - Showing your back to the others at a shared table is for v0.8.9.
- **The titles.** Unbowed, Witness of the Unbound, Concordance Breaker. The one you choose stands under your name on your profile, for everyone who may open it.
- **Only what's earned.** Only what your current campaign progress has earned may be chosen, and what's shown is checked on every read. A reset of the account or of its campaign takes a cosmetic away with its reward.
- **In the API.** `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/me/cosmetics`, and `title` on player profiles. Migration 0037 adds `users.card_back` and `users.title`.

### The payment prompt names what raised the cost

- **What it says.** When an effect made a spell or ability cost more, its payment prompt now says so, for example "The cost is raised by commander tax and Thalia, Guardian of Thraben."
- **How the engine finds them.** It names each of XMage's cost-increasing effects that applies to the spell or ability being paid for, by its source, and commander tax by name.
- **When it says nothing.** A payment another player's spell asks of you (a tax such as Rhystic Study's) names nothing.

## Changed

### Administrators and moderators are players too

- **Their own libraries.** Administrators build from a library of their own, as every player does: starter decks, packs, the auction house, alliances, the campaign's spoils. Only SysOp builds from the whole catalog now (`library.unlimited`).
- **What changes for an administrator.**
  - A deck holding cards their library lacks now says so, and can't start a game until they own the copies.
  - The worker revalidates every staff account's decks each time it starts, so their flags say so from the first deploy.
- **Moderators** already played from their own libraries. Nothing changes for them.
- **The content editor role is removed.** Migration 0037 deletes its grants. Its one permission, `content.edit`, stays with administrators, and the accounts that held it sign in as players with a fresh privilege version ([identity](../identity.md)).

## Fixed

- **A low-skill bot's habits under turn control.** A skill-1 to skill-5 bot's slips, naive plays, held-back attackers and missing blocks no longer happen while another player controls its turn (Mindslaver, 723.5). The bot could pass for the controlling human without asking them. `TurnControlTest` now checks that the controller is asked in both main phases.
- **King's Table**:
  - **The live result** sent to the seats now names the winning team (`winner_team`) and every seat of it, as the recorded result has since v0.8.7.
  - **Deploy** is marked in its prompt with an action of its own, `deploy`, instead of being told apart by its words ([the seat-view contract](../../contracts/engine-seat-view.schema.json)).
  - **The practice table's fixture decks** come by deck format (`decks_by_format`), so each list fits the deck format the settings choose.

## Deploying 0.8.8

**Migration 0037.** It's additive. v0.8.7 keeps serving while it runs.

- It adds `player_results.granted_by` and lets `game_id` be empty for a granted result.
- It adds `grant` to the library grants' sources.
- It adds `users.card_back` and `users.title`.
- It deletes the content editor role's grants and narrows the role check.

There's no `.env` change.

**The engine image** rebuilds only its own layer. The XMage pin and its patches are unchanged.

**The private images must exist at the tag first.** Run the private repository's engine and web workflows once the tag's run has published the public `0.8.8` images. Run the engine workflow first, then the web one.

**Then, on the VM, as always:** `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`.
