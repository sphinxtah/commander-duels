# v0.8.9: card sleeves, administrators as SysOp, basic lands first, packs that sell out

A patch version, opened and closed on 2026-11-06. The next version is v0.9.0 ([the plan](../plans/0.9.0.md)).

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-11-06, at the start, took the three items noted during v0.8.8 and added two of their own:

- **Administrators see the app as SysOp does**, with the recommended split of powers, and one addition: "make sure all of the cards owned by the admins takes away from the actual total of cards in circulation."
- **Basic lands lead the mana payment.**
- **Each player's card back at a shared table**, which became part of the sleeves.
- **Card sleeves** (new): "the ability for players to upload an image for their virtual card sleeves… The default is just the normal MTG card back." Up to 20 per account, 5 MB each, PNG, BMP or JPG, any one per deck, seen by everyone and reportable from "a small popup of their profile" at the table. During the evening the owner added three default sleeves everyone has from the start.
- **The top bar tucks away on a phone** (new, added at midnight): "On mobile when scrolling down, the top bar should retract, just like how facebook app works… Then scrolling back up shows the top bar again."
- **Print runs and packs that sell out** (new): SysOp's cards come out of no print run; packs whose set's run is spent show SOLD OUT and are locked; the counts are researched real-life numbers.

The owner chose to have it all live that night. The carried items move to v0.9.0: Weathered Sentinels, the Two-Headed Giant and King's Table browser journeys, the campaign's tuning in play, and the v0.6.2–v0.6.4 notes.

## Added

### Card sleeves

([Card sleeves](../sleeves.md).) In the deck editor's This deck panel, a Card sleeve section chooses the deck's sleeve: the official back, one of three default sleeves (the owner's designs), an earned campaign back, or one of up to 20 uploaded pictures. Uploads are cropped to a card's shape and re-encoded as WebP on the server. At a table every seat's cards show their owner's sleeve to everyone. A click on another person's name opens their profile over the table, where their sleeve is reported; Administration › Reports shows the picture and removes it from every table, audited.

### Administrators see the app as SysOp does

([Identity](../identity.md#administrators-see-the-app-as-sysop-does-v089).) The eye icon and the Inspection drawer at the table, with the passive-bots switch there; observing and listing every game; every chat; any player's history; wins and the testing grants; tokens and packs. Never concealed information in a game they play with another person. SysOp alone keeps the system-level powers: custom cards, its own account, administrators, the economy's limits and auction cancelling, account resets, and any card in a deck or library. `economy.manage` is split: giving is the new `economy.grant`.

### Basic lands lead the mana payment

The payment prompt lists any mana in the pool first, then the basic lands in W, U, B, R, G order (then Wastes; snow basics among their type), then every other source by name (`Prompts.payMana`, `BasicLandsLeadTest`).

### Packs that sell out

([Shop](../shop.md#sold-out-v089).) `content/pack-supply.json` holds the print runs, in cards, of the sets whose run a source of the time states: Alpha, Beta, Unlimited, Arabian Nights, Antiquities, Legends, The Dark and Fallen Empires, from the 1995 Rulings Summary. Every product of such a set takes its real size out of the run; when the run cannot fill one more, the shelf shows SOLD OUT across the pack and neither sells nor gives it. Revised and every later set have no published run and never sell out. At the real numbers no set will sell out soon: Alpha alone holds about 173,000 boosters.

### The top bar tucks away on a phone

On screens 820 px wide or less, scrolling down slides the top bar up out of sight, and scrolling up brings it straight back, as the Facebook app's does. It stays at the top of a page, while one of its pop-overs (the bell, the search, the account's menu) is open, and while it holds the keyboard's focus; a few pixels' jitter moves nothing. Larger screens keep it in place (`TopBar.tsx` `useRetracting`).

## Changed

- **Print runs** count every account's copies but SysOp's; an administrator's count as a player's. A testing grant of cards past a run is refused.
- **The summary's seats** carry `sleeve` and `player_id`; the game summary carries `inspectable`.
- **Tests** keep seating administrators as players: the test-only shortcut also leaves out v0.8.9's powers, and tests marked `real_roles` see them.
