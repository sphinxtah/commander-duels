## The owner's report: the fifth daily quest did not complete

"I just did Step 5 quest for today and it didn't complete that quest for me because the 2-4 hadn't been completed." He asked for the quests to complete in any order, and for another word than "Steps".

- **What happened.** The quests never depended on each other: each is judged by itself, and a test now proves the fifth completes and pays while the first four are open. The live site's audit log shows the cause: the game was played with **passive bots**, the administrators' testing switch (`game.bots_passive`, twelve minutes before the report), and a game whose bots have been passive counts for nothing, by the rule of v0.8.6: no win, no tokens, no quest, no achievement. The quest was "Win a four-player Commander game against skill-6 bots"; the board showed it open because no game had counted.
- **The switch says so now.** Before it is used: "Once used, the game counts for nothing, even with the switch off again: no win, no tokens, no quests or achievements." Once it has been: a warning, "This game no longer counts." And the game's result says "Passive bots were used, so this game counts for nothing" (a campaign duel: no spoils or rewards, though a win opens the next ward). The game's summary carries it (`uncounted`).
- **"Quest 5", not "Step 5".** The daily cards are numbered Quest 1 to Quest 5, the easiest to the hardest, and the Quests page says "Do them in any order: each counts by itself and pays once." The contract's field keeps its name (`step`: the quest's difficulty).
- **Testing a quest without playing it.** The testing grants' wins by format take a Commander table's size (Players at the table: 2, 3 or 4), so a win "in a four-player Commander game against skill-6 bots" can be granted; they already took the deck's colours, the opponents and the bots' skill.

## Caught up from older versions

- **The quest boards in play: "any goal players can't reach"** ([the plan](../plans/0.9.7.md)) is finding 2 above.
- **The court's difficulty, measured.** `CampaignDifficulty` plays ward 15 as a King's Table game of six bots: the reference deck as King A at the reference skill, two bot Generals on fixture decks at the ward's skill (what a player who brings no friend gets), against the court as the API serves it. The difficulty workflow measures ward 15 with the others (at most three games a reference deck, each with a 25-minute budget). It plays without the owner's incarnation, which only the owner's engine has, so the live court is harder than it measures.

## Claude's choices, for the owner to confirm

- **The word:** "Quest 1" to "Quest 5" (the owner offered "quests" or "achievements"; achievements are already their own thing, the 35 on the profile).
- **Passive bots still count for nothing.** The rule is the owner's own of v0.8.6, and it keeps testing out of the economy; the fix is that the game says so. Say the word and a passive-bots game counts for quests on an administrator's own account.
- **The market's margin:** an auction goal is drawn while the house holds twice what it asks for, and an auction to bid on needs more than a day left. A board already drawn keeps its quests: this week's board on the live site still asks for three bids, which three timed auctions listed from another account make reachable.
- **A friend eliminated at a won court is paid**, one who conceded is not (finding 4).
- **Left as it is, to decide:** a court closed, left idle or run to the turn limit changes nothing, as anywhere in the campaign ([the campaign](../campaign.md#the-unbound-court-ward-15-v096)). So a player losing the court can close the game instead of losing it, and keep their place at the court instead of going back to ward 14. Making a closed court a lost one would end that, at the price of a trip back for a game closed because it stalled.

## Contract

- `GameSummary.uncounted` (boolean): the game's bots have been passive, so its end counts for nothing; true only for the game's own player and a viewer of its concealed information.
- `POST /api/v1/admin/users/{id}/results` takes `seats` (2 to 4) for a Commander table's size; another format with another size answers `422 invalid_seats`.
- `GET /api/v1/tables/browse`: `host` matches handles only.
- `QuestView.step` is described as the quest's difficulty, no order.
