# v0.6.4: basic land packs in Leng's shop

A patch version, as the owner named it, opened and closed on 2026-10-25 (tag `v0.6.4`). The owner gave its whole scope at the start, so [the v0.6.4 plan](../plans/0.6.4.md)'s carried items stay open for later versions.

## Scope agreed for this version

The owner, 2026-10-25: "The only change is going to be that in Leng's Shop there are packs of basic land for sale. 200 mana (40 of each color) ... They can also be part of the rotation for weekly sales", with the newest editions getting the most pulls and the older ones, back to Alpha, lower chances on a scale; 1 to 5 foils of each land type, chosen at random; only basic lands. Also "individual color land packs of 40 lands (same foil rules, etc as above) for 50 tokens each." A correction during the work set the Basic Land Pack's price at **200 tokens** (first given as 100).

## Added

- **The Basic Land Pack**: 200 lands, 40 each of Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain and Forest, for 200 tokens.
- **A pack of each basic land**: 40 Plains, Islands, Swamps, Mountains or Forests, for 50 tokens each.
- **Editions on a scale.** The lands come from every English paper printing of the five basics in the editions Wizards sold (core sets, expansions, masters, draft innovation, starter and Commander sets), ranked by release; each edition's chance is its rank, so the newest are the likeliest and Alpha the rarest. With today's 133 editions, an Alpha Mountain comes about once in 8,900 Mountains and a Bloomburrow Mountain about once in 77. Only the five basic lands: no Wastes, snow-covered basics, legendary, colorless or nonbasic lands.
- **Foils.** Each color's 40 lands hold 1 to 5 foils, drawn at random per color when the pack opens.
- **The weekly sale** draws the land packs among the products on the shelf, like any pack.
- **On the page**, the packs stand on their own shelf, "Basic land packs", above the eras, with a drawn pack striped in the colors it holds, and open at Leng's counter like any pack.
- Details: [the shop](../shop.md#basic-land-packs-v064). In the API, `kind` and `era` take `lands`, `price_basis` takes `fixed`, and a product's `origin` (Administration) takes `lands`.

## Deploying 0.6.4

No `.env` change. One migration (`0031_land_packs`), which `upgrade.sh` runs: it lets a shop product's origin be `lands`. The API adds the six land packs and builds their collations from the catalog on the shop's first request. The engine did not change, but `deploy.sh` pulls the private engine image at the tag, so `commander-duels-engine-sphinx:0.6.4` is built too. Once the tag's run has published the `0.6.4` images and the private engine image exists, on the VM: `cd ~/commander-duels && git pull && bash deploy/upgrade.sh`. Afterwards, Leng's shop shows the "Basic land packs" shelf with the six packs.
