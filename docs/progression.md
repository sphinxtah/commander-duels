# The Planeswalker Alliance (v0.3.1)

Since v0.3.1 a player's game wins raise their level, each level opens more of the owner's Planeswalkers to ally with (88 since v0.4.2, and the Godlike tier's own), and the alliance brings that Planeswalker's cards into the player's card library. Players build decks from their own library, which starts with two of ten Commander starter decks; administrators and SysOp build from the whole catalog. The owner's decisions are in [the v0.3.1 plan](plans/0.3.1.md#decisions-at-the-start-of-v031). This is the scope's earned collection policy (PROG01), with the ledger and the result rules of PROG03–PROG05.

## Wins and levels

- **What counts.** A win is a game the engine reports as `completed` with a player's seat as the winning seat: against bots of any skill, in Legacy or Commander. A turn limit, a concession, an idle timeout, a closed game or a draw has no winner and counts for no one. In Two-Headed Giant every seat of the winning team won (`winner_seats`), and since v0.5.1 each of its players counts a win, under their own seat's key.
- **Counted once, from the server's own record** (PROG04, PROG05, NET10). When the API records a finished game, the same database transaction writes one entry in the progression ledger, keyed `win:<game>:<seat>`. A replayed end, a retry or a restart finds the game no longer active and the key taken, and writes nothing. The browser never reports a result.
- **The ledger** (`progress_ledger`) is append-only: PostgreSQL refuses updates and deletes. Each entry records its kind, the change in wins, its unique key, the game, the rule it was counted under (`wins-v1`), and for a setting its reason and who made it. An account's wins are the sum of its entries.
- **SysOp's settings.** SysOp starts at 100,000 wins, so every tier is open to it. In Administration it may set any account's wins, its own included: the API adds the difference as a `wins_set` entry with a required reason, and audits it (`progress.wins_set`, PROG03). Settings stay apart from counted wins, and the profile shows both. Administrators see each player's wins, level and alliance but cannot set them.
- **A reset** (v0.4.5) sets an account's wins back to 0 the same way, with a `wins_set` entry whose reason is "Account reset by SysOp", audited with the reset ([resetting an account](identity.md#resetting-an-account-v045)).
- **Games before v0.3.1 count.** Migration 0007 entered every finished game a player won, from the result the API had recorded.
- **Quests, achievements and the record** (v0.5.0) count games won from the same record, and SysOp's settings never ([quests, achievements and statistics](quests.md)).

Changing a tier is a commit, not an administrative setting.

## Alliances

- **One at a time, any time.** A player allies with a Planeswalker their tier has opened, and may switch or leave whenever they like. Each change is audited (`alliance.joined`, `alliance.left`). An alliance stays if SysOp later lowers the account's wins: it was a choice the tier allowed.
- **Its cards.** While the alliance lasts, the library holds a playset (4) of each of the Planeswalker's own Planeswalker cards: every deck card in the catalog with a face whose types include Planeswalker and whose subtypes include the roster's subtype (Tibalt's cards include the back face of Valki, God of Lies). The Wanderer, whose card has no subtype, is matched by name. Cards that only name a Planeswalker ("Chandra's Outrage") are not included. Since v0.4.2 every Planeswalker in the roster has at least one card a game can play (above).
- **Leaving** takes the cards out at once, except copies owned from another source. Every deck of the player is revalidated then, so a deck that needed the alliance's cards is shown as collection-locked straight away.
- **The portrait is the alliance's.** The allied Planeswalker's portrait is the player's picture on the profile, in the top bar, on the main menu (since v0.4.5, where it stands large beside the way to play) and at the table. With no alliance, the generated sigil stays.
- **The Cards page's alliance tab** (v0.4.2). Named for the allied Planeswalker ("Teferi's cards"), it shows only that Planeswalker's cards, for every account, SysOp's too, and follows the alliance when it changes (`GET /api/v1/me/alliance/cards`). Since v0.4.5 it shows only those a game can play. For The Sphinx it shows the custom cards. It replaced the "Godlike cards" tab, which had shown every custom card to any Godlike account whatever its alliance (the owner's report at the start of v0.4.2).

## The library

A player's library is what they may build from (PROG01's earned policy, DECK08):

| Source | Copies | Edition (v0.5.0) | For how long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The two starter decks chosen at onboarding | every copy of every card in both | the printing the owner chose for each card | for good |
| Decks saved before v0.3.1 (migration 0007) | per card, the most any one of the player's decks held outside its maybeboard | that deck's printings (migration 0025) | for good |
| The alliance | 4 of each of the Planeswalker's cards | each card's default printing | while allied |
| Basic lands | without limit until v0.6.0; since then only those granted, by packs and starter decks ([below](#the-library-by-finish-and-basic-lands-v060)) | every edition until v0.6.0; since then the printing granted | always |
| Packs (v0.4.0) | the cards pulled | the printing pulled (and since v0.6.0 its finish) | for good |
| Tivit's Auction House (v0.6.0) | the copies bought, less the player's own on live or sold listings | the printing and finish sold | for good |

- **Copies count, as in Magic Duels.** A deck may hold no more copies of a card than the library does, within the format's own limit, counting every printing and Oracle version of the card together. Copies are a limit per deck: two decks may each hold the same owned card. Since v0.5.0 they count by edition too ([below](#the-library-by-edition-v050)).
- **Grants are recorded.** Every grant is a row in `library_grants` with its source, a key naming what granted it (`starter:<deck>`, `grandfathered:0.3.1`, `pack:<opening>`) and since v0.5.0 the edition it gives (`printing_id`); PostgreSQL refuses updates and deletes. A starter grant is made once, whatever retries. The alliance's cards are not granted: they are in the library while the alliance lasts.
- **After a reset** (v0.4.5, [resetting an account](identity.md#resetting-an-account-v045)) the library is empty: the grants stay recorded, and the library counts only those made after the reset's time (`users.reset_at`). A starter deck chosen or bought again is a new grant, keyed with that time (`starter:<deck>:<time>`).
- **Collection-locked decks.** Validation reports a deck holding more copies than the library as `collection.locked` (kind `collection_locked`), with what the library holds. Such a deck is legal and stays saved, but it cannot start a game until it is fixed. The deck's stored flags are refreshed whenever the library changes.
- **Administrators and SysOp** hold `library.unlimited`: they build from the whole catalog, and only the format's rules apply. SysOp keeps its any-card deck lists.
- **Where it shows.** Every card in search results and card details says how many copies the player's library holds (`owned`; null when not limited). The deck builder's finder starts on the library, and the Cards page browses the whole catalog with an "In my library" filter and a library view.
- **Cards out of the game** (v0.4.5). Copies of a card the game cannot play ([catalog](catalog.md#only-playable-cards-are-in-the-game-v045)) stay recorded, as every grant does, but are hidden: the library's list and the alliance tab leave the card out, and no deck's draft holds it. If the engine learns the card, the copies are there again, as many as before.

## The library by edition (v0.5.0)

The owner's request of 2026-10-22, with a screenshot of Goblin King's details opened from a pack, which listed all 27 of its printings: "someone's library always only shows the specific cards by edition." Asked whether decks should follow, the owner chose to lock them to owned editions: "a deck may hold only editions the player owns, copy by copy." Basic lands, administrators and SysOp stay as they were.

- **Grants name their edition.** A pack's cards are granted in the printings it pulled, a starter deck's (chosen or bought) in the printings it holds, so it plays at once: the owner's choice for each card, or the card's default printing where the catalog lacks that one. One grant gives one edition (`uq_library_grants_edition`: account, key and printing); a grant of several editions of a card is a row each.
- **The alliance's playset** is in each card's default printing (`card_concepts.default_printing_id`, else its newest), one playset per card.
- **Grants from before v0.5.0** got their editions from migration `0025_library_editions`: a pack's from the printings the opening pulled (`pack_cards`, by the opening in its key); a starter deck's from `content/starter-decks.json`, read as the API reads it (`CD_CONTENT_DIR`); a grandfathered one (v0.3.1) from the player's deck that held the most copies of the card, among its drafts and the versions saved by then, preferring one holding exactly as many, a copy that named no printing taking the default; and wherever none was found, the card's default printing. Its downgrade adds each grant's editions back into one row per card.
- **The library lists each card by edition** (`GET /api/v1/library`): one item per card and edition, with the edition (set name and code, collector number, release date, rarity), its copies and where they came from, and the card's picture that edition's image. My library's tiles name the edition under the card's name ("Revised Edition (3ED #150)"); the list has an Edition column; the line above counts cards, editions and copies ("3 cards in 4 editions, 8 copies"). "Find in your library" also finds a set by name or code, and "From" (now with "Packs you opened") keeps working.
- **Card details** show only the editions that matter where they are opened: from a pack, only the edition it held; from My library, only the editions the library holds; from All cards, every printing, with the editions held and their copies under "Your library" ([catalog](catalog.md#on-screen)). Every card a player is sent carries `editions`, the editions their library holds of it.
- **Decks hold only owned editions** ([decks](decks.md#validation-deck08-t03t05)). Each deck entry's copies count against its edition's; a copy naming no edition takes one the library holds when the draft is saved. A deck holding more copies of an edition than the library is collection-locked, with what to switch to.
- **Once, at deploy,** every player's deck drafts were switched to the editions their library holds where it held enough copies ([decks](decks.md#editions-switched-at-deploy-v050)).
- **Packs.** A pack's card is "new" when its edition is new to the library ([shop](shop.md#buying-and-opening)).
- **Reset and deletion** are unchanged: the library counts the grants since `users.reset_at`, by edition as by card.
- **Checked by** `tests/test_library_editions.py` (grants from packs and starter decks, the library's items, card details, validation by edition, saves, imports, guided creation, the deploy's switch with its note, a reset, and the migration's backfill and downgrade on SQLite and PostgreSQL), and Vitest for My library, card details from a pack and from the library, and the deck editor's edition choice.

## The library by finish, and basic lands (v0.6.0)

With Tivit's Auction House ([the auction house](auctions.md)) the owner decided on 2026-10-23 that editions and finishes are separate items, and then that basic lands are no longer exempt: "Lands come only from packs and decks", each a real copy of its exact printing, sold like any card, and a deck must own every basic land it holds. Decks still do not care about the finish; the foil look is for display, outside games.
- **Grants name their finish** (`library_grants.finish`: nonfoil, foil or etched), and a grant gives one edition in one finish (`uq_library_grants_finish`: account, key, printing and finish). A pack's cards are granted in the finish they were pulled; a starter deck's, a grandfathered deck's and the one-of-a-kind copy are nonfoil; a card bought at the auction house in the finish it was sold (source `auction`, key `auction:<id>`).
- **Basic lands are granted** like any card: a pack's in the printing and finish pulled (a basic land pulled is "new" once, as any card is), a starter deck's in the printings its list names (the card's default printing where the catalog lacks the one named), every copy of them. Validation counts them against the library's copies, by edition, like any card ([decks](decks.md#validation-deck08-t03t05)); the search's "In your library" and the guided builder keep to those held, and the builder suggests no more of a basic land than the library holds, so its deck may come out short. Administrators and SysOp build from the whole catalog as before.
- **The library is its grants less the copies on its own live and sold listings** at the auction house, since any reset; a copy bought there is a grant. Unsold, withdrawn and cancelled listings stop counting. No grant is ever negative: grants stay append-only.
- **The library's items** (`GET /api/v1/library`) carry `finishes` (`{nonfoil, foil, etched}`, the copies held now, an alliance's playset counted nonfoil) and `sellable` (the copies that may be listed: granted ones, never an alliance's or the one-of-a-kind copy). Validation counts copies per edition whatever their finish.
- **Past grants** got theirs from migration `0028_auction_house`: a pack's grant split by the finishes its opening pulled of that edition (`pack_cards`, a row per finish), everything else nonfoil; every pack opened before it was granted the basic lands it pulled, and every starter deck chosen or bought the basic lands of its list (`content/starter-decks.json`, read as the API reads it, `CD_CONTENT_DIR`), under the grant's own key and time. Grandfathered decks (v0.3.1) and decks a player built with basic lands they were never granted are **collection-locked** until the library holds them (from packs, starter decks or the auction house), or the deck holds fewer. The downgrade adds each grant's finishes back into one row per edition and takes back every basic land granted, unlimited again before v0.6.0.
- **Print runs** count every copy granted except those bought at the auction house: a copy changing hands is not printed again ([print runs](shop.md#print-runs-v051)).
- **Checked by** `tests/test_auctions.py` (finishes from a pack, listing and selling by finish, the migration's back-fill and downgrade on SQLite and PostgreSQL) and the updated `tests/test_library_editions.py`, `tests/test_progression.py` and `tests/test_shop.py` (basic lands granted and counted).

## One of a kind (v0.5.1)

On 2026-10-23 the owner asked for the only copy of The One Ring ever printed to be Sphinx's: The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth #0, the serialized 001/001, whose card is printed in the Black Speech of Mordor (Scryfall `93de9042-cc62-4ade-8d8d-68fdbc84bfae`, language `qya`, in the catalog like any printing). "That will be the only one."
- **Sphinx's copy.** Migration `0027_one_of_a_kind` grants one copy to the active player whose handle is Sphinx, once (`library_grants`, source `unique`, key `unique:<Scryfall ID>`). The account is named by its handle, never its email: the repository is public. A database without the printing or without that player (a fresh one, a test's) gets nothing.
- **What it looks like.** The edition shows its own picture everywhere, so the card reads in Black Speech, in Tengwar letters; its details give the English Oracle text to read, as every card's do. My library lists it as "One of a kind".
- **No one else.** A pack never deals it: it gives way to another card from its sheet, as a card the game cannot play does ([the shop](shop.md#buying-and-opening)). It is in no starter deck, and a card's default edition, which an alliance's playset takes, is never it (a borderless printing ranks below the black-bordered ones). A deck may hold it only for its owner: a library holds no copy of it, and an account that builds from the whole catalog is refused it too ("The One Ring from The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth (LTR #0) is one of a kind: another player owns the only copy."), except SysOp, who builds with anything.
- **The list** is `ONE_OF_A_KIND` in `api/library.py` (Scryfall ID and the owner's handle), which the migration repeats; another card would need both and a migration of its own.
- **A reset** (SysOp's, [resetting an account](identity.md#resetting-an-account-v045)) starts a library afresh, so it would take the copy away from Sphinx's library, and no one else could hold it.
- **Checked by** `tests/test_one_of_a_kind.py`: packs give it way, decks refuse it but for its owner and SysOp, and the migration grants it once and goes back, on SQLite and PostgreSQL.

## The starter decks

- **The ten decks** are `content/starter-decks.json`: one 100-card Commander deck per two-colour pair, each with its commander, colours, description, how to play it and what it teaches, and the printing the owner chose for every card. `scripts/starter_decks.py` makes it from the owner's workbook, which stays out of git: the workbook also holds private production notes (reference prices, and which card is each deck's guaranteed bonus) that must not reach players or the client, and the script keeps none of them. Each list was checked against the catalog on 2026-10-20: every card is certified and Commander-legal, and none is a Game Changer.
- **Choosing two.** After choosing a handle, a player chooses two of the ten (`onboarding` goes `handle`, then `starters`, then `complete`). Both become decks of their own, with every copy of their cards granted to the library for good, and the choice is audited (`library.starters_chosen`). They are free: the 1,000 welcome tokens stay for the shop. Players who had finished onboarding before v0.3.1 choose theirs at their next visit.
- **The setup wizard** (v0.4.2, `web/src/app/SetupWizard.tsx`). A new account sets up its game in one dialog: a welcome with four screenshots of the game, its handle (the name other players see, at the table, in the lobby and later in a game's chat), the two free starter decks, each shown with its commander's card, and an alliance, which may wait. It opens by itself each time the site opens on an account that has not chosen its handle and its starter decks, a new sign-in or a reload, until both are done; "Finish later" closes it, and the notice on every page and Profile's "Continue setup" open it again at the account's step. The server decides every step, as before. After SysOp resets an account (v0.4.5) it runs again from the welcome, as for a new player.
- **The other eight in Leng's shop** (v0.4.2). Once the two free ones are chosen, the shop sells each of the others once, priced by the market value of its cards ([shop](shop.md#starter-decks-v042)).
- **The six old starter decks** (the engine's fixture decks) are no longer offered to players. They remain the engine's test decks: SysOp sees them at the practice table, and the browser journeys and the engine's smoke games play them.
- **The bots** at the practice table play the ten in Commander, a different one each, when no deck is chosen for them; Legacy bots keep the two Legacy fixture decks. `CD_BOT_DECKS=fixtures` keeps Commander bots on the fixture decks too, as CI's browser game journeys need: their leak checks tell concealed cards apart by name, and the starter decks share cards. A starter deck whose cards the catalog cannot all map to the engine (since v0.4.5, whose cards are not all in the game) falls back to a fixture deck. On 2026-10-22 every card of the ten starter decks and of the six fixture decks was in the game.

## The Godlike tier's custom cards

The Godlike tier opens a Planeswalker of the owner's own making and its cards, available only by reaching 100,000 wins. Nothing about them is in this repository: not their names, text or images, in code, tests, docs or logs. Tests use made-up stand-ins.

- **SysOp uploads them** in Administration, over the site's HTTPS (permission `custom_cards.manage`). The browser fits each image to Scryfall's 745 × 1040 card size and encodes it as WebP under 750 KB, which base64 makes 1,000,000 characters, so a request stays under the proxies' 1 MB limit; the API checks it is the image it claims to be, at most 900 KB. A card of the same name is replaced. The audit trail records each upload and removal by the card's ID, never its name or text.
- **A table of their own** (`custom_cards`), outside the catalog's: no search, card page or catalog count reaches them. The images are stored in the database, so they are in the nightly backups.
- **Only Godlike accounts see them.** `GET /api/v1/custom-cards` and each card's image answer only an account with 100,000 wins or more (SysOp included); to everyone else they answer exactly as a route that does not exist. Images are sent `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. Since v0.4.2 the list holds only the allied Planeswalker's custom cards, so it is empty unless the account is allied with The Sphinx, SysOp's too: the Cards page and the deck builder's finder show them only then. SysOp's upload list in Administration stays whole.
- **In the library.** An alliance with The Sphinx puts a playset (4) of each in the library, while the account is at the Godlike tier. Administrators and SysOp at that tier build with them as with any card.

### Playing them (v0.4.0)

Since v0.4.0 the custom cards are playable in every game, practice games and human tables alike, by accounts at the Godlike tier (the owner's decision of 2026-10-21, [v0.4.0 plan](plans/0.4.0.md#added-on-2026-10-21-the-godlike-tiers-custom-cards-become-playable)). Bots never play them.

- **In decks** (`api/custom_play.py`, [decks](decks.md#the-godlike-tiers-custom-cards-v040)). A Godlike account adds them from the deck builder's finder, which shows them with their images under "Godlike cards". Drafts, revisions, validation, statistics, import by exact name and export of the owner's own deck all hold them. Copies follow the library. They count as legal in Legacy and Commander. One may be a commander by the catalog's rule: a legendary creature, or a card whose text says it can be your commander. Its color identity comes from the mana symbols of its cost and rules text, reminder text aside. A revision stores a custom card as such (`deck_entries.custom_card_id`, migration 0011).
- **Below Godlike.** If SysOp lowers an account's wins below 100,000, its decks are revalidated at once. A deck holding a custom card becomes collection-locked (`custom.godlike_only`): legal and kept, but it cannot start a game. The deck shows each such card only as a hidden "Godlike card", with no name, text or image, and the custom card list answers 404 again.
- **The engine.** Their rules live in the owner's private repository, whose CI builds a private engine image: the public image plus a plugin on its classpath and a card database rebuilt with it ([engine plugins](engine/xmage-spike.md#plugins-v040)). The live site runs it through `CD_ENGINE_IMAGE` ([deployment](deployment.md#the-engine-with-the-custom-cards-v040)). A custom card is playable only where the running engine knows its exact name (case aside). The API asks the engine's card search for each custom card's name (`api/engine_cards.py`) and keeps the answers for one engine start, while the engine's `/health` describes the same card database; it looks again at most every 30 seconds. On an engine without them, such as the public image, the custom card list and the deck builder say "Not loaded by this engine", validation reports the card as not playable here, and a game holding one is refused (`422 custom_not_loaded`, naming the cards). Every game start asks the engine afresh. A seat whose deck holds a custom card can never be a bot's (`422 custom_cards_for_bots`).
- **At the table.** On the stack and the battlefield they are public, so every player at that table, and SysOp observing it, may zoom and read them with their images. The table asks for images by name (`GET /api/v1/card-images?name=`). For a custom card's name the API serves the card's own image, `private, no-store`, to a Godlike account, and to an account seated in a game where some seat's deck holds that card: a game it names, or else one still running or ended in the last day. SysOp, who may observe any game, sees it in any such game. To anyone else the name gets exactly the answer a name Scryfall does not know gets, and it is never sent to Scryfall.
- **Nobody else sees them in a deck list.** A shared deck, its export and a copy of it leave the custom cards out, whoever looks. A game's seats list no deck contents.

## The portraits and badges

- **Portraits.** The owner's 98 portraits (1254 × 1254, transparent), and since v0.4.1 The Sphinx's, the Godlike tier's, stay outside the repository; since v0.4.2 the site keeps 89 of them, the roster's. `scripts/planeswalkers.py portraits` writes their web sizes, WebP with alpha at 128, 256 and 512 pixels, contain-fitted and never cropped, to `web/public/portraits/<id>-<size>.webp` (about 11 MB). The installed app does not precache them: a screen fetches one when it shows it, and the web server lets browsers keep them for a week. `docs/asset-provenance.csv` records them.
- **Badges.** Until the owner's own arrive, each tier's badge is a generated metal crest (bronze, silver, gold and platinum metal, Mythic in the mythic rarity's orange-red, Godlike prismatic in the five colours), drawn as SVG in the page.
