About Commander Duels

Commander Duels is a free, unofficial beta of a Magic: The Gathering game in the style of Magic Duels, played in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

What you can do

  • Play Commander with 2 to 4 players, or Legacy with 2, against computer opponents (bots) or against other players at human tables.
  • Build your own decks from your card library. Every player starts with two starter decks of their choice.
  • Earn levels by winning, and ally with one of the Planeswalkers as you level up. Each alliance adds that Planeswalker's cards to your library.
  • Earn virtual tokens from wins and spend them on virtual booster packs in Leng's card shop.

There is no real money anywhere: playing is free, tokens cannot be bought, and the site shows no advertising.

How to join

During the beta, accounts are by approval. Press "Sign in with Google" on the home page to request one. The site's owner reviews each request by hand, and the page opens the game for you once you are approved.

Go to the home page

Signing in with Google shares your name, your email address and your Google account's ID with the site, and nothing else. The privacy policy says what the site keeps and why, and the terms of use say what is expected of players.

A beta

Commander Duels is unfinished. Things will break, change or be reset while it is tested, and bug reports from players help fix them.
