1. First Things
 
100. Beginnings
 
100.1. A test game is played by two or more pretend players.
 
100.1a A pretend player may also be a teapot. See rule 101.2.
 
Example: A teapot joins the game. It is a pretend player.
 
100.1b. A teapot can't be the starting player.
 
100.2. Each pretend player starts with 9 marbles.

Blue marbles
 
101. Middles
 
101.1. In the middle of the game, a player may sing. See rules 200 and 704.5aa.
 
101.2. A teapot whistles instead.
 
101.2aa A whistle counts as a song.
 
2. Second Things
 
200. Endings
 
200.1. The game ends when every marble is lost.
 
Example: The last marble rolls under the sofa. The game ends.
 
Example: A teapot holds the last marble. The game goes on.
